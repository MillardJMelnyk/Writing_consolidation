# About the item lists

[← Master catalog](../Master_catalog.md)

The item lists show every item in the catalog — every Substack note and post and every AI chat — with its date, title and type.

## How they are organized

- **By date:** one page per year, newest items first.
- **By title:** one page per letter of the alphabet, A to Z, plus one page for titles that start with a number.

The start page, [All items](All_items.md), links to every year and every letter.

## How to use them

1. Open [All items](All_items.md).
2. Click a year to see that year's items newest first, or click a letter to see items whose titles start with it.
3. Click any title to open the item's own page: date, type, summary, topics, index terms, a link to the original, and the full text.

For a Substack note, the "title" is the first words of the note.
