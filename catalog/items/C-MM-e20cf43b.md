### Claude · 2026-05-30 13:46

It's a no-code AI app builder — describe what you want, it generates frontend, backend, database, hosting, and claims to handle mobile publishing to both app stores.

Real-world verdict is mixed-to-ugly. Some users call it a money pit — tasks don't complete despite detailed prompts, and billing complaints are common. Google Play reviews include accusations of bugs being written in deliberately to charge credits for fixes, difficulty getting refunds, and no real live support despite advertised claims. On the flip side, non-technical users who attended their orientation/training sessions report genuinely positive experiences building apps quickly.

Pattern: works well enough for simple demos and non-technical users willing to do their onboarding; turns predatory for anyone hitting complexity or billing issues.
