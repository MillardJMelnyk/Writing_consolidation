Ever wonder why "fitting in" is such a huge fucking deal for people?

Well, maybe psychology's practically exclusive focus on mental health as a by-product of social functionality -- **fitting into society, whether it's a healthy or fucking sick one** -- could be a reason?

> ----------------------------------------------------------------
>
> **AI weigh-in on goal orientation of psychology**
>
> ----------------------------------------------------------------
>
> <https://claude.ai/share/526458d3-fa11-40fc-ac09-6e103051b353>
>
> By 1970, roughly **95% of all psychological work** focused on fixing problems so people could function better in society—hold jobs, maintain relationships, follow norms. Only about **5%** focused on making people's inner lives richer or more meaningful.
>
> Today it's shifted slightly to maybe **85-90% functional** vs. **10-15% experiential**, but it's still nowhere close to balanced.
>
> The field has always been overwhelmingly about **repair and social adjustment**, not about **making life feel better from the inside**.
>
> ---------
>
> <https://chatgpt.com/share/6917359a-e904-8013-9270-3e267f899e88>
>
> About **70–80%** of the work has focused on **making people function better in society**, with improved experience coming as a side-effect.
>
> About **20–30%** has focused on **directly improving people’s inner experience** itself.
>
> ---------
>
> <https://copilot.microsoft.com/shares/xFU72v144QHhkmtqM2nzs>
>
> **1970:** About **85–90%** of psychology focused on helping people *fit into society*; only **10–15%** aimed directly at improving inner life experience.
>
> **Today:** Roughly **75–80%** still leans toward social functionality, while **20–25%** now targets enriching personal experience directly.
>
> ---------
>
> <https://grok.com/share/c2hhcmQtMg%3D%3D_613df19f-7f76-4dc6-9583-8c059b25c866>
>
> In 1970, most psych work (85%) focused on fitting into society first, with better personal feelings as a side effect. Only 15% aimed straight at inner joy and flow.
>
> Today, it's shifted: 65% prioritizes social fit first, while 35% targets personal inner experience directly.
