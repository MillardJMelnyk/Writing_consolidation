Very good list. None of those things are characteristic of healthy relationships. Which brings up a point you didn't highlight:

Relationships are between real people. Relationships in a cult are ALWAYS functions of your "relationship" to the "group" and its "truth".

Groups are abstract mental constructs that each person connects to actual people. This is done in each persons mind, not in the real world. Groups don't exist outside the minds of people who behave as if they do. This is why branding and distinctive names are necessary. Without them, a group would have no physical evidence, no material "existence".

A relationship with another person that runs through the group construct is NOT a personal relationship. It's a relationship to an avatar -- someone playing a role having adopted the identity as a representative/instance of the group.

Sorry, that's not a real human being at all. It's a character in a play. C.S. Lewis' \*The Great Divorce\* has an excellent rendition of how this works in Chapter 5 in the interchange between the "Tragedian" and his wife.

One other trait I think is important.

Before many of the tells you mentioned become apparent, all cults have an overriding and inviolable principle: personal judgment MUST be subservient to group judgment. It's not just about accepting the group narrative and its "truth". It's about psychological conditioning to innately accept your own judgment as permanently overridden by the authority of God, the group as God's incorporation on Earth, and its leaders as the voices of that authority.

It's codependence on steroids.

This goes far beyond the point of the group's judgment and will being unquestionable. It goes to unquestionably regarding the impulse to question itself as suspect, a flaw, a hidden "sin" or maliciousness, and probably evil, coming from a damaged, defective mind/soul.

Of course, shrewd cult members are not going to make this clear from the start, but the patten becomes very evident early on if you're looking for it. It's invisible to people who are looking for a surrogate Mommy/Daddy/Big Brother authority to help them make sense of life and offer connection.
