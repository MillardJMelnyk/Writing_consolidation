In every case I've ever seen, the reason why murderous violence "make sense" to people is because they've been triggered -- in many cases permanently -- into a psychotic state where, to them, the highest priority is to secure safety from evils and enemies.

It's psychotic because no amount of proof that the others don't want to be enemies and do not intend evil makes a difference to them.

Just like is true for every single cultist on the planet.

The futility of all the efforts I've seen and read about in the 60 years since I became socio-politically aware (watching the Watts riots on KCLA on a black and white TV while staying with family friends in Burbank) -- and I mean \*\*all\*\* of them -- is that none have targeted the psychosis and its triggers. All of them have targeted symptoms.

That's no way to deal with a disease.

But the galling, tragic thing is that the people supposedly protesting and advocating and pressing "rights" and each one touting their own special, proprietary solutions for the world's ills HAVE NO INTENTION OF ACHIEVING A CURE.

Just ask them.

Is a world in which evil has been eradicated, where the NORM is honesty and goodwill and mutual care and cooperation, even possible?

Is a world freed from the psychosis that makes "leaders" absolutely mandatory and defines "power" as murderous violence, where supremacists are enshrined, and where the normal, healthy goodness of most normal, healthy people (far more healthy than their "heroes" at least) is pooh-poohed and ignored -- is any such freed world a realistic, achievable goal?

They'll tell you no. If they're honest.

Most aren't.
