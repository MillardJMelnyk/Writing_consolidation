The perpetual quest for leaders, the demand that there must be leaders, is called codependence.

Not just fascism and religion are predicated on codependence, but so is cultism, of which they are just types.

Without the demand for leaders, "government" radically changes its nature such that the concept of governing is stretched to the snapping point.

There is no governance in a partnership. There is mutualism, communality, cooperation, and agreement.

Partnership terrifies the living shit out of codependents -- the kind of terror that won't let them rest until the potential for partnership is eradicated and its proponents exterminated.
