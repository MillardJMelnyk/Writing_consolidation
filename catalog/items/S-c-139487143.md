This is called codependence. It's essential to cult formation. It doesn't guarantee that a person will join a cult, but it does guarantee that they will think like a cult member.

Codependence is precipitated by a primal kind of dehumanization in which we lose confidence in our own capabilities, especially our judgment. This puts us into a state of existential need for parental proxy figures to think for us, to make judgments and come to conclusions for us, and even to do for us what we have excluded ourselves from doing.

> **Quoted note — , 2025-07-26:** [link](https://substack.com/@/note/c-139179768)  
> “Black and white thinking is a sign you’re in your adapted child.” It hit me the other day while listening to therapist Terry Real - mature adults don’t categorize people into “my side” or “against me”, or you’re “all good” or “all bad” terms - but children do. Most of the political and culture wars we see today are people’s wounded inner children, battling each other, while they pretend to be adults.
