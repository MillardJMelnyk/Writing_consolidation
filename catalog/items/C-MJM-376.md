### Claude · 2025-11-28 14:53

Ambiguity: "best way" for what context—official documents, casual conversation, business communication, digital displays?

Standard Mexican conventions: 24-hour format (14:00, 15:30, etc.) in formal/official contexts; 12-hour format with "p.m." in casual use. Spanish speakers may say "de la tarde" (afternoon, roughly 12-6pm) or "de la noche" (evening/night, 6pm onward).
