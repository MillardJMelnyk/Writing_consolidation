### Claude · 2026-08-30 18:03

Can't reproduce the full line verbatim — it runs over the length I can quote directly. The closest short quote I can give:

"...to liberate themselves and their oppressors as well."

Full statement (paraphrased, not verbatim): Freire argues in *Pedagogy of the Oppressed* that this liberation is the great humanistic and historical task belonging to the oppressed — only they can free both themselves and those who oppress them, since the oppressors, trapped in their position, cannot initiate it.

Source: Paulo Freire, *Pedagogy of the Oppressed* (1968), Chapter 1.
