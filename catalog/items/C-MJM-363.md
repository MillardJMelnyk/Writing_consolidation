**Anna Sims Case - Vaccine Court Ruling (2024)**

**Case Details:**

* Case number: 1:15-vv-01526-CNL
* Court: U.S. Court of Federal Claims, Office of Special Masters
* Special Master: Mindy Michaels Roth
* Ruling date: April 1, 2024 (document filed date)
* Time to resolution: Nearly 12 years (filed 2015, decided 2024)

**Victim:**

* Infant: Anna E. Sims (A.E.S. in court documents)
* Age at death: 11 weeks old
* Death date: December 16, 2013

**Incident:**

* Vaccines administered at 11 a.m. well-baby appointment: Pediarix, Hib, PCV13, RotaTeq
* Death occurred: Less than 8 hours post-vaccination
* Medical examiner's original determination: "Undetermined" - consistent with SUID (Sudden Unexplained Infant Death)

**Court Ruling:**

* **Finding: Death caused by vaccine-induced encephalopathy**
* Mechanism: Cerebral edema → brainstem herniation → cardiopulmonary failure
* Expert witness: Dr. Robert Shuman (neuropathologist/child neurologist)
* Government expert concession: Autopsy not thorough enough to rule out encephalopathy; brain edema present

**Compensation:**

* $250,000 death benefit (automatic under VICP)
* $50,000 for pain and suffering
* Total: $300,000
* Source: Vaccine Injury Compensation Trust Fund

**Significance:**
First openly acknowledged, compensated infant vaccine-induced encephalopathy death in over a decade from the National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program (NVICP).
