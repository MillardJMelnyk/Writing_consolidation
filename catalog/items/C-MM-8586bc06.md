* **[ANP wire (via nieuws.nl)](https://nieuws.nl/algemeen/tussen-december-en-februari-vonnis-over-claim-vaccinatieschade):** The court will rule on December 9 at the earliest, possibly not until late February; Stassen spoke of a bioweapon several times during the hearing. ANP labels Recht OpRecht a "corona-sceptic club." Same story at [Reformatorisch Dagblad](https://www.rd.nl/a/tussen-december-en-februari-vonnis-over-claim-vaccinatieschade).

The courtroom was packed, the judge called that turnout exceptional for a civil case, Gates' lawyer denied all allegations, and Stassen told the judge both sides can't be right at once.
* **AD (as relayed by Niburu):** AD called Leeuwarden the center of the international antivax movement, compared the square outside to a circus, and reported Stassen as claiming there was no pandemic and the virus doesn't exist.
