### Claude · 2026-09-28 20:00

Path of Exile 1 weapons (and all gear) come in four rarities, shown by the color of the item name.

**Normal (white):** No random modifiers. Some bases have a fixed implicit mod, like a crit bonus on daggers. This is the starting point for crafting.

**Magic (blue):** Up to 2 random mods, 1 prefix and 1 suffix. The name is the base plus the affixes, e.g. "Heated Rusted Sword of the Leopard." Magic items are cheap to reroll, so they're good for fishing for one or two specific mods.

**Rare (yellow):** Up to 6 random mods, at most 3 prefixes and 3 suffixes, with a random two-word name. Most endgame weapons are rares because they can stack several strong mods, like flat physical damage, % physical, attack speed, and crit.

**Unique (orange-brown):** Fixed, named items with set mods, often build-defining effects that can't appear on rares. Only the numeric values roll within ranges. Some uniques also have Relic versions (green-teal) with different rolls, mostly for cosmetic or collector value.

**Changing rarity with currency:**  
- **Transmutation:** normal to magic  
- **Alteration:** rerolls a magic item  
- **Augmentation:** adds a mod to a magic item with an open slot  
- **Regal:** magic to rare, adding one mod  
- **Alchemy:** normal straight to rare  
- **Chaos:** rerolls a rare  
- **Scouring:** strips an item back to normal

**Corruption:** Any rarity can be corrupted with a Vaal Orb. A corrupted item can't be modified by normal currency afterward.
