### Claude · 2025-12-17 15:22

Two provisions convert into refundable credits (money you could receive even with zero tax liability):

**1. Adoption Credit (effective 2025)**

* Up to $5,000 is now refundable (indexed for inflation)
* Total credit remains ~$17,280 in 2025
* Would require filing to claim

**2. Child Tax Credit**

* $2,200 per qualifying child
* $1,400 of that amount is refundable
* Requires SSN for taxpayer and child
* Phases out at $200,000 (single) / $400,000 (married filing jointly)
* Would require filing to claim

All other provisions (tips deduction, overtime deduction, senior deduction $6,000, car loan interest deduction) are **deductions**, not credits. They only reduce taxable income, so they provide zero benefit if you have little or no income to begin with.

If you're low-income with no children and haven't adopted, nothing in this bill translates into refundable credits you could claim.
