Parsons was a follower of Crowley's occult philosophy called Thelema, and Crowley was his spiritual mentor who hand-picked Parsons to head the Agape Lodge, the American branch of Crowley's Ordo Templi Orientis (OTO).

1. **NASA itself was founded in 1958** by the U.S. President and Congress through the NASA Act, with Keith Glennan and Hugh Dryden appointed as its first directors.
2. **Parsons co-founded JPL in the 1930s-40s**, which later became a NASA center. His rocket fuel innovations were genuinely important to early American space efforts.
3. **Crowley's role was purely personal/spiritual** - he was Parsons' occult mentor but had no involvement in rocketry or space science.

So while Parsons' rocket science work was legitimate and important to what eventually became NASA, claims that Crowley was a "founder" of NASA are false. The connection is that a NASA pioneer happened to be involved in Crowley's occult movement, but Crowley himself contributed nothing to space science or NASA's actual founding.
