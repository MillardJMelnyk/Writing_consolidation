Got it. The hearing was September 9, 2025, titled *"How the Corruption of Science Has Impacted Public Perception and Policies Regarding Vaccines."* Two full YouTube recordings turned up:

* **C-SPAN/NBC live stream**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMY85SKXLhg
* **Alternate full stream**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1RofqrkIPHI

Witnesses were Aaron Siri (vaccine injury attorney), Toby Rogers (Brownstone Institute fellow), and Dr. Jake Scott (Stanford infectious disease specialist). Note: Scott is the Stanford physician — the "Big Pharma lawyer" framing in the GP article may be characterizing him loosely, or conflating him with another participant; he's actually a clinician, not a pharma lawyer.

The official Senate hearing page with submitted written statements is at: https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/subcommittees/investigations/hearings/how-the-corruption-of-science-has-impacted-public-perception-and-policies-regarding-vaccines/
