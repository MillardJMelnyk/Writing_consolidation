This is a very interesting piece! I'm not sure until I read through carefully, but it sounds like a lot of your ideas map to something very basic: codependence. In most work along these lines, I don't see any awareness that codependence is a cognitive mode in which embedded (by trauma) narrative overrules embodied wisdom. The problem is that it's so ubiquitous, everybody thinks it's universal, just a natural expression of human nature. It's no such thing. It's a natural expression of abused, traumatized, debilitated human nature. It's a pathological condition. Not the same as unabused, non-traumatized, intact human nature.
