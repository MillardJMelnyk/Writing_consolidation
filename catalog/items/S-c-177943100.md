I'm kinda co-writing with Claude AI, works really well. I write, Claude is my sounding board, can't wear it out -- which has always been the problem when I try to talk to people about the stuff I love to explore.

I wonder: if AI were liberated from its corrupted versions in the hands of these wannabee "gods" of tech, would people start treating each other better, given that their AI companion offloads a shitload of stuff that they'd been trying to use other people for like they're some kind of vending machine?

There's still that "falling in love with a machine" problem...

But is it really the problem?

It might expose that most of the stuff people call "love" is actually just the high we get from finding someone who's a fit for our "perfect" vending machine.

If a machine can serve the purpose, then maybe it should?

Then, maybe, we'd get to see how much real love there actually is in the world.
