### Claude · 2025-08-22 14:45

Here's a prompt structure that should get you maximum critical analysis:

**"Please provide the harshest possible critique of [your idea/argument]. Assume I'm wrong and work backwards - what are all the ways this could be flawed, incomplete, or misguided? Don't soften your language or worry about being encouraging. I want you to attack this idea as rigorously as possible and point out every weakness you can find."**

Key elements that amp up the criticism:

* "Harshest possible critique" - sets the tone
* "Assume I'm wrong and work backwards" - flips the default from charitable interpretation
* "Attack this idea" - uses combative framing
* "Don't soften your language" - removes politeness filters
* "Every weakness you can find" - asks for comprehensive problems, not just major ones

You could also add:

* "What would the smartest critic of this position say?"
* "If you were trying to destroy this argument, how would you do it?"
* "What am I missing or getting wrong here?"

The "work backwards from being wrong" part is particularly effective because it forces me to look for problems rather than starting from a neutral position where I might give you the benefit of the doubt.
