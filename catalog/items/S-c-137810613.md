Just a little recalibration of what has come to be considered normal when it's anything but…

I'm pretty sure that hunter-gatherers didn't liken their lives to a “rat race”.

PS. Just as an example of how insidious it all is, they weren't hunter-gatherers. They were gatherer-hunters. The mind-conditioning is built right into our language.
