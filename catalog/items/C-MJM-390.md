### Millard · 2025-12-01 23:59

I like my steaks on the rare side of medium rare, I'm in Mexico how do I say that in Spanish

### Claude · 2025-12-01 23:59

"Término medio tirando a rojo"

or

"Entre rojo y término medio"

### Millard · 2025-12-02 00:00

Please translate those

### Claude · 2025-12-02 00:00

"Término medio tirando a rojo" = "Medium rare leaning toward red/rare"

"Entre rojo y término medio" = "Between rare and medium rare"

### Millard · 2025-12-02 00:01

Isn't "Término medio" medium not medium rare

### Claude · 2025-12-02 00:01

Correct. I was wrong.

"Término medio" = medium

For medium rare, you'd say "término medio rojo" or "tres cuartos rojo"

For rare side of medium rare: "Rojo plus" or "casi término medio rojo"

### Millard · 2025-12-02 00:06

What about Rojo ingles?

### Claude · 2025-12-02 00:06

"Rojo inglés" = rare (sometimes very rare), literally "English red"

That would likely be too rare for what you want. It typically means quite red/bloody throughout.

For rare side of medium rare, "término medio rojo" is closer to your target.
