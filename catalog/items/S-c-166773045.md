2a - 2c: I really don't see a material difference between those 3. Clarify if you would. As it stands, I disagree with the assumption evident in the wording that humans have not, over and over since the Big Change (whatever it was) , \*\*already\*\* proven incontestably that they have the ability to do what you're asking about because they've already, actually done just that. Most people have the opposite impression because they're woefully ignorant of the many, many places and times where different groups did just that, to the tune of many hundreds of millions of people involved over thousands of years. I don't "think" this actual, present world is merely capable of correcting the shit that so many take as normal and necessary and inevitable, I have no doubt that it's inevitable.

Instead, though, of challenging me to offer info that challenges/contradicts the myths you've been treating as foregone conclusions, I'm going to encourage you to do some digging and thinking, because you're asking me questions that amount to "Is it possible to find water to drink?" while you're standing next to an ancient freshwater lake. The question really isn't whether it's possible. It's why, when the evidence is all around you -- just not included in your indoctrination -- are you asking these questions?
