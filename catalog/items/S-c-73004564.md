Honest people ***want*** to know if and where they might be wrong, *so they* ***listen****.*

If you’re dishonest; if your ego can’t bear being questioned, criticized, contradicted, and having your faults and problems exposed, *you are wrong* in a fundamental way that no mountain of facts or amazing ideas could ever make right.
