Why the fuck not?

The top 4 answers to that question, de-euphemized, are:

1. It’s different.
2. I’ll lose my unfair advantages and privileges.
3. It’s scary.
4. Even though I’d never thought about this before, even though I’ve done shitall to find out, I’m absolutely certain this will never work.
