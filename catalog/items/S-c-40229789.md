I read stuff I wrote a decade past or more, and it still speaks to me.

Or at least I don’t cringe! 😆

Either way, I’m mostly pleasantly surprised.

These days, I’d say it like this:

Pleasure motivates us. Pain motivates us. Love motivates us. Hate motivates us.

Comfort lulls us into catalepsy. Comfort is the devil.
