### Claude · 2025-08-26 19:24

Military doctrine: attacker gains tactical/strategic benefits by striking first before defender can respond or prepare. Advantages include:

* Element of surprise
* Initiative control
* Force positioning before enemy mobilization
* Disruption of enemy command/communication
* Potential to destroy enemy capabilities before use

Context matters - nuclear warfare, conventional conflict, cybersecurity, game theory applications?

### Claude · 2025-08-26 19:25

Defined as "rapid and continuous integration of all domains of warfare" across air, land, maritime, space, cyberspace.

Alternative current terms: "Cross-Domain Fires," "Multi-Domain Task Force" operations.
