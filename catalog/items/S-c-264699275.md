I have been a woman for 84 yrs.  This means that I was female from conception when an ovum produced by my mother’s ovaries fused, in one of her fallopian tubes, with a sperm produced by my father’s testicles to create the first cell of what eventually became my female infant body.  This first cell, from which all the other cells in my body have been derived, throughout my life, was a fertilized ovum known as a *zygote.*

This zygote contained 46 chromosomes; two paired sex-chromosomes and 22 pairs of *autosomes*, one set contributed by each of my parents.  One of my zygote’s two sex chromosomes was a female X chromosome contributed by my mother’s ovum (all her ova carried a female sex chromosome).  My zygote’s other sex chromosome was also a female X chromosome contributed by my father’s sperm.  Sperm can carry either a female X chromosome or a male Y chromosome, but an X carrying sperm was the winner in the race to fertilise my mother’s ovum.

Because the sperm that fertilised my mother’s ovum carried a X sex chromosome, every nucleated somatic (body) cell comprising every tissue of every organ in my body, from conception to birth and throughout my 84 yrs of life, has been sexed female (46-XX, meaning 22 pairs of autosomes and a pair of X sex chromosomes).

Because all of my nucleated somatic cells, throughout my life, have had a female karyotype (the set of chromosomes in the nucleus of a cell), my body and my embodied experience of my life have followed a female developmental pathway, during gestation in my mother’s womb, from infancy to puberty and the onset of menstruation, through reproductive maturity to menopause and then old age, while I experienced the biological and social vicissitudes of womanhood.

Now an Australian Federal Court has told me that I am no more a woman than a middle-aged man who claims that he is “living as a woman” because he has long hair and dresses like a frumpish 50s housewife. The Court has decided that male posers, predators and paraphiliacs who claim to be women are as entitled as I am to be included in any female-only space, service, organisation or activity.  This decision erases the existence of all provisions in Australian law enacted to protect women’s dignity, privacy, safety, health and well being from the biological and social vulnerabilities women experience because of their female bodies and embodied female experience of life.

The silence of the Australia political left on this Court case is shameful!
