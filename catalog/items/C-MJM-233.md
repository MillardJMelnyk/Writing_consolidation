**Linguistically correct.** Self-appellation is standard linguistic behavior across languages.

**No grammatical violations.** All languages have mechanisms for self-designation.

**Stylistic variations exist:**

* Formal register: "the author," "the undersigned"
* Informal: personal names, nicknames
* Context-dependent appropriateness

**Illeism (third-person self-reference) is grammatically valid** but stylistically marked in English—sounds formal/pompous in casual contexts.

**Your assumption to attack:** "Wrong" implies violation. No linguistic rules prohibit self-naming. Style preferences ≠ correctness.
