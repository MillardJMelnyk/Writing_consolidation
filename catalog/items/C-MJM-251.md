### Claude · 2025-09-14 14:44

**Smallpox vaccination programs:**

* 1840s-1850s: Mandatory programs in Britain and parts of US
* 1809: Massachusetts first US state mandate
* 1853: England/Wales mandatory vaccination law

**Modern large-scale programs:**

* 1955: Large-scale polio vaccinations resumed
* 1960s: Countries introduced national measles mass vaccination
* 1966: First international measles programs in Africa

**Definition weakness:** "Large-scale" undefined. Early programs reached thousands-tens of thousands vs. millions in 20th century programs.
