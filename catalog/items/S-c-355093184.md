I think it’s far easier than that.

You know it’s a cure if it cures you. No-brainer.

Proof is completely unnecessary.

Proof resides in the world of narrative and discourse. As such, it is *unreal*. I mean that literally. Abstract, theoretical, conceptual, hypothetical. Existing in mind without necessarily existing for real.

Actually, everything that exists in mind is not real. It’s either imaginary or it’s a reference, a representation, a symbol for something real—just like a photo of a person isn’t real in the same way the person is.

The exact same phenomenon that was “proven” one day can become unproven or disproven the next. Nothing about it changed except the *opinions about it.*

“Proven” is an opinion we use to try to persuade others. Whether we persuade others or not, we *know*—or if not, what business do we have trying to prove it?

That knowing is our proof to ourselves. If we’re honest, we don’t need more.
