It's a lot clearer now.

Most of the shit I've gotten over the years has nothing to do with what I've been saying and everything to do with the fact that many people get offended by what they perceive as audaciousness. They wouldn't dare bring up topics, ask questions, or make assertions the way I do for fear they'd be taken as total A-holes. So, they automatically peg me for an A-hole putting them on the spot, completely zone out about what I said, and attack me personally -- some less flagrantly than others.

So, I'm taken for a "troll". OK. If outing posers and liars and airheads and bloviating fools is "trolling", I'm guilty, gladly.

Honest people don't take me that way.

As soon as I'm satisfied that I can shut fools down and make them painfully obvious, I might switch my approach. I don't like talking with people like that, anyway. But as long as people defend and fight to protect their follies, especially when they're more than willing to hurt and harm people in the process, I'll probably keep it up.

Too bad they'll never get to see that I don't behave in any way remotely like that when I'm talking with honest people who want the truth just like I do.

Egos can just go fuck off and die. I've got no respect for them, because they deserve none, mine included.
