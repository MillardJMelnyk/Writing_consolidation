Explain to me how words on a page constitute "protections".

After showing footage that looks no different than scenes from Chicago or Los Angeles, Swan says:

//

Even though a lot of people would argue in the US they're still gonna get beat, if you go out there and you protest. If you go out into the streets police will still attack you. Uh, it happens here and, certainly, it happens there -- but there, there are no protections.

//

What protections?

It happens here and it happens there, but here there are words on a page that say we're protected when the fact is we're not?

So, the claim to fame he's pointing out is: in the US, they're not only more hypocritical -- they're more delusional.

They treat words on a page as "protections" when they do shitall in reality.

And they can't even tell the difference.
