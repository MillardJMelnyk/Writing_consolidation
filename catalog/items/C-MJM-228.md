Grains with similar satiety properties to barley:

**High fiber/protein grains (similar "stick to ribs" effect):**

* Wheat berries: higher fiber and protein than farro
* Farro: ancient wheat grain, high protein
* Bulgur: higher fiber than barley, lower calories
* Spelt: ancient wheat variety
* Quinoa: 5g protein per serving
* Oats: high-protein grain

**Barley specifications:** 1/4 cup = 32% daily fiber value, highest fiber among grains

**Cooking times:** Farro cooks faster than barley, bulgur = 10 minutes

**Cost:** Barley cheaper than farro

**Assumption attacked:** Rice's lower satiety likely due to lower fiber/protein vs. barley's beta-glucans and density. Your preference may correlate with grain processing level rather than grain type specifically.
