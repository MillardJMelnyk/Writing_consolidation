### Claude · 2025-11-21 23:51

Ranking requires criteria. I'll use: **death toll, scale of suffering, ethical violation severity, and documentation quality**.

**Tier 1 — Mass Casualty**

1. **Indonesian Mass Killings Support (1965-66)** — CIA provided kill lists to Indonesian military. 500,000–1,000,000 dead. Declassified documents confirm US role.
2. **Operation Condor Support (1970s)** — Coordinated backing of South American dictatorships' cross-border assassination/disappearance network. 60,000+ killed/disappeared across six countries.
3. **Phoenix Program (1965-72)** — Vietnam counterinsurgency. 20,000–40,000 killed, systematic torture documented. Senate confirmed.
4. **Guatemala Coup & Aftermath (1954–1990s)** — PBSUCCESS overthrew Arbenz; subsequent US-backed regimes killed 200,000+ (UN truth commission figure), including Mayan genocide.

**Tier 2 — Severe Ethical/Legal Violations**

5. **MKUltra (1953-73)** — Non-consensual human experimentation with LSD, electroshock, torture. Deaths confirmed. Destroyed most records in 1973.
6. **Extraordinary Rendition/Black Sites (2001-2009)** — Kidnapping, torture, secret prisons. Senate Intelligence Committee report documented systematic abuse.
7. **Chile Coup Support (1973)** — Destabilization of Allende, support for Pinochet. 3,000+ killed, 40,000 tortured under resulting regime.
8. **Iran Coup (1953)** — Operation Ajax overthrew Mossadegh. Lower immediate death toll but catastrophic long-term regional destabilization.

**Tier 3 — Significant but Narrower Scope**

9. **Assassination Programs** — Church Committee documented plots against Castro, Lumumba, Trujillo, others.
10. **Contra Support/Drug Links (1980s)** — Kerry Committee found CIA awareness of drug trafficking by assets.

### Claude · 2025-11-21 23:52

You're likely conflating two separate things with "Tuskegee" in the name:

**Tuskegee Syphilis Study (1932-1972)** — This is probably what you're thinking of. **Not CIA** — it was the **U.S. Public Health Service**. 399 Black men in Alabama were told they were receiving free healthcare but were actually left untreated for syphilis to study disease progression. Exposed in 1972, formally apologized by Clinton in 1997.

**Tuskegee Airmen** — Black WWII fighter pilots. Not a scandal — actually a celebrated unit that fought discrimination.

There's no documented CIA program specifically targeting Black Air Force pilots that I'm aware of.

Is the Tuskegee Syphilis Study what you were thinking of, or are you recalling something else?
