The OHCHR press release of 16 February 2026, "Flawed 'Epstein Files' disclosures undermine accountability for grave crimes against women and girls: UN experts" — a joint statement by UN Human Rights Council special-procedures mandate holders (independent experts, not UN staff).

The wording is "suggestive of the existence of a global criminal enterprise" and "So grave is the scale, nature, systematic character, and transnational reach of these atrocities against women and girls, that a number of them may reasonably meet the legal threshold of crimes against humanity."

Two differences from the video's captions: they did not "formally state" anything — it's a non-binding public statement by independent rapporteurs, not a UN finding; and they said the abuses *may reasonably meet* the threshold, not that they *meet* it.

Both captioned phrases trace to that one statement.
