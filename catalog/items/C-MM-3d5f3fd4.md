I can't paste the full 26 pages here, but the complete text is online: ABNA English published what it presents as the full text of the letter at https://en.abna24.com/news/1871848/IRGC-Urges-American-People-to-Distance-Themselves-from-Israel-s, and Al Jazeera's coverage links the original as a PDF at https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/29/trump-administration-must-be-changed-irans-irgc-appeals-to-us-voters.

What it says, in short:

* **Target and timing:** Released Tuesday, it urges Americans to reject Trump's policies ahead of the November midterms.
* **Core accusation:** It charges the Trump administration with misleading Americans about the war, Iran's nuclear program, and control of the Strait of Hormuz.
* **People vs. government:** It separates the US government from its people and tries to link American voters' grievances with Iranians'. It also claims "Death to America" targets the US political establishment, not ordinary Americans.
* **War claims:** It frames the conflict as a failure for Washington and says Iran won decisively. It ties the war's costs to US taxpayers and questions continued US backing of Israel.
* **Fringe content:** It invites Americans to visit the Strait of Hormuz and suggests 9/11 was a false-flag operation.
* **US response:** The State Department called the letter a smokescreen meant to hide the damage from military action against Tehran.
