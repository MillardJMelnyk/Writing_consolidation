### Claude · 2026-10-29 13:52

Thursday, October 29, 2026, 7:52 AM CST — It's the Israeli military strategy, publicly stated in 2008 by then-Northern Command chief Gadi Eisenkot, of answering attacks with deliberately disproportionate force against whole areas, civilian infrastructure included, as he put it: "these are not civilian villages, they are military bases". It's named after the Beirut suburb leveled in 2006, a war in which Israel killed nearly 1,000 Lebanese civilians, about a third of them children, and the 2009 UN Fact Finding Mission on Gaza identified it as an Israeli strategy involving mass destruction of civilians and infrastructure.
