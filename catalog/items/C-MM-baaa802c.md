### Claude · 2026-09-11 19:21

uBlock Origin — and uBlock Origin Lite if the full version isn't in the Edge Add-ons store when you look.

Edge is the last Chromium browser where full uBO still installs, but Microsoft has it on the chopping block: MV2 deprecation warnings start in Partner Center August 2026, with enterprise removal in early 2027 and the consumer date still unannounced. Lite is a separate listing, not an update to the original — you install it deliberately.
