### Claude · 2025-12-31 20:01

Your friend has a few practical options:

**Immediate steps:**

1. **Appeals without login** - Facebook has an appeals form accessible without being logged in: facebook.com/help/contact/260749603972907 (for disabled accounts) or through the Help Center at facebook.com/help
2. **Email notification** - If Facebook sent an email about the suspension, there's often an appeal link directly in that email that doesn't require login
3. **Business Help Center** - If these were business pages, Meta Business Help at facebook.com/business/help has separate support channels
4. **Oversight Board** - For content-related takedowns, Meta's Oversight Board accepts appeals at oversightboard.com

**Pattern recognition:**

Mass page shutdowns without clear violation notices have increased since 2023, often triggered by:

* Automated systems flagging false positives
* Changes in enforcement algorithms
* Hacked account activity they may not be aware of

**Who helps with this:**

* No reliable third-party "fixers" exist - anyone claiming insider Facebook access is likely a scam
* Some social media attorneys handle these cases, but cost-benefit is usually poor unless significant business revenue is at stake

**Clarifying question:**

Does your friend know *why* the pages were shut down? The notification type (impersonation, community standards, intellectual property, etc.) determines which appeal pathway is appropriate.
