### Claude · 2026-08-01 18:56

**Traumatic-injury (violent) deaths, 7 Oct 2023 → 1 Aug 2026**

Reference points:  
- MoH recorded at 30 Jun 2024: ~37,900 → Lancet estimate 64,260 (×1.70)  
- MoH recorded at 31 Dec 2024: ~45,550 → uncertainty paper 70,614–87,504 (×1.55–1.92)  
- MoH recorded now (27 Jul 2026): 73,329, injuries 174,009

Extrapolations:

| Method | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Lancet 64,260 scaled by MoH growth (×1.94) | ~124,000 |
| Uncertainty paper scaled by MoH growth (×1.61) | ~114,000–141,000 |
| Additive (adds only genuinely new deaths since Dec 2024, ~20,000–24,000, to the Dec 2024 range) | ~92,000–116,000 |

**Central figure: ~103,000 killed by traumatic injury. Defensible range 92,000–125,000.**

The two scaling methods run high because roughly a quarter of MoH's growth since Dec 2024 is bodies recovered from rubble and belatedly identified — deaths already inside the 70,614–87,504 estimate. The additive method strips that double count.

Add ~17,000–20,000 non-violent excess deaths (starvation, disease, collapsed healthcare) for a total war mortality of roughly **120,000**.

### Claude · 2026-08-01 18:59

**+12–15% on the violent-death figure. Not another 35–40%.**

The 35–41% MoH undercount is already inside the 103,000 — both the capture-recapture and the household survey are estimates of *actual* deaths, not of recorded ones. Stacking another undercount multiplier on top double-counts.

What is genuinely still missing from those estimates:

| Residual bias | Middle-of-the-road add |
| --- | --- |
| Missing under rubble — GMS counted 12,200 missing and conservatively treated **all of them as alive** | +8,000 (≈65% dead) |
| Zero-survivor households — 2,200 documented families with no survivors as of May 2025; a household survey structurally cannot sample them | +5,000–6,000 net of overlap |
| List dependence in capture-recapture; northern reporting blackout | +1,000–2,000 |

Combined: **+12,000 to +15,000**, roughly +13%.

**Violent deaths as of today: ~115,000–120,000.**

Indirect deaths are the weaker number and deserve a bigger uplift — starvation, untreated chronic disease, neonatal loss, epidemics are captured far worse than blast trauma. Middle-of-the-road there is +30–50% on the ~17,000–20,000, giving ~25,000.

**Total war mortality: ~140,000.** That is about 6.4% of Gaza's pre-war population — one in sixteen.

For calibration, the preprint quantifying the zero-survivor bias notes conflict death tolls historically get revised upward post-war by factors ranging from 0.7 to 23.3. A 1.13 multiplier is at the conservative end of that distribution, not the aggressive one.

### Claude · 2026-08-01 19:00

**About 26 months — late September 1941 to early November 1943**, the span of the German occupation of Kyiv.

The structure of that number matters:

* **29–30 September 1941:** 33,771 Jews shot in two days. This is the firmly documented figure, from the Einsatzgruppe C report itself.
* **October 1941 – November 1943:** the ravine remained a killing site for Roma, Soviet POWs, psychiatric patients, Ukrainian nationalists, communists, and hostages, in repeated smaller actions.

The 100,000–150,000 total is the cumulative figure across that whole period. 150,000 is the upper end, largely from Soviet-era estimates; most current scholarship centers on roughly 100,000. The Germans exhumed and burned bodies in August–September 1943 to destroy evidence, which is why the total has never been pinned down and the range is that wide.
