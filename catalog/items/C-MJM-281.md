### Millard · 2025-09-24 18:10

I agree that without reality-testing mechanisms, someone might mistake temporarily satisfying but ultimately counterproductive approaches for genuinely effective ones. However, reality testing is precisely the crux of epistomics.
1. Recognize the distinction between disembodied and embodied "knowledge".
2. Disembodied knowledge must be completely and clearly subordinated to embodied knowledge -- whether the embodiment is first-hand or reported from/about other parties.
3. Firsthand, personally embodied knowledge trumps everything else, being the only innately reliable knowledge we have access to.
4. Embodying knowledge is simple: you must do it. When you do it, you find out, and that knowledge is inherently more credible than any other kind of knowledge.
5. If it's not doable, it can be logically, rationally assessed, but it can't be concretely verified or falsified. But in that case, neither is it relevant to doing per se. It's not relevant to actually living and navigating life.
