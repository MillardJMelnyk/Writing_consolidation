But it does make you conspirators.

If you could conspire with others of your ilk, why wouldn’t you?

And if you were good people, good families, bent on human progress and prosperity, not global domination, then would the world as we know it be the result?

This is why the only explanation for the bizarre levels of codependence we see everywhere -- without which twisted, perverted fucks like those behind this global FUBAR would have no "power", period -- is psychosis.

Codependence is a mental illness, and we've all come down with a deadly case.
