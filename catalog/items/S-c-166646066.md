Loved this right up to the close. Excellent piece.

Yes, there will be a final revolution, a decisive unmasking. Definitely. Absolutely. This current fiasco of straight-up madness isn't going to last forever. We’re watching it crumble now.

The cynical nihilism that was possible in the past reached its expiration date with postmodernism. What we’ve had since then has been the final scramble of the Wizards who, far more psychotic and evil than in Oz, still hide behind curtains manipulating spectacular lies. A pathetic and despicable bunch of Wile E. Coyotes, suspended, legs spinning before the inevitable plunge to the canyon floor. Headless chickens feverishly eeking out those last few bits of scratch.

I’m not even slightly kidding.

Boomers and Gen X will yield—by defeat or by death—to Millennials, but especially to Gen Z and Gen Alpha.

Millennials began the transition. Nothing their elders and ancestors taught them made any fucking sense, and they started recognizing it.

Gen Z has no doubt about it.

Gen Alpha is aptly named, because they’re the first who will begin figuring out the alternatives to the fucking massive fiasco that these last 5,000 - 15,000 years have been, having rejected its very foundations: so-called “civilization”.

Just you watch.
