### Claude · 2026-09-25 16:36

Tour, tourist, tourism, pure, cure, secure, mature, endure, during, jury, Europe, European, curious, furious, fury, rural, plural, lure, allure, manure, obscure, poor, sure, moor, your.

Most modern British speakers say poor, sure, moor and your with /ɔː/, so leave those out of tests.

### Claude · 2026-09-25 16:55

To allure (someone) to something means to tempt or attract them toward it, e.g., "The bright lights allured tourists to the city."

"Allure to an idea" isn't standard English. Did you mean "allude to an idea"? That means to mention it indirectly, without saying it outright, e.g., "She alluded to the problem but never named it."
