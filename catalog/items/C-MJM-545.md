Of the companies that offer English instruction (ESL) online, some do not require certification from their instructors. Of those companies, which are the best to work for as an instructor?

### Claude · 2025-12-29 23:12

Based on search results (December 2025):

**Companies not requiring certification:**

| Company | Pay | Key Characteristics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cambly** | $10-12/hr | Most flexible; no schedule minimum; conversation-based; adults and kids programs; premade materials |
| **Preply** | Self-set rates | Marketplace model; you build your own client base; high earners possible but commission taken |
| **italki** | Self-set rates | Freelance marketplace; competitive; no hiring process; higher demand for specialized niches |
| **SkimaTalk** | ~$10-16/hr | Japanese adult learners; conversational focus; flexible; limited career growth |
| **Lyngo** | Lower (entry-level) | Japan-focused; premade lessons; minimal requirements; good for experience building |
| **Outschool** | Variable | Not ESL-specific; you design/sell your own courses; US marketplace for K-12 subjects |

**"Best" depends on your criteria:**

* **Highest flexibility, lowest barrier:** Cambly
* **Highest earning potential:** Preply or italki (requires building reputation/client base)
* **Most stable workflow:** Cambly (consistent student matching)
* **Japanese market specialization:** Lyngo, SkimaTalk

**Caveat:** "No certification required" often correlates with lower pay ceilings and higher competition. Several sources indicate Cambly is the most commonly cited option for uncertified instructors seeking immediate work with reliable scheduling.

### Claude · 2025-12-29 23:21

Based on search results (December 2025):

**Outschool Overview**

Outschool is an online marketplace for independent teachers, founded 2015 in San Francisco. It is **not an ESL company** — teachers offer classes on any subject (coding, art, languages, Minecraft, D&D, math, music, etc.) to students ages 3-18.

**Key Characteristics**

| Aspect | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Pay | Teacher-set pricing; average ~$50/hr, top earners $100+/hr |
| Commission | Outschool takes 30% |
| Payment | PayPal, 7-13 days after class |
| Minimum hours | None |
| Certification required | No |
| Degree required | No |
| Curriculum | You create your own |
| Platform | Zoom-based |
| Student base | 1M+ learners in 195 countries (majority US) |

**Eligibility Requirements**

* Age 18+
* Reside in: US (50 states), Mexico, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, South Korea, Spain, or UK
* Must pass background check (free, via Checkr)
* Must pass identity verification (free, via Persona)
* Able to receive PayPal payments

**Application Process**

1. Submit written application detailing expertise and class ideas
2. Record 60-90 second profile video
3. Approval within 3 business days
4. Complete identity verification and background check
5. Watch policy videos
6. Create first class listing (must be one-time class initially)

**Pros**

* Complete autonomy over content, pricing, schedule
* High earning potential
* No certification barrier

**Cons**

* 30% commission is steep
* You must build your own student base
* Income depends entirely on enrollment
* Limited to specific countries

**Application URL:** https://outschool.com/teach
