Agreed, but still superficially focused on a symptom instead of the cause.

ASK YOURSELF WHY BELIEF IN AUTHORITY IS SO SEDUCTIVE AND BELIEVABLE.

Could it have something to do with being mindfucked since we were able to understand anything at all to OBEY THE POWERFUL?

Face it -- we were mindfucked long before we'd developed enough to understand the word or even the functional concept. All we knew was these beings were Gods and if we didn't respond to them the way they demanded, they hurt us, spurned us, isolated us, rejected us, got angry with us, and got very, very scary looks on their faces.

By the time we were old enough to grasp that there are some people who "rightly" behaved this way towards us -- because it was our fault, they were only doing it in response to what **we** had done -- the psychosomatic basis for belief in authority was already poured, set, and petrified.

No one wants to go that deep, because the configuration of our mindfucking which is completely and accurately described by "codependence" is terrifying to confront, even to merely acknowledge. The admission that there is an option to living codependently brings into question the literal death of ego -- and ego absolutely wills to never fucking die.

But it will, one way or another, sooner or later, while you still have a chance to live free from it or have no life left.
