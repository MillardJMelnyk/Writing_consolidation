Three solid picks on Amazon (as of April 2026):

**1. Redtwo Clay Beads Bracelet Making Kit**
~$15–18 | 5,100+ flat polymer clay beads with charms, elastic cord, instructions. Aligns with the current "preppy bead" trend so output is actually wearable. Ages 8–12. Well-reviewed, no fiddly tools required.

**2. Cool Maker Stack'd Heishi Studio**
~$20 | Rotating bead carousel with beading pen speeds up the process considerably — good for kids who lose patience with loose bead chaos. 1,200+ heishi beads, makes ~15 bracelets. Ages 7+.

**3. Rainbow Loom (original kit)**
~$15–20 | Rubber band loom with hook — different skill type (weaving vs. stringing), produces more structurally complex bracelets. 4x TOTY award winner, large tutorial community on YouTube so the kid isn't stuck if instructions are unclear. Ages 7+.

All three are Prime-eligible and under $25. If she's already done loom bracelets, skip #3 and go #1 or #2.

These three are adult-beginner level kits — real tools, real findings, real wire. Appropriate for a capable 9-year-old, possibly with light supervision on the pliers/wire work.

---

**1. Xmada Jewelry Making Supplies Kit — ~$20–25**
1,587 pcs: gemstone chip beads (20 colors), silver pendants, earring hooks, head pins, jump rings, lobster clasps, beading wire (clear/silver/gold), flat-nose pliers, jump ring opener, tweezers. Organized in a 24-grid box. Makes bracelets, necklaces, earrings. No hand-holding — open design, she figures out what she wants to make.
https://www.amazon.com/Xmada-Jewelry-Making-Kit-Supplies/dp/B093DPWBVC

---

**2. MODDA Deluxe Jewelry Making Kit — ~$30**
900 pcs glass beads, charms, findings (jump rings, eye pins, head pins, earring hooks, lobster clasps, crimp beads), beading wire, plus access to a 15-video course. The video course is the differentiator — structured learning rather than trial and error. Marketed at 13+, but the skill level is beginner; a capable 9-year-old is fine.
https://www.amazon.com/Supplies-Instructions-Necklace-Beginners-Christmas/dp/B0897378BF

---

**3. PAXCOO Crystal Jewelry Making Kit — ~$25–30**
28 colors of gemstone chip beads, jewelry wire, silver pendants, earring hooks, head pins, jump rings, lobster clasps, and jewelry pliers. Skews slightly more toward open-ended design than MODDA. Better bead variety than Xmada, fewer total components than MODDA.
https://www.amazon.com/Crystal-Jewelry-Making-Gemstone-Supplies/dp/B09NDLH6VZ

---

**Recommendation if choosing one:** MODDA, because the video course removes the "now what?" problem a kid hits after opening a supply kit.
