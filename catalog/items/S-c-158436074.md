None of us has a clue about the kind of power we possess.

No, it's not power in numbers.

No, you don't need to organize.

No, you don't need "leaders".

YOU NEED JUST TWO THINGS:

You need to be fucking honest.

Then you need to fucking speak up and TELL US WHAT YOU SEE.

That's it.

There's more power in one person's honest observation than there is in massive propaganda, gaslighting, cult indoctrination machines, because just one fact can level a mountain of lies.

Start using your superpowers. You'll find out that the fears which held you back were bullshit, too.
