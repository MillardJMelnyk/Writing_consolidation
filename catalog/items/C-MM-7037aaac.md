### Claude · 2026-09-10 13:25

**Fact check**

**The 75-year secrecy request — right event, wrong defendant.** It wasn't the pharma companies. It was the FDA, through DOJ lawyers, in PHMPT v. FDA (N.D. Texas, filed Sept 2021). The FDA proposed processing about 329,000 pages at 500 pages a month — 55 years, to 2076. Then the page count rose to 451,000+ and they still asked for 500 a month — 75 years, to 2096. Judge Pittman rejected it on Jan 6, 2022 and ordered 55,000 pages a month.

**The second, shorter attempt — right, and it's two things.** The FDA came back asking to push its first 55,000-page production to May 1, and Pfizer moved to intervene on Jan 21, 2022 to keep trade-secret material from being released. Pittman denied Pfizer on Feb 2.

Fix the attribution and your point gets sharper, not weaker: the agency that licensed the product is the one that went to court to bury the file for 75 years.

**"Should have been revealed as part of the regulatory process" — correct, and it's not an accident.** Raw trial data has never been public at approval. FDA reads it in confidence, the public gets summaries, and the underlying data only comes out by FOIA lawsuit. That's the standing system, not a COVID exception.

**"5 to 15 years" — this one is wrong, and it's the line that will get you dismissed.** There is no such rule in medical science. You don't need it. The real number is worse and it's on the record: FDA's own COVID guidance required a *median of two months* of safety follow-up after the last dose before authorization. Then the placebo groups were unblinded and offered the shot within months, which destroyed the only clean long-term comparison that would ever exist. Two months, and then they burned the control group. Use that.

**Operation Warp Speed — real program, and the speed was real, but not where you're pointing it.** The Phase 3 trials were full size — Pfizer enrolled about 44,000. What got compressed was follow-up time, the gaps between phases, review time, and manufacturing built in parallel. So "they skipped the trials" is attackable. "They cut the observation window to two months and then eliminated the control arm" is not.

**"Safe and effective" — the messaging outran the data.** The trials measured symptomatic infection over roughly two months. They never tested whether the shot stopped transmission. Pfizer's own executive confirmed that in front of the EU Parliament in October 2022 — after two years of mandates justified by exactly that claim.

**One thing I can't check.** You say the information is surfacing "only now." Most of the PHMPT documents came out in 2022–2023. If you mean something released recently, tell me what it is and I'll check that specifically.
