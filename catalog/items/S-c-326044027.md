What the fuck is wrong with everybody?

The fact that you, like Abby, see this as a fight is the successful result of the mindfuck done to you by the very same psychotic "powers" you pretend you're going to fight.

You're fighting for control of a slave-powered train designed, created, and controlled by people you fully believe are powerful and, increasingly, realize are evil.

It's a joke.

The whole system runs on slavery and extortion, was *designed* to run on them, so the whole system is an accurate expression of the psychosis we've long called "power"—and *you want to fight over it* because, in your mind, it’s the only thing that matters.

Completely delusional.

First, how could you ever hope to win a fight when you're so mindfucked, oblivious to the fact that the fight itself is part of the psychosis?

But let’s say you won this "fight".

OK.

*What are you going to do with the slave-run extortion train?*

Keep it going with you at the controls?

> You never change things by fighting the existing reality. To change something, build a new model that makes the existing model obsolete.
>
> — R. Buckminster Fuller

Bucky was half right—only half, because he never had to work in a war zone. In a war zone, you have to fight, but not to “win”. You have to fight to hold the destroyers at bay so the work on the new model can be done.

In this sense, “nonviolence” is a hypocritical crack-smoke dream espoused by people who have never confronted a destroyer in their lives—let alone *overcome* them.

This is why Paul the apostle wrote that our fight is not against flesh and blood.

*It doesn’t matter if you kill the beast, because the mind that rules the beast remains.*

Jesus said the same thing, standing on the opposite side:

> Do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul; fear rather the one able to destroy both soul and body in the valley of child sacrifice and eradication (*Gehenna*).
>
> — Matthew 10:28

He didn’t say, “Have courage when you fight…”

He said, “Do not fear…”

The impression that there is something fearful to fight is a *delusion*.

It doesn't matter if you win a fight against flesh and blood when both you and those you conquered continue psycho-spiritually locked under exactly the same psychosis responsible for the fight in the first place.

It doesn't matter who controls the slave extortion train. What matters is that you condone and endorse and cling to it, refusing to get off of it.

And you have no fucking clue in hell how to get off it, what to do and where to go once you’re off it, and no concept or confidence that you could survive if you escaped.

Do you?

So, you have absolutely no intention of getting off it.

Do you?

And that's why you fight over it. And that's why the fight is phony, ridiculous, and insane.

But it's all you know.

You use that as your excuse, too. Even though you know for a fucking fact where the corrective for your ignorance lies, you refuse it. You absolutely refuse it.

You refuse even to look in its direction.

You'd rather cling to the fake innocence of ignorance and die than rectify your stupidity and live.

I guess you'll have to mount this fight, win this fight, and realize that you accomplished absolutely nothing positive before, maybe, you'll stop clinging to your psychosis.

And by the way, Abby: Paulo Feire would have strenuously disagreed with you.

It does not “lie on our shoulders”, we who “are in the imperial core”.

Bullshit. That’s just your imperial arrogance talking.

> The oppressors, who oppress, exploit, and rape by virtue of their power, cannot find in this power the strength to liberate either the oppressed or themselves. Only power that springs from the weakness of the oppressed will be sufficiently strong to free both.
>
> — Paulo Freire, *Pedagogy of the Oppressed* (1968)
