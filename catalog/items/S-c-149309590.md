Great article to get you to start thinking outside the box.

If you don’t like what a government is doing, stop paying them to do it, for example. It’s a wonder that it’s taken us so long just mention the idea of doing this openly.

The blind side of Chris’s thinking is that “Blue” is any less totalitarian than “Red”. This would be a bit clearer if he’d used the correct term for what he’s talking about: *totalitarianism*. I get it: *fascism* is sexier. That’s fine—but it literally leaves other forms of totalitarianism aside, such as the Blue form.

The Blues proved their totalitarianism in spades in Seattle during the COVID spectacle. I dunno, maybe NY or SF were worse, but I warrant not by much. I, and some of my sons as well, met people who would have seen us dead, literally. I and my sons were literally, physically accosted, assaulted, cops called on us, mobbed too. If they hadn’t been so well-indoctrinated that lynching people is a Bozo No-no, I wouldn’t put it past them. Some actually said it: people like me should not exist. Others didn’t have the balls to say it, but I could see it in their eyes, their expressed hatred, their whole-body despisal. Fucking totalitarians who would rather rid the world of me than tolerate someone who fundamentally disagreed with them.

That’s not totalitarian? The fuck it’s not. That’s not fascism? Well, the operative difference being… what?

Chris presents options worth talking about. The problem is finding people who aren’t so indoctrinated and cultic that they can manage such a “courageous” feat. Imagine that: merely talk hypothetically about completely scrapping what’s obviously, mortally dysfunctional, go back to the drawing board, and try again. How brave!

Intelligent people would do exactly that—and they do: in every aspect of life where it doesn’t threaten their sense of security. Otherwise, “*Fuck them*! *Off with their heads*!”

I don’t think Chris intended to go there in this piece—so it’s not a fault—but he never asks *why*. Not how—that’s the empirical question. *Why*? The *psychological* question.

Isn’t it remarkable that, once legitimized as part of government, the psycho-fucks he mentions were able to do the most heinous evils and retain enough public assent so as not only to keep on keeping on, but make every next step more horrific than the one before? Mind-boggling—except each one of us has the feel for what that would have been like had we been there. In terms of the honesty and integrity required to stand alone, against a crowd—let alone against an entire city, county, state, or country—we’re all, basically, still in the state of emotional maturity we had in 10th grade.. or 8th grade… or 6th grade, depending.

And why is everyone so fucking fanatically obsessed with maintaining the current “order” and following the rules, the law, the authoritative dictates, when they are undeniably anti-life, anti-human, antagonistic to every human value we hold dear?

That’s the result of a shitload of mind-raped, soul-crushing indoctrination and behavioral conditioning, truly of cultic magnitudes.

A single, simple, and amazingly useful concept captures the “why”: *codependence*. (<https://reloveution.substack.com/i/170534838/codependence>)

One of the best ways to identify codependence is: blind demand for “leaders”.

If you would feel completely lost without “leaders”, you’re a codependent.

Codependents *always* drive in a totalitarian direction, because totalitarianism is the crown jewel of every “leader” (the euphemism for authoritarian ruler) and every one of their followers/subjects. It’s the predictable, psychologically inevitable, thoroughly logical result of every form of authoritarian rule.

Most people deliberately maintain their denial of this (contrary to evidence and historical fact) and fantasize a variety of delusionary regulation mechanisms that they mistakenly believe serve to limit the totalitarianism of “leaders”—*but that’s the whole point*.

If the essential, authentic drive of people who function as “leaders” and those who demand and elevate them as “leaders” were not totalitarian, if it were not something that *requires* regulation to keep it from going “too far” in the totalitarian direction—in other words, if leaving "leadership" unfettered and unregulated would take us in the direction *opposite* totalitarianism, then why the “checks and balances”? Why the “oversight”? Why the audits? Why establish “human rights” as a baseline to curb the atrocities of “leaders” (supposedly) once they’ve become secure in their positions, just like Chris describes?

Why can’t we we implicitly trust our “leaders”?

Well, because we all know what would fucking happen if we did.

So, why put “leaders” in positions of “power” when we can’t implicitly trust them?

Because we were mind-fucked to believe we’ve got no choice, no alternative, nothing better. I’m with Chris on this. I hope we’ll start realizing the mind-fuckery we endured, admit how it fucked us up, and start going outside the fuck box.

Every single one of the -isms we’ve, so far, found credible are totalitarian, and “totalitarianism” is just a fancy word for *cultism*.

Maybe discussing some of Chris’s points will help people realize their own codependence and cultism. I hope so.

Bottom line, though, unless we reject and escape codependence, we’ll just keep figuring out new ways to lay totalitarian tracks and ride them in our cult trains, fighting tooth and nail against genuine freedom, not to mention everyone whose tracks run counter to ours.
