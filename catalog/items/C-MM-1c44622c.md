### Claude · 2026-08-07 21:39

Receiving: ~1% deducted from the payment before it lands in your Payoneer balance. Withdrawing to a Mexican bank: since USD converts to pesos, that's cross-currency — roughly 1.2–2% above mid-market on top, so total cost lands around 2–3% of what lingoforme sends.
