I just read up on Directive 51. It uses the word "continuity", but it's actually doing the precise opposite. It provides for the ***discontinuation*** of the powers of Congress and the judiciary, placing all the power in the hands of the executive branch—which is where it already is, for the most part. But it legitimates the suspension of the current charade of pretending that the judiciary and Congress still have some power...
