Why is Assange suing over the Nobel Peace Prize being awarded to María Corina Machado?

### Claude · 2025-12-20 13:19

Assange filed a criminal complaint (December 18, 2025) in Sweden against the Nobel Foundation, not a lawsuit in the conventional sense. The core claims:

**Legal basis:** Alfred Nobel's 1895 will mandates the Peace Prize go to those who promote "fraternity between nations," work for "abolition or reduction of standing armies," and promote "peace congresses." Assange argues Machado's conduct violates all three criteria.

**Specific allegations against Machado:**

* Inciting and endorsing "commission of international crimes" by the United States as it applies military pressure to force Venezuela's President Nicolas Maduro to step down
* Vocal support for Israel during its ongoing war in Gaza, including a phone call with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu shortly after her Nobel win
* Justified US strikes on boats off Venezuela's coast, which have killed 95 people to date

**Relief sought:** Freezing of the 11 million kroner ($1.18 million) in prize money to prevent funds from being "diverted from their charitable purpose to facilitate aggression."

**Complaint targets:** Around 30 individuals, including Nobel Foundation chair Astrid Soderbergh Widding and executive director Hanna Stjarne

**Status:** Swedish police confirmed they received the complaint. It was also submitted to the Swedish Economic Crime Authority, which said it did not have the jurisdiction to handle it.
