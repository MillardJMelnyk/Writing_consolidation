**The Discovery vs. Creation Problem**

**The Bootstrapping Problem**

**The Universality Intuition**

While your English word analogy is clever, rights claims seem different from linguistic conventions. The convergence of moral systems on certain principles - despite lacking common stipulation - points toward something more fundamental.
