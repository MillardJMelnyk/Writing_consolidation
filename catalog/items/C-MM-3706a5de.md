### Millard · 2026-09-27 04:22

What was the context, intention, and purpose for the creation of the original powder fired firearms?

### Claude · 2026-09-27 04:22

They came out of Song-dynasty China, where gunpowder (a 9th-century accident by Taoist alchemists chasing an immortality elixir) was turned into the fire lance, a spear with a gunpowder tube first recorded at the 1132 siege of De'an, purely as a military weapon to burn, terrify, and wound attackers at close range during the Song's wars against the Jurchen Jin and later the Mongols, and it evolved into metal-barreled hand cannons by the late 1200s.
