Something nonviolence proponents have never understood, and I wonder if they even want to:

There is a difference between addiction to violence and utilization of violence.

Using violence does not in anyway imply that you’re addicted to it.

Those who live by violence are absolutely addicted to it. Violence is the most addictive “substance” on Earth for broken human beings. Substance addiction is likely a refuge for those who otherwise would have no option than violence: death to others or death to themselves.

Simply noticing this uncovers the base stupidity of the blanket claim that violence is wrong. That claim is made by people who don’t understand what they espouse at a ridiculously simple level.

Question: Did Jesus toss the moneychangers’ tables and brandish his whip GENTLY or VIOLENTLY?
