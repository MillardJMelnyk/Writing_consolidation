Some offendedness is legit. Offended ego is not.

I have no obligation to cater to your ego. I have no responsibility for the offense that the truth I tell you might cause your ego.

If the first thing that registers for you is offense that overshadows your honest desire to know what's really going on (aka, truth,) I'm talking about you below.

No one owes your ego shitall.

The biggest egotists of all are people who take up the offense of other egotists, casting them as victims.

Victimhood is egotism. Being victimized is one thing. That's an incident, not a state we capitulate into. Victimhood is a condition of mind in which we lose all track of ourselves, our agency, our power, and our honesty.

Genuine offense creates outrage, not complaint. It has *nothing* to do with the "injustice" a violator did to us. That's history. Victimhood is transient, short-lived, quickly driving us to do all we can to make sure no one again violates *anyone* like we were violated. It triggers power and action, not entrapment and pleading that scream for advocacy. The outraged don't need or wait for advocates to stand and speak for them, because you don't bring talkers to an overthrow.
