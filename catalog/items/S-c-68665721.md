Fundamentalists get it fucking ass-backwards.

There is no such thing as a "great nation".

So, there is no nation that could be made "great again".

There is no "again" because there's no "ever was".

The savior they preach is going to “strike down” *all of them.*

Which means: ALL NATIONS ARE EVIL.

"Fallen, fallen is Babylon the great, she who has made all the nations drink of the wine of the passion of her sexual immorality.” (Rev. 14:8)

"Coming out of his mouth is a sharp sword with which to strike down the nations." (Rev. 19:15)

And the kicker:

"And in her [Babylon] was found the blood of prophets and of saints, and of all who have been slaughtered on the earth.” (Rev. 18:24)

It doesn't matter if the fundamentalists are right or wrong, and it doesn’t matter whether those quotes are "true" or "false".

This note is about *hypocrisy*, not prophesy.
