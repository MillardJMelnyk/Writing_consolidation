It is a psychosis, but it goes further and deeper and back so far that none of us really get it.

It's the psychosis required for "civilization" to sound like a good thing, and "uncivilized" a horrid one.

It's the psychosis in which trapped, brutalized, manipulated people looked at those still living in freedom and called them "wild", "barbaric", "bestial", "savage", "feral".

It's the psychosis in which we elevate certain types -- degrees of psychopath, every one -- to such heights that it makes sense to call them "leaders" and abdicate all the functions that make us human, outsourcing them to people we never met, don't know, have no good reason to trust, and who prove, decade after decade and generation after generation, that they domesticated us to farm, fleece, flay, and fillet us at their pleasure.

It's called "codependence", and it's easily curable.
