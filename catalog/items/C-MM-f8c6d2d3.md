### Claude · 2026-10-22 15:36

That screen has no link to the parent note, so tap the share icon (bottom right of the note), copy the link, and open it in your phone's browser, where the note you replied to should show above Muad'Dib's reply (Likely). If it doesn't, your own note is under your profile picture (top right of Activity) → Notes tab, where you can tap it to see the replies.
