Two places to check:

**2. Local Group Policy (if setting #1 doesn't fix it)**

If `gpedit.msc` doesn't exist (Home edition), the block is more likely MDM/Family Safety related — check Microsoft Family Safety settings on your Microsoft account, or Settings → Accounts → Family & other users for any managed restrictions.

### Claude · 2026-07-27 23:10

Being an administrator doesn't bypass SmartScreen's "blocked for your protection" screen — that's a separate layer (app reputation / policy), not a file-permission check. Admin rights control what you're *allowed* to do; SmartScreen controls what Windows *lets run* regardless of permissions, until you disable the check itself (steps above) or the file/publisher builds enough reputation.
