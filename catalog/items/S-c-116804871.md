I still can't get over that there are people who expect me to believe them when they say America is a land of freedom.

Freedom where? To do what? To go where?

I know *exactly* what you can and can't do without the money to pay for it.

I know *exactly* what it takes to earn the money to buy my freedom, bit by bit, experience by experience -- and end up with no more freedom than I started with.

What a fucked up kind of "freedom" it is when your options are to ***earn*** a living -- ***EARN*** *THE RIGHT TO MERELY* ***LIVE*** -- or live what's left of your miserable existence in total shame, and die with people believing you deserved it.
