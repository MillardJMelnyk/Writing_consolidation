I call responses like these [feasibility fakeouts](https://reloveution.substack.com/i/170534838/feasibility-fakeout). They’re fakeouts because objections raised over feasibility have absolutely nothing to do with the most important question at hand: what do we fucking want?  
Feasibility concerns whether it can be done successfully and advantageously. Merit concerns whether we want it enough to make it worth trying. Raising feasibility concerns as if they have a bearing on the merit and desirability of a proposition only shows that the objector doesn’t really know what’s going on. An idea’s merit and desirability have nothing to do with implementation issues. Merit and desirability are an issue all on their own. Feasibility is a separate, subsequent question that isn’t even relevant unless we have already settled the more important issue.  
How the fuck are you going to determine the feasibility of an idea before you’ve tried it to see if it’s feasible, let alone before you’ve even decided that you want to? Therein lies the irrationality.
