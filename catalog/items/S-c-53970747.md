I wanted to quantify something I realized decades ago, so I had a couple of AI chats about it this morning: the incredibly small, infinitesimal fraction of what’s really gone on that supports human knowledge.

You can see my chat with Gemini here (you have to scroll way up, the link takes you to the end of the chat): <https://gemini.google.com/app/2e65be7663b34d45>

and the follow-up with ChatGPT here: <https://chat.openai.com/share/ea0eb17f-0e01-46c7-a628-6b5ee4f46db8>

We arrived at a number: our “knowledge” is based on observations of only around 50 sextillionths of the universe.

But that’s not all. We have to factor in the historical side, too. And then we have to factor in the faction of what people call “knowledge” that even takes into account that 50 sextillionths-worth of observations, “knowledge” having been mostly a matter of dogma, intuitions, hunches, guesses, tradition, myth which, in turn, is mostly based on a HUGE old pile of steaming bullshit that someones, somewheres, at some times just made the fuck up.

So, we arrived at 4.2 × 10 to the -27. No idea, right? Me neither. I asked ChatGPT to put that in human language.

A “trillionth of a trillionth of a trillionth”.

So, HOW THE FUCK do people get away with saying “I know” when you consider that we have a near-nonexistent basis in observed facts for anything we call “knowledge”?

This is why people “believe”.

When there’s no fucking way an person could credibly pretend there’s any real basis for “knowing”, but they want to proceed as if there were anyway, they say, “I believe that…”

Can you get an inkling of how dishonest, deceptive, and devious this stuff is that we call “knowledge”, and even worse, “belief”?

Everything we think we know is almost entirely bullshit, when you look at it like that.

We’ve got shitloads of hubris that most people consider “normal” for a “well-adjusted” person.

It stinks. And it makes people caked with the stuff glaring. See it, feel it, and smell it coming.
