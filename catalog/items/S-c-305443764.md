So think a minute how psychotic a person has to be to use "just following orders" or "just doing my job" as an exonerating defense.

"It wasn't my fault" isn't just beside the point -- who gives a fuck about "fault" when it's the damage that matters? It indicates a complete reversal of priorities in which moralism has overruled reality.

This is proof of a complete break from reality. The victim bleeding out becomes less important than establishing who is to blame for it.

This is nothing less than psychosis, because it's the mechanism that enables people to obliterate others in the name of "righteousness".

And if you don't think that's psychotic, then you yourself must be psychotic.
