What's the difference between legitimized and legitimated?
They are synonyms with no meaningful difference in meaning.
Legitimated = past tense/past participle of the verb legitimate (to make something lawful, valid, or acceptable).
Legitimized = past tense/past participle of the verb legitimize (same meaning).
Usage notes:
Legitimize / legitimized is far more common in modern English (especially American English).
Legitimate / legitimated is older, slightly more formal, and less frequent as a verb today.
