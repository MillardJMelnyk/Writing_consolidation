Well, yet another rant from one wing of the bird to the other as if "There can only be ONE!"

I agree with much of that you say. It's been a fucking psycho-show ever since 2020.

But what you're actually criticizing aren't the libs/blues, nor are the MAGAs/reds just peachy. You're criticizing the the very "rule of law" and rule by elitist parasites, because the shit that's going on now, BOTH sides, is the inexorable, predictable result when you conceive of "rule" as the imposition of monopolized violence by an elitist class. It doesn't matter what color you paint it. Society founded on the monopolization and imposition of violence is evil, face paint or none. And when people praise evil as if their lives depended on it, you're looking at entire sick societies chock full of psychotics. No -- you can no more get good from evil than good water from a cesspool.
