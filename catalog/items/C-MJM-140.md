### Claude · 2025-08-08 19:59

Current phrases for praise/admiration:

"Gas up" - give compliments to boost confidence
"Slay" - doing something exceptionally well  
"That's fire" or "it's fire" - really good/impressive
"No cap" - I'm being honest/real (when praising)
"Shout out" (SO) - public acknowledgment/praise

"Gas up" seems most common for direct praise equivalent to your examples.

### Claude · 2025-08-08 20:00

For someone handling a difficult situation well, try:

"You handled that brilliantly."
"You absolutely crushed it."
"That was expertly done."
"You nailed it."

Or more current: "You ate that" (slang for dominated/excelled at something).
