Foster, you're completely missing the real problem.

Morality is a symptom of the problem, not its cure.

Codependence is the problem. Only codependents need morality, because they need someone else to tell them when they're "good" or "bad".

In other words, aside from authorities and external systems, structures, and institutions, they literally don't know who the fuck they are.

In the metaphor of Jesus, codependence is the "broad way" and full humanness is the "narrow way". You can preserve ego on the broad way. You substitute the judgment of others for your own on the broad way.

The "strait gate" is your decision to love and cling to the truth, to honesty, to the best for BOTH others and yourself, and stop betraying yourself for the sake of the delusory "benefits" promised on the broad way -- where you pretend to move forward "together".

The strait gate allow only 1 to pass at a time, and only one -- you -- to begin on the way that is yours and yours alone.

Morality not only can't accomplish that, it doesn't even try, and it was never intended to.
