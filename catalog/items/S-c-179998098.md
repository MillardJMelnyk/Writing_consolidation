Dehumanizing others is actually the secondary purpose of propaganda. The first purpose is to put you into a mental position in which you will accept its second purpose.

The first purpose of propaganda is to make you forget that you are human. It isn't until you have been this dehumanized in your own mind that you will behave stupidly enough to accept the humanizing others.

The second dehumanization is called supremacism.

The first dehumanization is called codependence.
