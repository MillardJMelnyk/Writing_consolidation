Anyone who speaks the truth does know.

So, connect the dots. If you speak truth to people who don't know how hard it is, it means you're speaking to people who do not speak the truth -- otherwise, they too would know.

So, you're speaking to liars.

Why speak truth to liars?

1. Humiliate them by exposing their lies.

2. Realize, when you see they can't be humiliated, that they're psychopaths. Proceed accordingly.

3. Demonstrate to those who want to stop being liars that speaking truth is far easier and safer to do than they ever dreamed.

4. Force liars to react, revealing what they actually are beneath their sheepskin of choice.
