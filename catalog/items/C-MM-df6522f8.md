### Claude · 2026-10-15 16:29

Around 2008, as a back-formation from "degrowth," which first appeared at the First Degrowth Conference held in Paris in 2008 as the English rendering of the French *décroissance* (Likely; I found no dictionary first-use date for the verb itself).
