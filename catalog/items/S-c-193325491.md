It's not tricky or difficult.

5 years ago, would you have believed where things are now?

That shows you won't believe where things will be 5 years from now, either -- and you'd be wrong.

I knew which direction things were going, and that's proven out. I had no fucking clue how fast and how far it would go by now, though.

To stop being wrong year after year, you'll have to change the way your mind works -- because your own experience proves, at any point in time, that you don't have a fucking clue what's really going on nor where it's headed.

But you'd have to be honest and look at yourself to see it.
