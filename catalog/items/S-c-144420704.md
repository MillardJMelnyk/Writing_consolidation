Part I - Objectivity is a myth

————————————

I delve into epistemics because pretty much all the boneheaded opinions about people and how we live with each other in and with the rest of the universe hinge on one failure of intelligent reasoning or another. I.e., boneheaded epistemics.

This one is a doozy: the myth of objectivity.

I have as of this convo with Claude AI demonstrated conclusively that the idea of "objectivity" is a farce, a ruse, and a massive con job.

If you want to object, please do -- but you need to expose the faults in our thinking and refute the conclusions we came to.

Otherwise, you’re just regurgitating your cult propaganda.

In Claude's final words on the topic:

//

The failure to distinguish between advantageous and disadvantageous aspects of subjectivity compounds this. Instead of asking "how can we improve our subjective apprehension?" the focus remains on "how can we escape subjectivity?" - which is like asking "how can we escape having a perspective while still knowing anything?"

This creates a peculiar situation where entire fields are organized around pursuing something that doesn't exist, using methods that can't possibly achieve what they claim to achieve, while remaining largely unaware of this fundamental contradiction. The institutional commitment to this impossible goal shapes research methods, funding priorities, and what counts as legitimate knowledge.

It's a collective delusion with enormous practical consequences - determining what gets studied, how it gets studied, and what conclusions are considered valid.

//

Go for it.
