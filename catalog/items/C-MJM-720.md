**Real:** Letters were sent. **Misleading by omission:**

* "Criminal referral" ≠ indictment, charge, or conviction. These are private advocacy petitions asking AGs to open investigations — not government action, not court findings. No AG has publicly charged anyone off these letters as of this video.
* Offense list (murder, terrorism, racketeering, etc.) are the petitioners' legal theories/allegations, not adjudicated facts.
* Claimed result: "two active investigations/grand juries" per the filers themselves — unconfirmed by any AG office in independent reporting I found. Take that number as asserted-by-plaintiff, not verified.

**Source credibility flag:** Poster (Nicolas Hulscher) is McCullough Foundation epidemiologist/administrator — not a lawyer, not party to the filings, functions as amplifier/commentator. His broader publication record (ivermectin/mebendazole cancer claims, vaccine "negative efficacy" claims) has drawn documented fact-check pushback and at least one retracted paper — relevant to his reliability as a narrator, not to whether the letters themselves exist.

**Bottom line:** The filing campaign is real and verifiable across multiple outlets. The video's framing implies imminent/ongoing prosecution — unsupported. This is Stage 1 (petition), not Stage 2 (investigation) or Stage 3 (charges).
