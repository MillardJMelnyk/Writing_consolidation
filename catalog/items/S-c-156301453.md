We were taught that the world changes as a result of the actions of "leaders".

That's a lie, in the same way that saying MLK was responsible for Civil Rights would be a lie. MLK himself rejected the idea:

> I’m tired of people crediting me with starting this movement. The Montgomery Bus Boycott would have happened without me. The people were ready.

— Martin Luther King Jr., as recalled by Coretta Scott King in My Life with Martin Luther King, Jr. (1969).

"Leaders" are just not how major change happens. They wage wars and genocides and tyrannies, they fleece and butcher their own people, but how is that "change"?

That's just the same old same-old fuckery.
