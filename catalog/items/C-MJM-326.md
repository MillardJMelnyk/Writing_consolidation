Who is involved in murdering Fred Hampton

### Claude · 2025-11-20 23:58

Fred Hampton, Chairman of the Illinois Black Panther Party, was killed on December 4, 1969, in a pre-dawn raid on his Chicago apartment. Those involved:

**Direct participants:**

* **Chicago Police Department** – Officers from the State's Attorney's Special Prosecutions Unit conducted the raid. Fourteen officers fired approximately 90-99 rounds; only one shot was confirmed fired by a Panther occupant.
* **Cook County State's Attorney Edward Hanrahan** – Authorized and directed the raid.

**Federal involvement:**

* **FBI (COINTELPRO)** – The FBI, under J. Edgar Hoover's directive, targeted Hampton as a priority. FBI informant **William O'Neal**, who had infiltrated the Panthers and become Hampton's bodyguard, provided a floor plan of the apartment to the FBI, which was passed to Chicago police. O'Neal also reportedly drugged Hampton the night before, rendering him unconscious during the raid.

**1982 civil settlement:**
A federal civil rights lawsuit resulted in a $1.85 million settlement paid by the City of Chicago, Cook County, and the federal government—an implicit acknowledgment of wrongdoing by all three entities.

**Key finding:**
A federal grand jury and subsequent investigations concluded Hampton was likely shot while unconscious or incapacitated, and that the raid was coordinated between local law enforcement and federal agencies as part of COINTELPRO's program to neutralize Black Panther leadership.

### Millard · 2025-11-20 23:59

Did his girlfriend an unborn baby survive

### Claude · 2025-11-20 23:59

Yes.

**Deborah Johnson** (now Akua Njeri), Hampton's fiancée, was in bed next to him during the raid. She was approximately eight months pregnant. She survived and was pulled from the room before the final shots were fired into Hampton.

**Fred Hampton Jr.** was born on December 29, 1969—25 days after his father's death. He survived and later became a community activist and chairman of the Prisoners of Conscience Committee (POCC) and Black Panther Party Cubs.
