**Memory-related**, or **memorial** (as in "memorial capacity"), though "memorial" usually means commemorative.

Plain-language options: **mental** (broad), **recollective**, or **retentive** (relating to the ability to retain memory).

### Millard · 2026-09-28 20:39

### Claude · 2026-09-28 20:39

Not missing. English has no common standalone adjective for "of or relating to memory"; the job is done by the noun used attributively: "memory" (memory loss, memory span, memory test). "Mnemonic" and "mnemic" are the only single-word adjectives, and both are technical or narrower.

"Memorial" and "memorable" got captured by other senses (commemoration, worth remembering), which is why no plain adjective filled the slot.
