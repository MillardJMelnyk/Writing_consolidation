what gives ppt in planet crafter

### Claude · 2026-08-27 03:08

Oxygen — ppt is just the next unit up from ppq on the oxygen scale, so anything that makes oxygen: Vegetubes T1–T3, Algae Generators T1/T2, Grass/Flower/Tree Spreaders, Seed Spreader Rocket, Biodomes, Fish Farm, T2 Aquarium.

### Millard · 2026-08-27 04:22

how to get kti?

### Claude · 2026-08-27 04:23

Ti is the sum of your heat, pressure, and oxygen, so you get kTi by just stacking more heaters, drills, and vegetubes/algae generators until the total crosses 1,000 Ti.

### Millard · 2026-08-27 04:23

does it mattter where i put them>?

### Claude · 2026-08-27 04:23

No — output is the same anywhere. Spreaders don't need spacing between them; the game only counts whether they're placed. Only real constraints: some things must go outdoors, ore extractors need to sit on the right deposit, and T3 tree spreaders grow huge so keep them away from your base.
