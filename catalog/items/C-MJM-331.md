### Claude · 2025-11-21 23:31

To find your Facebook draft post:

**On mobile (Facebook app):**

1. Tap the "What's on your mind?" box at the top of your feed
2. If a draft exists, a prompt should appear asking if you want to restore it

**On desktop:**

1. Click the "What's on your mind?" box
2. Look for a "Restore" or "You have a draft" notification

**If that doesn't work:**
Facebook drafts are device-specific and session-dependent. They don't sync across devices and may expire if you logged out, cleared cache/cookies, or too much time passed.

Is this for personal profile, a Page you manage, or a Group? Page drafts have a different location.
