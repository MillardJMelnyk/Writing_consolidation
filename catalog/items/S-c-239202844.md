99 out of 100 people I hear using "cognitive dissonance" have not a fucking clue what it is. Framing it around "beliefs" is so shallow, you might as well just be skipping stones...

Cognitive dissonance *pales* to ***affective*** *dissonance*. How often do you hear that term being bandied around?

Affective dissonance is why you created "core beliefs" in the first fucking place.
