### Millard · 2026-10-11 18:02

Are you familiar with the concept of content independence in political science

### Claude · 2026-10-11 18:02

Yes — it's Hart's idea that an authority's directive gives you a reason to comply because of who issued it, regardless of what it says, which is the core of debates over political obligation and legitimacy.

### Millard · 2026-10-11 18:03

Is there strong consensus on the idea among proponents of political authority and law

### Claude · 2026-10-11 18:03

Broad agreement, not strong consensus: Hart, Raz, Green and most authority theorists treat content-independence as a defining feature of authority, but its meaning and even coherence are disputed, with critics like Markwick, Sciaraffa, Gur and Hershovitz arguing it's either confused or not what actually makes authority work.

### Millard · 2026-10-11 18:04

### Claude · 2026-10-11 18:05

It's a formal property of practical reasons: a claim about where a directive's binding force comes from — its source and form (who issued it, through what procedure) rather than the merits of what it demands.
