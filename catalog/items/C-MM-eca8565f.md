### Claude · 2026-08-08 00:56

Stalls get built automatically by families assigned to jobs that produce goods (granary, storehouse, farms, hunters, apiary, etc.) once you designate a marketplace area — you don't build stalls directly. Assign workers to the Storehouse and Granary, and those families will begin opening stalls in the marketplace selling goods stored in each building.

If nobody's building stalls:

* **No producers assigned nearby** — you need families working jobs that generate sellable goods (farming, hunting, gathering, granary/storehouse work). No workers = no stalls.
* **Market already saturated** — markets fill in the order you built them; a second marketplace won't get stalls until the first one is completely full. If you have multiple markets, this is the usual cause.
* **Zero goods surplus** — if production barely covers consumption, there's nothing to stock a stall with, so no family opens one.
* **Distance/pathing weirdness** — some report stalls stay empty even when the market's close, tied to whether granary/storehouse workers are actually hauling goods there.

Fix that consistently works: reassign or add new people to your storehouse, granary, or woodcutter — that forces a re-check and they'll build stalls. If a market's stuck with a stall that's permanently empty, delete that specific stall and a family will redo it.

Don't bother with a second marketplace to fix distance/upgrade issues — the marketplace isn't based on proximity; if there are enough goods for the village, everyone will be satisfied, and a second marketplace won't fix a shortage.
