### Claude · 2025-08-14 16:22

Yes! The term you're looking for is **"ethico-legal"** or **"ethicolegal"** - it combines ethical (moral) and legal considerations in the same way that "psychosocial" combines psychological and social.

This term is commonly used in:

* Medical ethics (ethico-legal dilemmas in healthcare)
* Professional ethics discussions
* Academic literature on law and morality
* Bioethics

You might also see:

* **"Moral-legal"** (more straightforward, less academic)
* **"Normative-legal"** (where normative refers to moral/ethical norms)

But **"ethico-legal"** is the standard compound term that parallels the structure of "psychosocial" and is widely recognized in academic and professional contexts.
