### Claude · 2025-09-04 17:32

Attack on original statement:

**Major flaws:**

* "Not being a social radical" - irrelevant qualifier, implies radicalism correlates with military/urban planning judgment
* "Cut him some slack" - minimizes potentially catastrophic planning failure
* False premise: No evidence provided that subject actually believes cities can be built during bombardment
* Strawman: Building "in the middle of" bombardment vs. building despite bombardment risk
* Missing context: Who is "him"? What specific claim?

**Improved version:**
"His belief that urban construction can proceed during active bombardment is strategically flawed."

**Counter-steelman:**
Original may refer to post-conflict reconstruction planning or dispersed construction strategies, not literal mid-bombardment building.

**Verification needed:**

* Specific quote/source for the construction claim
* Time period and context
* Definition of "bombardment" (frequency, intensity, duration)
