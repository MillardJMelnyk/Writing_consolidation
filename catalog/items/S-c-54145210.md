There is no significant nor essential difference between enculturation and encultizing, i.e., the process of conditioning which inducts people into cults. The distinction was made (who knows when) in order to normalize the cultism endemic to CULTure, by which some cultism is fine, healthy, and good, while other kinds are reprehensible, sick, and bad.

When people realize this, we as a people will be in a position to free ourselves from the idiocy, brutality, and psychosis which are essential to “civilization”.
