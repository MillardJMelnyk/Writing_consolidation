I haven't heard anyone yet point out the full depths of the psychosis, though.

1. The original claim to the Levant was by a "nation" of psychotics who believed that their god had promised them a territory inhabited by other people whom they were to eradicate by genocide.

2. The typical justification for this was that the current inhabitants were evil and needed to be wiped out.

3. So, Joshua led the children of Israel and invaded, exterminated the inhabitants, and occupied the territory.

4. So, the Zionist claim is that God told their ancestors to be genocidists and hijack the territory, so that gives them the right to do it again.

No one has reflected this level of psychotic, organic violence that is the Jewish mindset. I said "Jewish" deliberately.

A lot of people are claiming that there's a difference between Judaism and Zionism -- and they're right, but not like they mean.

There is a difference between a defective mentality that fosters psychosis and the full-blown psychosis that naturally and inevitably results from it. That is the relationship between Judaism and Zionism. Breeder and spawn. The recent Jewish protestations (which got traction quite suddenly last summer) are lies.

Like Jews are the apple of God’s eye, Shizrael is the apple of every Jew’s eye. Not one of them want to see it disintegrated.

And not one of them has done anything that amounts to a hill of beans and posturing to stop one of the worst cancers on Earth from metastasizing.
