Which mobile app does pepperstone trading offer its clients

### Claude · 2026-01-04 14:42

Pepperstone offers **five mobile trading apps** (as of January 2026):

1. **Pepperstone Trading App** — proprietary app (iOS and Android)
2. **MetaTrader 4 (MT4)** — iOS and Android
3. **MetaTrader 5 (MT5)** — iOS and Android
4. **cTrader** — iOS and Android
5. **TradingView** — iOS and Android (connects to Pepperstone account)

All are available on Google Play and Apple App Store.

### Millard · 2026-01-04 14:42

I already have a MT5 demo account with MetaTrader installed on my PC. Where do I get the pepperstone version of mobile MT5 to use for the same demo account?

### Claude · 2026-01-04 14:43

There is no "Pepperstone version" of MT5 mobile. MT5 is developed by MetaQuotes and is the same app for all brokers.

**Download:**

* iOS: App Store → search "MetaTrader 5"
* Android: Google Play → search "MetaTrader 5"

**Login:**

1. Open MT5 app
2. Tap "Settings" → "New Account" → "Login to an existing account"
3. Search for "Pepperstone" in the broker/server list
4. Select your server (demo server for demo account)
5. Enter your existing MT5 login credentials

Your demo account credentials from desktop MT5 work on mobile.
