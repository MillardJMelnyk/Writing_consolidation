"DO NOT COMPLY." What a pathetic, puerile, sniveling outlook.

Codependent much?

Consider the difference:

**IGNORE**

**DEFY**

**ABANDON**

"I will not comply" is exactly what thugs and bullies and tyrants and monsters WANT, fools.

They LOVE getting showcased as the Goliath that squashes little hapless David.

AND YOU LOVE BEING LITTLE HAPLESS DAVID -- because once in a while you manage to strike a blow.

You get so insanely hyped what that happens. You celebrate, tell stories, hold press conferences, create organizations.

Maybe all that will help you strike another little blow.

Your problem is: every time you step onto the battlefield, you go without knowing Goliath will fall.

In fact, you have no expectation that he will fall. None.

The epitome of your genuine expectations is to make a small dent. That's as high as you've ever aimed. You're going to "do your part".

That's why you get so ecstatic when you manage to inflict a scratch.

In the actual story -- you really should read it sometime -- there was no fucking way in David’s mind that Goliath was not going down.

None.

This wasn't some Herculean feat of mental self-manipulation and -delusion on his part.

That's not what faith is.

David spent a lot of time information gathering, thinking, turning over one scenario after another in his mind. The differences between the lion and the bear and this oversized blind fuck were negligible. And besides, the oaf had to go down. He could not be left standing. No way. He was going down.

By the time David was done thinking about it, he would have had to lapse into psychosis to deny the fact that Goliath was going down. At that point, it wasn’t a hope, a “faith”, a determination. It was a fact. A foregone conclusion. An inevitability.

It was the only thing he could see happening when looking at the situation with a sane mind.

That is what genuine faith is.

But you?

You noncompliers are still operating inside Goliath’s frame of reference. You have never left the false paradigm in which he looms gigantic. You have no concept of taking him down with a stone.

You are all fake Davids.

You not only have no such faith -- you have NO INTENTION OF GETTING IT.

Fuck all you posers.
