### Millard · 2026-09-28 16:37

aree they names or why is the company named this/

### Claude · 2026-09-28 16:37

Not names: the "John Pull and his trained bear" origin story was a hoax from a Spanish satire site that went viral. Real version: "pull" means tug and "bear" means endure, meant to say the clothes are tough and can take anything. Other Spanish sites give different readings, like "pull and carry", and the company's own history page gives no reason for the name. So the "tough clothes" reading is only Likely.
