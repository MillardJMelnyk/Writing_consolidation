do you throw a punch with your right hand off your left or right foot?

### Claude · 2025-06-07 23:25

When throwing a punch with your right hand, you typically step forward with your **left foot** and pivot off your **right foot** (your back foot).

Here's the basic mechanics:

* Your right foot stays planted as your base, providing power and stability
* Your left foot steps forward into the punch for reach and weight transfer
* You pivot on the ball of your right foot, rotating your hips and shoulders
* This hip rotation generates much of the punch's power

This is true for both a straight right cross and most other right-hand punches in boxing and martial arts. The power comes from the ground up - pushing off that back right foot, rotating through your core, and transferring that energy through your arm.

The opposite would be true for a left-hand punch - you'd step with your right foot and pivot off your left.
