The problem with all thinking like this is the fact that it surreptitiously and insidiously pretends, without cause or grounds, that the fictitious entities manipulated in the imaginary world of political and legal entities -- all of them absolutely, incorrigibly, definitionally, and ontologically abstract and disconnected from the real world -- are in fact connected to the real world.

Well, no they're not. But the longer you tell elaborate stories based on the presumption that they are, the more they will appear to be so to those who keep listening to your yarns.
