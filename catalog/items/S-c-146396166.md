Interesting fact about power:

Codependent power depends on numbers. The more people who join, the less power each one adds.

Independent power multiplies. Not only do independent people have more power to contribute, each person added adds 100% of the power they have. 10 people of equal power means 10 times the power of just one of them.

Codependents actually don't contribute any power at all, because they'll tell you themselves that, apart from the movement they joined, they have no power at all.

The power of a movement doesn't come from its people, but from its numbers. That's a very different matter.

So the power of one or more independent people can't even be compared to the lack of power of every single person in a movement.

This is why movements are so easily countered, disrupted, and disintegrated.

It really doesn't take much.

But just try to counter, disrupt, or disintegrate the power of an independent person. I don't mean sit there on your ass and imagine it. I mean get up and go ahead and try to do it, and see what happens.

Good luck! 😁
