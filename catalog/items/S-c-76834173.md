It's so disheartening that even the greatest minds never aspired to eradicating evil. The best that virtually everyone can imagine is holding it in check.

*WHEN THE FUCK DO WE GET RID OF IT?*

Every single argument I've examined that claims we cannot eradicate evil has been based on blind, fearful belief -- no evidence, no facts, no sound logic, no sane thinking. Terrified hysteria lies at the root of them all.

Unless the hysterical can be true, which is essentially a contradiction in terms, all that thinking amounts to bullshit.

Evil will be eradicated. The only question will be whether we contributed or not.
