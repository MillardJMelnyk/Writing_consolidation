Democracy is nothing better than the least obviously egregious form of tyranny, in which a continual (although revolving) near-half a population is always tyrannized by "the majority".

It is not essentially different than "might makes right" -- it is exactly and none other than might makes right, but with a facelift.

As long as self-declared monopolies on legitimized violence exist -- you call them "states" -- and as long as institutions for the imposition of legitimized violence exist -- you call them "governments" -- human rights will be precarious at best and crimes against humanity will persist.

This isn't because a state employs the wrong system of rule, euphemized as "order" and "organization", but because the entire paradigm is founded on violence.

This is the intrinsic irony of so-called civilization: every civilization that has ever existed was based and ultimately relied on the most barbaric behavioral technique known to humankind for both its survival and expansion.

Power corrupts because what is known as "power" is essentially the mastery of mass violence.

Massive injustice and suffering are not just inevitable but guaranteed when the chosen means of control to achieve order is the deliberate infliction of the evil chaos which is violence.

To expect any other result is fully and literally psychotic.
