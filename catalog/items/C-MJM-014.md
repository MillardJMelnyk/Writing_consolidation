For free Level 2 data on GBP/USD, you have several options:

**Direct from Brokers:**

* **Interactive Brokers** - Offers free Level 2 data for forex pairs including GBP/USD with a funded account
* **TD Ameritrade/Charles Schwab** - Provides free forex Level 2 data through thinkorswim platform
* **OANDA** - Free Level 2 market depth data through their trading platform and API

**Trading Platforms:**

* **TradingView** - Limited free Level 2 data, more comprehensive with paid plans
* **MetaTrader 4/5** - Many brokers offer free Level 2 data through MT4/MT5 platforms
* **cTrader** - Several brokers provide free market depth through this platform

**Data Providers:**

* **Yahoo Finance** - Basic market depth information (limited)
* **Investing.com** - Some Level 2 data available for major forex pairs
* **FXCM** - Offers market depth data even for demo accounts

**APIs:**

* **Alpha Vantage** - Free tier includes some forex market depth data
* **Twelve Data** - Limited free Level 2 forex data
* **OANDA API** - Free market depth data with account registration

Keep in mind that truly comprehensive Level 2 data for forex is often limited in free offerings since the forex market is decentralized. Most free sources will show you market depth from specific liquidity providers rather than the complete interbank market picture. For professional trading, you might eventually need paid data feeds from providers like Reuters, Bloomberg, or specialized forex data vendors.
