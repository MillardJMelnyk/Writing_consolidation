Background and NGO Work
-----------------------

Peter Dalglish was a Canadian humanitarian who founded Street Kids International in 1988 alongside Frank O'Dea. Street Kids International focused on three main programmes for street children: street health, street work and street rights. This organization eventually became part of Save the Children.

UN and Other Positions
----------------------

Dalglish was hired by the United Nations in 1985 to coordinate humanitarian assistance for 50,000 children displaced by famine and drought in Sudan. He held multiple UN positions throughout his career:

* Between 2006 and 2010, he served as executive director of the South Asia Children's Fund, which promotes education for disadvantaged children
* He served as Senior Advisor and Deputy Chief of Party for UN-Habitat in Kabul, Afghanistan from October 2010 to December 2014
* Until 2015, he was the Country Representative for UN-Habitat in Afghanistan

Recognition and Awards
----------------------

In December 2016, Peter John Dalglish was named a Member of the Order of Canada. He also received various other honors for his humanitarian work.

Criminal Conviction
