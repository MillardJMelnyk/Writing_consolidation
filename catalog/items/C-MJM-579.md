Over the decades we've used different words for the same thing -- congrats, kudos, props, respect, etc. what are the latest cutting edge versions of this?

### Claude · 2026-01-12 15:09

Monday, January 12, 2026, 10:47 AM PST

**Current (as of early 2025):**

* **W** (or "big W", "massive W") — short for "win"
* **Ate** (or "ate that up", "ate and left no crumbs") — performed exceptionally
* **Cooked** — delivered/performed well
* **Valid** — legitimate, worthy of respect
* **Real** (or "real one") — authentic, deserving recognition
* **Based** — respect for holding unpopular/controversial stance
* **Locked in** — extreme dedication/focus (overlaps with respect)
* **Goated** — from "GOAT" (Greatest Of All Time)
* **Slay/Slayed** — killed it, especially creative/performative contexts
* **Fire/🔥** — excellent (emoji often standalone)
* **King/Queen** — showing respect/status
* **Icon/Iconic** — legendary status

**Platform-specific intensifiers:** "No cap" (truthful validation), "Fr fr" (for real, for real).
