Was George Orwell AKA Eric Arthur Blair a member of the Fabian society?

### Claude · 2025-08-04 03:41

No, George Orwell (Eric Arthur Blair) was never a member of the Fabian Society. Orwell was never a member of the Fabian Society (led by Sidney and Beatrice Webb with adherents like George Bernard Shaw) which he said was comprised of blow-hard intellectuals pretending to be Socialists when deep down they were Communists lusting for power and control.

This is also confirmed by discussions on Wikipedia's Talk page for the Fabian Society, where editors noted that "the inclusion of Eric Blair (aka George Orwell) as a Fabian is incorrect" and that "he was explicitly critical Fabian and reformist Socialists."

So not only was Orwell not a member, he was actually quite critical of the Fabian Society and their approach to socialism. This aligns with his general skepticism of intellectual socialist movements that he felt were disconnected from working-class realities.
