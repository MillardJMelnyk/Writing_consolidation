Egocentrism is not a cause—it's a symptom.

Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi’s work on “flow” proves that when we’re in our most authentic, native psychological mode, “ego” simply dissolves. (\*Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience\*, <https://www.amazon.com/Flow-Psychology-Experience-Perennial-Classics/dp/0061339202>)

// Typically, awareness of self is a burden. In flow we are too involved in what we are doing to care about protecting the ego. Yet after an episode of flow is over, we generally emerge from it with a stronger self-concept; we know that we have succeeded in meeting a difficult challenge. We might even feel that we have stepped out of the boundaries of the ego and have become part, at least temporarily, of a larger entity. The musician feels at one with the harmony of the cosmos, the athlete moves at one with the team, the reader of a novel lives for a few hours in a different reality. Paradoxically, the self expands through acts of self- forgetfulness.

// -- (<https://www.amazon.com/Flow-Psychology-Experience-Perennial-Classics/dp/0061339202>)

Ego is an artificial construct we create for purposes of self-defense, including hiding from danger. If we were safe to be perfectly honest, transparent, and authentic, we never would have constructed one. This is obvious, because in situations where we feel completely safe, intimately seen, and eminently valued, ego falls away spontaneously.

The problem is that so few people experience that kind of safety and connection so little of the time.

So, the root cause of ego is traumatic abuse/injury. Ego is an artifact of PTSD. Cure the PTSD and ego vanishes.

The problem with that is two-fold:

1.      Traumatic abuse/injury is denied in all but its most egregious forms.

2.      Virtually no one believes that we can eradicate traumatic abuse/injury.

In other words, no one believes that we can eradicate evil. They believe in the power and durability of evil far, far more than they do of good.

There is a word that encapsulates the mental mode required for ego to consist: *codependence*. A close synonym of “codependence” is “dehumanized”.

So, the opposite of codependence is *humanness*.

Codependence is the completely irrational, hysterical, paranoid delusion in which we're convinced that unless someone else does for us when it comes to what we perceive as existential threats (which perception is, itself, as near to 100% paranoid delusion as you can get,) we will die and the world will end.

There are very down-to-earth, sensible, and effective steps that any and every one of us can take to escape from codependence—and therapy isn’t one of them—let alone from living a lifetime so characterized by the psychotic mode (yeah, completely psychotic, broken from reality) that we come to identify ourselves (internally—far be it from us to admit it!) as codependents.
