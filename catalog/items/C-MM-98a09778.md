### Claude · 2026-05-29 22:39

Context determines value, not the thing itself.

The hot dog isn't better food. The game makes it better — the crowd, the moment, the belonging. Strip the context, the hot dog is cheap meat in a bun. Strip the context from the Ritz, and it's just expensive food eaten formally.

The "riddle" is that we think we're evaluating the object when we're actually evaluating the experience surrounding it. Which means most of what we call taste, preference, or quality is situational, not intrinsic.
