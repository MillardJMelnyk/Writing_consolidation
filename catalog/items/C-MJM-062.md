### Millard · 2025-07-03 15:59

in opposing evil, you can err on the side of caution for the sake of the evildoers. What's the converse?

### Claude · 2025-07-03 15:59

In opposing evil, you can err on the side of aggression for the sake of the victims.

This captures the moral tension between two forms of potential error: being too cautious (and thus allowing harm to continue) versus being too aggressive (and potentially causing collateral damage or becoming what you're fighting against). The converse suggests prioritizing the protection of those being harmed, even at the risk of overreach.

This dilemma appears throughout ethics, law, and politics - from questions about when intervention is justified, to how much force is appropriate in stopping wrongdoing, to balancing civil liberties against security measures. Both sides reflect genuine moral concerns: the dignity and rights of all people (including wrongdoers) versus the urgent need to protect the vulnerable from harm.
