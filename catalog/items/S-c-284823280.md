Except "stole the land" is the language of the psychotics that did it.

Their crime is only secondarily about the land -- and this is what's so weird about protesting symptoms without even being aware of the root illness, let alone touching on it.

Supremacism -- the dehumanization of others in order to elevate oneself -- is closer to the root. You've got to become a supremacist and dehumanize the people you steal from before you're even in a mental position to steal anything.

But even supremacism is a characteristic of the root illness, not the illness itself.

The illness is codependence. All supremacists are codependents. That mean they constitutionally have dehumanized themselves.

Everyone that embraces, accepts, accommodates, tolerates supremacism -- whether as though "over" their "lessers" or as "under" their "superiors" -- is a codependent.

Cure codependence and you will have solved every problem humankind has ever had.
