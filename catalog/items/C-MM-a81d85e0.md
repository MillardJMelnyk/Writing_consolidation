how do i eliminate Open link in Split View and Open link in New Container Tab from Firefox's context menu?

### Claude · 2026-09-12 01:17

In about:config, set `browser.tabs.splitView.enabled` to false and `privacy.userContext.enabled` to false, then restart Firefox.
