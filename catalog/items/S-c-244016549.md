Dude, I'm sure it's not lost on you whose ideas these resemble. I'm not intimating the bastardization done to them by the so-called "Christian church". Jesus and Paul weren't peddling theories. This is the lived experience of everyone, I have no doubt, who puts their love and lot into the truth, into living uncompromisingly honest. It's really got shitall to do with what most people think "religion" and "spirituality" are. Or "morality" for that matter. I'm enjoying your posts. You're inspiring me to write shorter and more often.
