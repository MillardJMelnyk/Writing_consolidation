### Millard · 2025-12-08 16:08

I need a clear, punchy name for a chronic occurrence.
1. Person A expresses a conclusion about event X, having witnessed the event.
2. Person B responds with a "but maybe".
B clearly doesn't understand that responding to statements about an actuality with a hypothetical is a category error.

### Claude · 2025-12-08 16:08

**Hypothetical Displacement** - B substitutes speculation for A's observed reality.

Alternative: **Actuality Evasion** - emphasizes B's avoidance of the witnessed event.

### Millard · 2025-12-08 16:10

Another key factor:  "but maybe not" is equally as probable as "but maybe" without additional facts that demonstrate one or the other, and the fact is that the facts supporting what was said have already been put on the table.

### Claude · 2025-12-08 16:10

**Equiprobable Deflection** - B counters presented evidence with symmetrically worthless speculation.

Alternative: **Null Hypothesis Evasion** - B evades A's evidenced conclusion with epistemically void alternatives that cancel to zero.
