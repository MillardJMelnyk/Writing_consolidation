### Claude · 2026-08-24 21:25

No native gift-wrap/gift-receipt/gift-messaging feature (unlike Amazon's checkout-integrated gift options). What exists:

* **Gift cards**: MercadoLibre sells its own prepaid gift cards (varying denominations) plus third-party digital gift cards (Robux, Binance/crypto, etc.) as regular catalog products.
* No checkout-level "this is a gift" wrapping, gift receipt, or gift message option comparable to Amazon's.

Gift cards are sold as products, not offered as an add-on service.
