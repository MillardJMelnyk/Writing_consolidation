This is called "codependence".

Every codependent relationship is a micro-cult.

No codependence? Cults would be impossible.

You're not actually living as long as you're stuck in codependence. The way you know it's codependence is that you feel stuck.

Living real life does not feel like being stuck.

You have no obligation to another human being, nor to all other human beings. Only codependents obligate each other.

Most of you probably can't even imagine what that would be like.

There is a shitload of good things we can do for each other. Not one of them is obligatory. Obligation is a lie foisted on you -- not by people who care about you, but by people that want to use and abuse and take advantage of you.

It's certainly got nothing at all to do with love.
