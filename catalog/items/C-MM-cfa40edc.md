### Claude · 2026-08-31 23:19

One-hour C2 presentation lesson, student presents on their own work (assuming that, same as before):

**0–5 — Warm-up**  
Ask what they'd present to a room of strangers about their job. Force a one-sentence pitch. Push for precision, not fluency.

**5–15 — Input: signposting and stance**  
Give ~12 C2-level phrases and drill them cold:  
- Framing: *What I want to do today is…* / *I'll come at this from three angles.*  
- Transitions: *That brings me to…* / *Building on that…*  
- Hedging vs. asserting: *The evidence points fairly strongly to…* vs. *I'd argue…*  
- Handling pushback: *That's a fair challenge — let me reframe it.*  
- Closing: *If you take one thing away…*

**15–25 — Structure drill**  
Student outlines a 4-minute talk on paper: hook, thesis, three supports, close. No sentences, only headings. You check the logic, not the English.

**25–40 — Delivery round 1**  
They present. You do not interrupt. Note: filler words, pace, eye contact, sentence length, any L1 stress patterns.

**40–50 — Feedback + Q&A pressure**  
Give three fixes maximum, ranked. Then hit them with four hostile questions — the kind a real audience asks. Grade the answers on stance and hedging, not content.

**50–60 — Delivery round 2**  
Same talk, incorporating fixes. Compare. End with one specific target for next session.

Homework: record the talk on their phone, send it before next class.

### Claude · 2026-08-31 23:46

Two built-in options, no install:

**Xbox Game Bar** — press this key plus G:

Click **Capture** → record button. Records the active app window plus mic and system audio. Won't record File Explorer or the desktop itself. Files land in Videos\Captures.

**Snipping Tool** (Windows 11) — open it, switch to the video camera icon, drag a region, hit Start. Toggles for mic and system audio are in the toolbar. Records any region including the desktop.

Use Snipping Tool if the student needs the whole screen; Game Bar if it's a single app.
