I hear ya, but this applies only to people still listening to these professional liars and hoping some sanity will emit from their pie holes.

"It willingly labors for their happiness..."

Well, de Tocqueville had an unrealistically rosy view of American democracy, didn't he? It has never done that, although just the little word "own" could correct his statement and expose its naivete: it willingly labors for their \*own\* happiness -- "them" being those qualifying for inclusion as "people", which in his time was radically different than what people today take the word to mean.

"It would be like the authority of a parent if, like that authority, its object was to prepare men for manhood; but it seeks, on the contrary, to keep them in perpetual childhood."

"Perpetual childhood" = codependence.
