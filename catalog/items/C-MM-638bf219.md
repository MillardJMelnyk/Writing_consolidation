### Millard · 2026-10-27 19:23

What were the death tolls on both sides for both the American revolution and the American civil War

### Claude · 2026-10-27 19:24

Tuesday, October 27, 2026, 1:23 PM CST

Revolution: about 25,300 American deaths in service per Peckham's *Toll of Independence* (some estimates run as high as 70,000), and roughly 24,000 British dead from all causes, a figure the American Battlefield Trust calls unreliable; one tally instead puts it at 43,633 British plus 7,774 German auxiliaries. Civil War: about 360,000 Union and 260,000 Confederate dead (the traditional Fox/Livermore count), with demographer J. David Hacker's 2011 revision raising the combined total to around 750,000.

What's the death toll in the Ukraine war

### Claude · 2026-10-27 19:25

Military: CSIS, a US think tank, estimated as of mid-2026 up to 450,000 Russian deaths and roughly 125,000–150,000 Ukrainian military fatalities, against Zelenskyy's own February 2026 claim of 55,000 Ukrainian soldiers killed and a BBC/Mediazona name-by-name count of over 200,186 Russian deaths by February 2026. Civilians: the UN has verified 17,605 killed as of the end of September 2026, and says the real number is likely higher.

### Claude · 2026-10-27 19:26

Fair hit — Zelensky's 55,000 is clearly low, since the volunteer UALosses project had documented 96,821 Ukrainian fighters killed by name as of late June 2026, alongside 97,938 missing, for nearly 195,000 dead or missing in all, and the Wikipedia casualty table cites a BBC News estimate of 200,000 Ukrainian forces killed through February 2026. Likely the real military death toll is 150,000–200,000+, with the missing being the swing factor; even Meduza notes the CSIS 100,000–140,000 range is only plausible if about half of Ukraine's missing are still alive, and all of the dead have been identified.
