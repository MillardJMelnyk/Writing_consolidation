### Claude · 2026-05-18 17:15

"Bien" works but feels a bit flat. More natural options depending on tone:

* **"¡Órale!"** — the most Mexican response; acknowledges and says "got it, cool"
* **"¡Andale!"** — similar energy, "alright then, let's go"
* **"Perfecto, gracias"** — polite and warm if it's a delivery or someone coming to help you
* **"¡Qué bueno!"** — "great news!" if you're genuinely relieved they're on their way

If it's casual (friends coming over), **"¡Órale!"** is the most natural Queretano reflex.
