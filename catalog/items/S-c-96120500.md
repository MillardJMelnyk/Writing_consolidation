I don't agree with this, just like I don't agree with "consent" in the area of sex/rape. Both cases are excellent examples of how our codependence has been normalized.

Consent *necessarily* implies the pre-existing will on someone's part to do what we, **in response**, either consent to allow or refuse to allow.

Why the fuck would we approach either government or sex/rape this way? Why the fuck would we just gloss over the questions of what motivates the will we've been accosted by, whether it's OK for them to be so motivated, whether that will intends on good or evil, and whether it should have **been allowed to form at all** (as opposed to **be allowed to act**) -- **as if there were no questions at all about it**?

Well, the answer to all of that is: our *codependence*.

This is why I insist that "consent" is a Trojan Horse. The real test should be **permission**.

**WE NEED TO ASK FOR PERMISSION** ***BEFORE*** **WE FORM THE WILL TO MAKE DECISIONS AND TAKE ACTIONS THAT DIRECTLY AFFECT OTHERS**.

THAT is what true lovers do. THAT is what true mentors, guides, and experts do.

"Consent" is pigshit.
