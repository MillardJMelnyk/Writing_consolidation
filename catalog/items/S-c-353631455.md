It's pretty wild that this even needs to be stated.

But when the zone has been flooded with so much shit that it would even make Steve Bannon saucer-eyed, and when so many people so easily fall for it, I'm glad I'm not the only one calling it out.

I've listened openly and honestly, intelligently and critically, to every argument to the contrary, looking to find any shred of truth or insight I could find, and every single one of them hasn't just been misleading or incorrect or false, they've been downright *shit—not even intended to be accurate or correct or true.*

They fall into two categories, as all forms of propaganda always do:

1. Utter bullshit in the Franfurtian sense (see <https://reloveution.substack.com/i/170534838/bullshit>

   ). In other words, statements made solely for effect which, in fact, were never intended to reflect what's real and true, but only to achieve a desired influence on the reader.
2. Flooding the zone with shit, so that people have no fucking clue what's up or down, rendering them suggestible to anyone who puts themself forward confidently as knowing what is actually up or down. (<https://www.vox.com/policy-and-politics/2020/1/16/20991816/impeachment-trial-trump-bannon-misinformation>)

Both are techniques for *cultifying* people.

At this point I'm pretty sure, literally, that I've heard it all.

If someone thinks they have a legit argument that Zionism is not a racist ideology, I'd love to hear it. I doubt very much that someone can come up with one, though.
