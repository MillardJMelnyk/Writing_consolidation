### Claude · 2025-11-25 14:27

**Direct answer: ~98%**

**Calculation:**

* Roman Catholic Church existence: ~1992 years (33 CE to 2025)
* Period explicitly reflecting women/children as male property: ~1950 years (33 CE to ~1983)

**Paradigmatic shift markers:**

* **1983 Code of Canon Law**: Removed explicit legal dependency language treating wives as under husband's authority
* **1979-1984 Theology of the Body**: First systematic rejection of "property" framework in favor of "complementarian" language
* **Pre-1960s**: Canon law explicitly codified patria potestas concepts, wives required husband's permission for legal/economic acts, children under absolute paternal authority

**Why 1983 as inflection point:**
Prior canon law (1917 Code) treated women as legal dependents requiring male permission. The 1983 revision removed this explicit framework. Theological documents from late 1970s-1980s explicitly rejected property language while maintaining hierarchy.

**Note:** "Complementarian" theology maintaining male headship/female exclusion from priesthood continues, but this differs from explicit property status. If you require complete structural equality for the paradigm to have shifted, the answer is **100%** (ongoing).
