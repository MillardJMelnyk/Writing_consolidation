COMPOSITE HEALTHY POPULATION RANKING
------------------------------------

**Methodology:**

* Five factors, equally weighted (20% each)
* Each factor normalized to 0-100 scale
* Higher composite score = healthier population
* Direction assumptions:
  + **Obesity rate:** Lower = healthier (inverted)
  + **Pharmaceutical spending per capita:** Higher = less healthy (inverted) — interpreted as over-medicalization/dependency, not access quality
  + **Illicit drug use rate:** Lower = healthier (inverted)
  + **Mental health (depression rate):** Lower = healthier (inverted)
  + **Health system performance:** Bloomberg/CEOWORLD index score used directly (higher = healthier)

**Data sources:**

* Obesity: WHO 2022
* Illicit drugs: IHME 2023 (drug use rate per 100k)
* Pharmaceutical spending: OECD 2021-2023
* Depression: Global Health Data Exchange / CEOWORLD 2024
* Health system: Bloomberg Global Health Index 2024

**Critical limitation:** Only ~40 countries have complete data across all five factors (primarily OECD nations). Rankings below exclude countries with incomplete data.
