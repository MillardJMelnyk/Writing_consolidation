### Millard · 2026-08-27 23:11

I amtrying to sign up for internet at telmex for our new house, why are they asking me if it is for entertainment or work?

### Claude · 2026-08-27 23:11

To route you to the right package tier — "laboral" pushes you toward a business plan (higher price, business SLA, sometimes static IP), "entretenimiento" keeps you on the cheaper residential plan. Say entretenimiento/hogar unless you actually need a business contract with invoicing.

### Millard · 2026-08-27 23:12

I work from home but want a cheap plan

### Claude · 2026-08-27 23:12

Say "entretenimiento" / uso en casa. Working from home on a residential plan is normal and allowed — "laboral" to Telmex means a business account (Telmex Infinitum Negocio), which costs more and is meant for offices with billing under an RFC.
