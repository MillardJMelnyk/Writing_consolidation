Veils.

Moses covered his face with a veil so that people couldn't see the glory was fading.

The Temple Veil hid the Holy of Holies.

The instant Jesus died, so the Gospels say, the Temple Veil was torn in two from top to bottom.

What is the reality these metaphors refer to?

Concealment.

The time for concealment ended and died 2000 years ago.

And yet, concealment -- behind veils, behind walls, behind doors, behind lies and distortion and bullshit -- still rules the day, probably never as much as it does now.

Why?

Psychosis. Literal, clinical, diagnosable breaks from reality, from the truth that the need for concealment has already been proven as a hallucination, a delusion.

We're living in a reality in which the safest, best, most beneficial route is transparency, divulging, disclosure, exposure: **lights set on a hill which refuse being hidden, period**.

The degree to which this seems foreign or wrong or dangerous to you marks the severity of your psychosis.

The veil was rent.
