Try it out. Whenever you ask yourself, “Is this a cult?” or whenever you muse, “This seems cultish,” then look to see if the proponents trying to achieve dominance do it by overriding personal experience and judgment with narrative.

Actually, the fact alone that they’re seeking dominance is a dead giveaway.

If that’s what they’re doing—overriding an individual’s experience and judgment with some “authoritative” narrative—then it’s a cult.

It doesn’t matter how big the group is. Most cults have only 2 members: the cult leader and the cult follower, often called “relationships”. But they’re not.
