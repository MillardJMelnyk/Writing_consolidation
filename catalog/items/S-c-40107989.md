Machiavelli asked whether it’s better to be loved or feared.

Of course he did.

He thought that the ideal was to be both loved *and* feared. But, of course, that unholy wedlock seems possible only in the deranged and psychopathic. It’s impossible in reality.

Fear is the devil’s tool, and only his servants use it as a “motivator”.

Extortion is the devil’s business.

No government, ever, was more or better than an organized extortion racket. Any government that does not extort its people will not be recognized as government at all by those who can’t tell racketeering from “politics” — which means, virtually everyone.
