### Claude · 2025-12-29 16:41

**Official Mexico INM Documentation:**

Two Pathways for Permanent Residency via Family Unity
-----------------------------------------------------

### 1. VISA APPLICATION (for applicants outside Mexico)

**Source:** [INM Official PDF - Visa by Family Unit](https://www.inm.gob.mx/static/Tramites/en/VISA_BY_FAMILY_UNIT.pdf)

**Eligible Applicants:**
Mexican citizens or foreign persons with temporary/permanent resident status can request visas for:

* Parents
* Minor unmarried children under their legal representation (or adult children with incapacity)
* Spouse or cohabitant (5+ years documented cohabitation, or children in common)
* Minor unmarried siblings under their legal representation (Mexicans/permanent residents only)
* Stepchildren (minor unmarried or with incapacity under legal representation)

**Requirements:**

1. Original + copy of sponsor's valid official ID (Mexican) or residency card (foreign)
2. Copy of applicant's valid passport/travel document
3. No pending visa application for same applicant
4. Proof of family relationship via:
   * Birth certificates (original + copy) for parent/child relationships
   * Marriage certificate (original + copy) for spouses
   * Cohabitation document from competent authority showing minimum 5 years continuous cohabitation, OR proof of children in common
   * Prior marriage dissolution documents if previously married
5. For refugee cases: refugee recognition documentation

**Timeline:**

* Authorization valid 30 business days from notification
* Visa valid 6 months, single entry
* Upon arrival: must exchange FMM for residency card within 30 calendar days at INM

---

### 2. CHANGE OF STATUS (for those already in Mexico with visitor/temporary resident status)

**Source:** [INM Official PDF - Change to Permanent Resident by Family Relationship](https://www.inm.gob.mx/static/Tramites/en/CHANGE_OF_CONDITION_PERMANENT_RESIDENT_BY_FAMILY_RELATIONSHIP.pdf)

**Eligible Situations (visitor or temporary residents):**

* Parent of Mexican or permanent resident
* Child of permanent resident (minor unmarried or under guardianship)
* Stepchild of Mexican/permanent resident (minor unmarried or under guardianship)
* Child/teenager under guardianship of Mexican/permanent resident
* Child of Mexican born abroad (per Article 30 of Constitution)
* Sibling of Mexican/permanent resident (minor or under legal representation)
* **Spouse/cohabitant of Mexican or permanent resident with 2 years regular stay as temporary resident AND relationship continues**
* Having Mexican children by birth
* Parent, grandparent, child, or grandchild of Mexican by birth

**Requirements:**

1. Copy of passport/travel document or FMM (original FMM if submitting)
2. Current temporary resident card or visitor card or valid FMM
3. Payment of fees (see below)
4. Original + copy of sponsor's valid ID (Mexican) or residency card (foreign)
5. Proof of relationship per applicable category:
   * Birth certificates (original + copy)
   * Marriage certificate (original + copy)
   * For spouse/cohabitant: marriage certificate or cohabitation document PLUS sworn statement signed by both parties stating marital residence and continuity of relationship for 2 years
   * Guardianship documents from competent authority
   * Naturalization letters where applicable
6. Application form filled electronically, printed, with signature
7. Must appear personally to provide photo, signature, fingerprints

**Fees (as of document date):**

* First payment: $1,780.00 MXN (application processing + change authorization)
* Second payment (if approved): $6,789.00 MXN (DRP card issuance)

**Validity:**

* Indefinite (except minors: 1 year if under 3; 4 years if 3-17)

**Additional Requirements:**

* Foreign documents (except passport) must be apostilled/legalized
* Translations to Spanish if documents in other language
* Must notify INM within 90 days of any changes to: civil status, nationality, address, workplace

---

**Official Application Portal:** https://www.inm.gob.mx/tramites/publico/solicitud\_estancia.html

**Appointment System:** https://www.inm.gob.mx/spublic/portal/inmex.html

### Claude · 2025-12-29 16:58

I don't see an explicit requirement for apostille on your son's birth certificate in the Article 38 document.

The apostille requirement appears in the simplified version (cambio\_condicion-residente\_permanente\_unidad\_familiar.pdf):

"Los documentos públicos extranjeros deberán estar apostillados o legalizados y acompañados de la traducción al español"

But this states "foreign public documents" generally, without specifying whether it applies to the sponsor's documents or only the applicant's documents.

The Article 38 document only states for requirement 5a:
"deberá presentar original y copia del acta de nacimiento del mexicano o del residente permanente"

No apostille mentioned.

If your son was born in Mexico, his birth certificate is Mexican, not foreign, so no apostille would be required. If he was born outside Mexico, the general foreign document apostille rule might apply, but it's not explicitly stated in the Article 38 procedure.
