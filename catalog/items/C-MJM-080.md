### Claude · 2025-07-15 20:16

This appears to be Deadvlei (also spelled Dead Vlei) in Namibia's Namib-Naukluft National Park, within the Namib Desert. The image shows the characteristic features of this famous location:

* Ancient dead camel thorn trees (Vachellia erioloba) that died around 600-700 years ago when the climate changed and the area dried up
* A white clay pan (the flat, cracked ground surface)
* Towering red sand dunes in the background, which are some of the world's highest at around 300-400 meters
* The distinctive reddish-orange coloration of the dunes caused by iron oxide

Deadvlei is one of the most photographed locations in Namibia and is renowned for creating these surreal, almost otherworldly landscapes. The contrast between the stark white clay pan, the black skeletal trees, and the vibrant red dunes creates a striking visual composition that has made it a popular destination for photographers and tourists from around the world.

The area is located near Sossusvlei, approximately 60 kilometers from the entrance gate to the Namib-Naukluft National Park.
