> I just can't understand why everybody glibly accepts this story about Jews being indigenous to the Levant.
> 
> If you go by the story in the Torah, the people who descended from Israel and his sons were actually indigenous to Egypt. They invaded, attacked, committed genocide, and hijacked the so-called promised Land, claiming to own it after decimating and displacing the people who lived there.
> 
> By what stretch of imagination or reason did that make them indigenous?
> 
> They were invaders, pirates, and genocidists without any right to the land. None at all. The only semblance of the "right" they claimed was self-bestowed according to their own story of their genocidal God that promised them they could steal it from the current residents.
> 
> How does that give the large percentage European people now in Palestine any right to the land at all? They did it once before so that gives them a right to do it again?
> 
> It doesn't make a shred of sense, and yet everybody swallows it. I don't know how they avoid choking.
