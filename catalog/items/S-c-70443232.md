Why? Because our upbringing, education, religious training, socialization, and the ongoing mind-fuck of news media and authoritative, expert opinion have so monumentally alienated us from ourselves, we don't so much as recognize our true selves when we meet us -- let alone relate to ourselves, understand ourselves, or appreciate ourselves. We feel so foreign to ourselves, and we're so sure that this self-defensive facade we call "ego" that seems so familiar is "me", we can't put the two “me-s” together. But I know that the ego is the false "me". The only self that's left to be me, then, is this genius who seems like some other guy completely.

Why does that thought make us so uncomfortable?
