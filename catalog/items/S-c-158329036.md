The shitshow that has been made out of the Charlie Kirk murder is, straight up, an orgy of idolatry.

Instead of responding to his murder humanly, honestly, with love and empathy (or compassion, as he would have us say,) you have made a mockery of the man and his work by co-opting them to promote your own egotistic views.

Shame on all of you.

I haven't seen genuine, heartfelt grief or mourning in any of the shit that's been spewed online.

No wonder, because the principal trait of genuine grief and mourning is silence.

You all sound like a pack of wild dogs barking.

People have asked me where my sympathy, empathy, compassion are because I've expressed no grief or mourning for Charlie.

That's because the environment that's developed has nothing to do with grief or mourning or the human being himself.

It's all been politicized and it's being weaponized, and you're all falling for it.

Grief and mourning are among the most intimately personal, vulnerable, and sensitive experiences we ever go through.

Fuck if I'm going to bring mine out in the company of barking dogs.
