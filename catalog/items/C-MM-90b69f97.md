They're black boxes with a green light, mounted on walls, usually next to doors and often in the room the guard came from. While holding a counting-down phone, every unused one gets a white outline through walls; touch the phone to one with a green light (red means already used this heist) and hold it until it beeps. ### Claude · 2026-10-09 04:26

Outside guards usually have their scanner just inside the nearest locked door, so lockpick that door before you take him down or you won't reach it in the 15-second countdown (e.g. Jewelry Store alley guard: pick the locked door first; he faces away from it on the far end of his patrol for about 22 seconds). Some outdoor guards have one in their own post, like the Mansion booth guard whose phone checks in at the spot in the booth, and an EMP grenade temporarily disables the phone if there's no scanner reachable.
