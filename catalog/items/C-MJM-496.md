**Yeshayahu Leibowitz** (1903–1994).

He was an Israeli Orthodox Jewish philosopher, professor at Hebrew University, and editor of *Encyclopaedia Hebraica*. He coined the term "Judeo-Nazi" and warned that Israeli conduct in the occupied territories reflected a "Nazi mentality." The timing in the quote ("50 years ago") aligns with his statements beginning in the 1970s following the 1967 occupation.
