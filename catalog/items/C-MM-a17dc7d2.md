i received a call on my mexican number from the states but cant hear anything, do you know why that might be?

### Claude · 2026-09-22 15:51

Most likely an autodialer or spam call. These systems ring many numbers at once and leave you on dead air if no agent picks up, or they call just to confirm your number is active. Likely.

To check, look up the number the call came from online. If it shows up as spam or a telemarketer, that settles it. If it's someone you know, have them call back. If you still get silence, the cause is poor international call routing on their carrier or yours.

### Claude · 2026-09-22 15:51

Then the most likely cause is how the call was routed. PayPal's outbound calls go through automated VoIP systems, and when those reach a Mexican mobile, the audio often gets lost in transit, so the line connects but you hear nothing. Likely.

To find out what they wanted, open the PayPal app or website yourself and check your notifications and account activity, or call PayPal using the number listed in the app. Don't call back the number that rang you.

How did you confirm the call was from PayPal? Scammers commonly fake PayPal's caller ID, which is how numbers like this end up marked as spam.

### Claude · 2026-09-22 15:52

That points to your end, not theirs. When two separate US companies both give you dead air, the most likely cause is your Mexican carrier or your phone's settings mishandling incoming international calls. Likely.

To confirm, have someone in the US call you from a regular US mobile. If that's silent too, it's your line. Then turn Wi-Fi calling off (or on, if it's already off) and repeat the test. If the result changes, that setting is the cause. If nothing changes, call your carrier and tell them incoming international calls connect with no audio.

### Millard · 2026-09-22 15:52

could they make it so i dont hear the audio if i dont have saldo?

### Claude · 2026-09-22 15:52

Unlikely. In Mexico the caller pays, so receiving calls, including international ones, is free even with zero saldo. And if your balance were blocking the call, it normally wouldn't ring at all rather than connecting with no sound.

The one exception is a prepaid line that has sat at zero long enough for the carrier to restrict it. To rule that out, add the minimum recharge and have the same kind of call come in again.
