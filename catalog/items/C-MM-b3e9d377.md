### Claude · 2026-08-09 13:28

The CDC page now says: "'Vaccines do not cause autism' is not an evidence-based claim because studies have not ruled out the possibility that infant vaccines cause autism." It also states studies supporting a link "have been ignored by health authorities," and that HHS has launched a "comprehensive assessment" of autism's causes.

The old header "Vaccines do not cause autism" is still on the page but now carries an asterisk noting it wasn't removed because the evidence supports it — only because of a prior agreement. Previously the page said no link had been found between vaccines and autism.
