If you want to control, manipulate, or deprive access to information and the ability to share it, liars included, I don’t care how you’ve dressed and made yourself up to look:

*You are seriously, psychopathologically sick in the head and heart.*

Such people have a deep, abiding mental illness.

It’s got nothing to do with good or bad, virtue or vice.

It’s got everything to do with the level of stupidity of someone who chops off their foot because their sole itches, then chops off their calf because their ankle is killing them.

It’s a special kind of stupid.
