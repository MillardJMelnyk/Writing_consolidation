As a matter of fact, “We are all obedient to someone,” is flatly false.

Spoken like a thorough codependent, though.

All *codependents* are not only obedient to someone, they ***demand*** to have “leaders” to obey—thought leaders, political leaders, spiritual leaders, movement leaders, on and on.

And as is typical for all codependents, they think everyone else is just like them.

Nope.

> **Post:** [David Morens and the Problem of Obedience](https://substack.brownstone.org/p/david-morens-and-the-problem-of-obedience) — Brownstone Insights · Brownstone Institute  
> We are all obedient to someone. When in the service of evil, obedience destroys freedom. When in the service of good, obedience perfects freedom.
