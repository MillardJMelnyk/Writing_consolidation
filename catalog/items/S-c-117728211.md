The real question here isn’t whether he’s right or wrong.

The real question here is: Are *you* a cultist?

* Do you prefer ignoramusing yourself to facing contradictory information?
* Do you, therefore, refuse to look and honestly consider information that does not already obviously align with your “beliefs”?
* Do you mock what you have not spent a single second or ounce of energy trying to understand?
* Do you automatically suspect new sources just because your trusted authorities haven’t pre-approved them?

If so, you are a cultist—so what relevance does the truth or merit of what this guy says have to anything that matters?

You yourself are your biggest problem.

Fix yourself before you pretend that you can fix anyone else.
