**Global Military Spending in 2024:**
World military expenditure rose to $2718 billion in 2024 according to the Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI), which is the authoritative source for military spending data. This represents an increase of 9.4% from 2023 and the steepest year-on-year rise since at least the end of the Cold War.

**Cost to Address World Hunger:**
The most recent UN data shows that The World Food Programme (WFP) on Friday called for some $16.9 billion to address the escalating global hunger crisis in 2025. However, this is for WFP's operational needs, not necessarily the total cost to eliminate hunger.

An Oxfam analysis from 2024 found that funds equivalent to just 2.9 percent ($35.7 billion) of their combined annual military spending, Group of Seven (G7) countries could help end world hunger and solve the debt crisis in the Global South.

**Verification of the Meme:**
The meme appears to be based on older UN estimates. In 2008, the United Nations said that $30 billion per year could end hunger on earth, but this figure hasn't been updated by the FAO since then.

**The Math:**
With global military spending at $2.718 trillion in 2024, that equals approximately $7.4 billion per day. So $30 billion would indeed be spent by the world's militaries in about 4 days, making the "every 3 days" claim in the meme reasonably close to accurate using the 2008 UN estimate.

However, the $30 billion figure is from 2008 and likely outdated. More recent estimates suggest higher amounts would be needed, though still representing a small fraction of global military spending.
