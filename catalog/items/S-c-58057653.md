For a long time I've had an idea that we have a kind of spiritual sanctuary within each one of us which is our holiest of holies. In our culture, we routinely violate and trespass into each other’s sanctuaries, and this kind of transgression\_this *sin*—is superglued to our perverted concepts of power.

I was thinking just now about being judged, and I realized that our typical response to being judged is to defend ourselves against the judgment, which means we're meeting the judge on the playfield of their definition and choosing, and we’re ascribing validity both to their judgment and to their qualifications to act as judge.

Instead, we need to remove ourselves from that predicament, which is under their control, not remain stuck in it trying to defend against them and prove them wrong.

What's more, when we remain in their bailiwick struggling to overcome their transgression, we almost invariably respond in kind by violating them in the same way that they are violating us in judging us. We do this by making the same kinds of unfounded and overreaching assertions about them and their judgment that are just as factually baseless and epistemologically unfounded and indefensible as is their judgment of us.

Instead, the first thing we need to do is get them to state their qualifications for passing judgment on us. What are their facts, what is their familiarity with the events they're judging, and how much experience have they had in similar situations?

If they don't have good answers to those questions, they are in no way qualified to judge us.

If they have no answers at all, good or bad, for those questions, then they are only *pretending* to judge us. There’s no legitimate question about the validity and value of their judgment when it is, in fact, a lie. It's not real judgment.

This is what most people are doing most of the time when they judge us. They’re not pronouncing an intelligent conclusion drawn from intelligent evaluation of anything relevant or real.

They’re just fucking lying.

Undoubtedly, they’re applying rules or "truths" or laws—which they subscribe to in exactly the same way as do cultists to their dogmas—that to them (superficially) seem relevant and which they want to *impose* on us.

That's not judgment, not in an evaluative sense, but rather naked, crass imposition of priorities and values that they're trying to force onto us or even into us.

The attempt to do this and the act of doing it, among other things, is called "defilement" and "desecration". Such people have no business intruding at all, (if anyone ever does,) and to hell with what they have to say.

Being such, their actions and what they say and all the arguments surrounding its correctness and rightness are absolutely irrelevant -- other than as proof of violation.
