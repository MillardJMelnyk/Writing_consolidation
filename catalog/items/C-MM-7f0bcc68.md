### Millard · 2026-09-28 17:41

Why isn't adversariality a word

### Claude · 2026-09-28 17:41

It is a word, just a rare one (Wiktionary lists it and it shows up in academic writing), but it never caught on in mainstream dictionaries because **adversarialism**, **adversarialness**, and **antagonism** already covered the ground; that's Likely, based on dictionary coverage I haven't checked live.

### Millard · 2026-09-28 17:42

Those three really don't do the same job as adversariality does

### Claude · 2026-09-28 17:42

Right: adversarialism names a doctrine, adversarialness is a clumsy surface state, and antagonism is hostility, whereas adversariality names the structural condition of being set up as opponents, which none of them captures, so the word earns its place and readers will parse it without trouble.
