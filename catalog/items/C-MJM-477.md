### Claude · 2025-12-17 14:53

**Findings (as of December 17, 2025):**

**Real club exists:** Williamsburg Modern Lit Book Club is a legitimate Meetup group (founded 2010, ~1,220 members). Leadership includes "Katharina Botlo."

**Widespread scam confirmed:** September-November 2025 saw an epidemic of book club scams targeting authors. Pattern matches your email exactly:

* Gmail addresses (all scam reports cite Gmail)
* Impersonates real book clubs and uses names of actual organizers
* Requests fees ($55-$500 range reported, labeled as "spotlight fees," "participation fees," or "cost sharing")
* Calls non-books "books" (your Substack article)
* AI-generated flattering text

**Specific warnings found:**

* "The Williamsburg Book Club" (different club) posted November 2025: "**Attention authors: someone is pretending to be the organizer of this group and contacting authors by email to offer some kind of collaboration. They are trying to steal money from you.**"
* Writer Beware (Victoria Strauss): Documented this exact scam pattern starting September 19, 2025
* Multiple author blogs confirm: Kevin G. Chapman (Oct 3, 2025), Kendall Dunkelberg (Oct 5, 2025), Jason Sanford (Sept 28, 2025), John Scalzi (Nov 22, 2025)

**Red flags in your case:**

1. Gmail address (williamsburgmodernlit@gmail.com)
2. Cost-sharing mention
3. Called Substack article a "book"
4. Unsolicited contact

**Conclusion:** Almost certainly a scam impersonating the real Katharina Botlo/real club. Scammers steal names from legitimate clubs and organizers.
