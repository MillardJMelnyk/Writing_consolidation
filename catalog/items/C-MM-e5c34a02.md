when do we use mostly

### Claude · 2026-10-20 16:05

Use **mostly** as an adverb meaning "mainly" or "in most cases" (*The audience was mostly students*; *I mostly work from home*), but never as a determiner before a noun, where you need **most** (*Most people agree*, not *Mostly people agree*).
