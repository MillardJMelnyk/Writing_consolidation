### Millard · 2025-09-24 19:46

Is "consistent, overweening arrogance" redundant? It just feels like it makes more of an impact than "consistent arrogance".

### Claude · 2025-09-24 19:46

It's not technically redundant, but it's very close to it. "Overweening" specifically means excessive arrogance or presumption - it's arrogance that goes beyond reasonable bounds. So when you say "overweening arrogance," you're essentially saying "excessively excessive arrogance," which does border on redundancy.

However, you're right that it creates more impact. "Overweening arrogance" has a more emphatic, almost literary quality that "consistent arrogance" lacks. The redundancy actually works rhetorically here - it's like saying someone is "brutally savage" or "utterly devastating." The slight redundancy amplifies the effect.

If you want to avoid any hint of redundancy while keeping the intensity, you could try alternatives like:

* "relentless arrogance"
* "insufferable arrogance"
* "breathtaking arrogance"
* Simply "overweening presumption" (since overweening already implies consistency)

But honestly, "consistent, overweening arrogance" works well if you want that punchy, emphatic effect. Sometimes a little redundancy serves the rhetoric better than perfect precision.
