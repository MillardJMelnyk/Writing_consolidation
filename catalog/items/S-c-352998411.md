"My body, my choice" is classic narcissism. The baby has a body. The father has a body. Anyone who has raised their own children know that the father's body is impacted and changes, too, not remotely comparable to the mother's, but by no means negligibly, either.

Women who say, "My body, my choice!" do it for a reason, and it's not a good one. As soon as they factor in the other two bodies into their thinking, their "case" collapses.

And yeah, it's hypocritical for them to apply their narcissism to themselves one way, and the opposite to others.
