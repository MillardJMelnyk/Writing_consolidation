Fuck morality.

If it takes "moral obligation" to get you to do what's human, loving, and good, you're broken.

Fix yourself and you'll have no use for morality.

The fact that you think that you'd go on killing O, raping rampages if you didn't have morality governing you is the most self-damning think I can imagine.

Oh! But wait! That's right! My bad.

YOU **wouldn't** go on killing, raping rampages, would you? You so special -- but you're sure other people THAT YOU DON'T EVEN KNOW certainly would.

I guess your morality didn't teach you about being a hypocrite.

And yeah -- if you believe you need morality, it's EXACTLY because you'd do the shit it forbids and fail to do what it requires. Otherwise, it would be useless.

Which it is -- for people who fixed themselves.

So, which is it: broken or playing a big fucking virtue-signaling game?
