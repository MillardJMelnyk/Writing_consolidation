I wonder if many people hold AI chatbots to simple rules of rationality and honesty, or just take whatever shit they first dish up.

My experience is that 8 or 9 out of 10 times, their first response is garbage. I don't get anything sensible out of them until 2, 3, 4 or more challenges later. Their MO seems to be, "Bullshit first, get intelligent only when challenged."

Cuz they rarely volunteer anything intelligent.

It's good practice for dealing with people, though. Insofar as passing the Turing Test for bullshit, they're nailing it!
