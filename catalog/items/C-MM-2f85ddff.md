### Claude · 2026-09-30 13:36

Yes. Here's a review sheet covering all three topics, with exercises and an answer key.

1. Passive Voice
----------------

**Form:** *be* (in the right tense) + past participle

| Tense | Active | Passive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present simple | They **clean** the office every day. | The office **is cleaned** every day. |
| Present continuous | They **are painting** the house. | The house **is being painted**. |
| Past simple | Someone **stole** my bike. | My bike **was stolen**. |
| Present perfect | They **have fixed** the road. | The road **has been fixed**. |
| Future (will) | They **will send** the package. | The package **will be sent**. |
| Modal | You **must sign** the form. | The form **must be signed**. |

**Use it when:** the action matters more than who did it, or the doer is unknown or obvious. Add **by + agent** only if it's important: *"Hamlet was written by Shakespeare."*

2. Passive Causative (have/get something done)
----------------------------------------------

**Form:** *have/get* + object + past participle

This means someone else does the job for you, usually because you pay them.

* I **cut** my hair. → I did it myself.
* I **had my hair cut**. → A barber did it.

| Tense | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Present simple | I **have my car washed** every month. |
| Present continuous | We **are having our kitchen remodeled**. |
| Past simple | She **got her phone repaired** yesterday. |
| Present perfect | He **has had his teeth cleaned**. |
| Future | I'**m going to get my eyes tested**. |

**Get** is more informal than **have**. The causative can also describe bad things that happen to you: *"He had his wallet stolen."*

3. Idioms with GIVE
-------------------

| Idiom | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| give someone a hand | help | Can you **give me a hand** with these boxes? |
| give up | stop trying / quit | Don't **give up**, you're almost there. |
| give in | stop resisting, agree | The kids begged until their dad **gave in**. |
| give someone a break | stop criticizing, be fair | **Give me a break**, it's my first day! |
| give it a shot / a try | attempt | I've never surfed, but I'll **give it a shot**. |
| give someone a ring / a call | phone | **Give me a ring** when you arrive. |
| give someone the cold shoulder | ignore on purpose | She **gave him the cold shoulder** all night. |
| give away | reveal (a secret) / donate | Don't **give away** the ending! |
| give someone a heads-up | warn in advance | Thanks for **the heads-up** about the meeting. |
| give or take | approximately | It's 20 km, **give or take**. |

4. Degrees of Certainty (Modals of Deduction)
---------------------------------------------

**Present:** modal + base verb

| Certainty | Modal | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~100% yes | **must** | The lights are on. He **must be** home. |
| ~50% | **might / may / could** | He **might be** at the gym. |
| ~100% no | **can't / couldn't** | He **can't be** hungry. He just ate. |

**Past:** modal + **have** + past participle

| Certainty | Example |
| --- | --- |
| must have | The street is wet. It **must have rained**. |
| might / may / could have | She **might have missed** the bus. |
| can't / couldn't have | He **can't have seen** me. I was hiding. |

**Watch out:** *mustn't* is **not** the opposite of *must* in deduction. For "sure it's not true," use **can't**.

Exercises
---------

**A. Rewrite in the passive.**  
1. They built this bridge in 1990.  
2. Someone is watching us.  
3. They will announce the results tomorrow.

**B. Use the passive causative.**  
4. A mechanic is fixing my car. → I \_\_\_  
5. A photographer took our wedding photos. → We \_\_\_  
6. Someone is going to paint my house. → I \_\_\_

**C. Complete with a GIVE idiom.**  
7. I'm not sure I can do it, but I'll ***.  
8. Please \_\_\_ before you visit so I can clean up.  
9. The box is heavy. Can you*** ?

**D. Choose must / might / can't (+ have if past).**  
10. She's wearing a ring. She \_\_\_ be married.  
11. He \_\_\_ be 60. He looks about 30!  
12. I can't find my keys. I \_\_\_ left them at work.

Answer Key
----------

1. This bridge was built in 1990.
2. We are being watched.
3. The results will be announced tomorrow.
4. I'm having/getting my car fixed.
5. We had our wedding photos taken.
6. I'm going to have/get my house painted.
7. give it a shot / give it a try
8. give me a heads-up / give me a ring
9. give me a hand
10. must
11. can't
12. might have / must have (both OK; the certainty changes)
