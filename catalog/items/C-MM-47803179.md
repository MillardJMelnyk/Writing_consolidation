Compare Hamas and Fatah

### Claude · 2026-10-07 13:13

Fatah is the secular nationalist party founded by Arafat in 1959 that dominates the PLO and the Palestinian Authority, recognized Israel under Oslo in 1993, coordinates security with Israel, and runs parts of the West Bank under Abbas. Hamas is the Islamist offshoot of the Muslim Brotherhood founded in 1987, rejected Oslo and recognition of Israel (though its 2017 charter accepts a state on 1967 lines as an interim step), won the 2006 legislative elections, took Gaza by force in 2007 after the US-backed Fatah effort to oust it, and relies on armed resistance; today they're tied at 24% each in PCPSR's August 2026 poll.
