### Millard · 2026-09-29 02:08

if i rerolla unique item in path of exile 1, can it change its rarity or only the stats?

### Claude · 2026-09-29 02:08

Only the stats: a Divine Orb rerolls the numeric values of a unique's existing mods within their ranges, and the item always stays unique.

### Millard · 2026-09-29 02:09

what about orb of alteration? will that chang e uniqueness?

### Claude · 2026-09-29 02:09

No, an Orb of Alteration only works on magic items, so the game won't let you use it on a unique at all.

### Millard · 2026-09-29 02:10

what is a magic item?

### Claude · 2026-09-29 02:10

A magic item is a blue-named item with one or two random mods (up to one prefix and one suffix), one tier above white normal items and below yellow rare and orange/brown unique items.
