Almost no one, anywhere, not even in academia, does this well.

Validity" here is the philosophical term: only *valid* claims are ***candidates*** for judging how true or false they are.

If your thinking tests high for bullshit, it means it's not even worth considering: not even *in the running* for "right"; ***not even wrong****.*

Take any chronically unresolved debate you like -- "theism vs. atheism", "nature vs. nurture", "determinism vs. free will", "chicken vs. egg"🤣--and do the checklist for both sides.

What will you find?

Both sides are bullshit.

In fact, were anyone to subject the complete corpus of academic thought to this checklist, they would find the same thing for the overwhelming majority of it.

The plain fact is that humans don’t know very much at all. If we did, we wouldn’t have made an Eden into such a hell as it is. And we wouldn’t make such a ridiculous to-do over what little we know.
