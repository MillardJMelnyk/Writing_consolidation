### Claude · 2025-09-21 13:49

"The straw that broke the camel's back" IS the metaphor you're describing. It refers to one final small addition that causes total collapse.

Variants:

* "The last straw"
* "The final drop that makes the cup overflow"
* "The grain of sand that tips the scales"
* "The butterfly effect" (chaos theory context)

All describe minor triggers causing disproportionate system failure.
