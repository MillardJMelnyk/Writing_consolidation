“Equality” has always been a sham, and it was framed by the same psychopath supremacists that perpetuate the crimes against humanity that the world's problem is represent.

The issue isn’t inequality. Equality is the result of a valuation that no human being and no number of human beings are in a position to perform. Obviously, no one has the kind of information they need to perform in such valuation. What's more, no one has a right to the kind of superiority required to be in a position to evaluate others.

Valuation itself is a crime against the human soul and spirit and the integrity of being that no one has a right to disrupt.

The valuation is not the root of the evil. The root is the deliberate denial of the preciousness of every single person.

Preciousness is not estimable. You can't quantify it, therefore, you cannot evaluate it.

“Equality” is meaningful only to people who have forsaken their humanness, elevated themselves as judges and evaluators, then proceed to deliberately violate others by objectifying them, reducing them to their social functionality, refusing to recognize their preciousness -- and after all that evil, having dehumanized them, proceed to do whatever evil acts they feel like doing to them.
