At base, psychosis is a disconnection from reality.

The normal mental/emotional/sensory processes that connect the non-psychotic to the real world have been damaged and, to a serious degree, severed in the psychotic.

They live in their own hallucinatory worlds, where they see themselves on Mt. Olympus and the rest of us far below them, mired in muck of their creation.
