### Millard · 2025-12-01 02:19

If I have a trading account with $100 in it and I make 5% a day how long will it take me to double the account

### Claude · 2025-12-01 02:20

Approximately 14.2 days.

Calculation: ln(2) / ln(1.05) = 14.21 days

On day 14: $197.99
On day 15: $207.89

### Millard · 2025-12-01 02:21

Assume that I trade 5 days a week and average 5% a day how much money will I have in the account after 6 months

### Claude · 2025-12-01 02:21

**$56,857**

Calculation:

* 6 months = ~26 weeks
* Trading days = 26 weeks × 5 days/week = 130 days
* Final balance = $100 × (1.05)^130 = $56,857

### Millard · 2025-12-01 02:36

What if I only make 2.5% per day

### Claude · 2025-12-01 02:36

**$2,478**

Calculation:

* Starting balance: $100
* Daily return: 2.5% = 1.025
* Trading days: 130 (26 weeks × 5 days/week)
* Final balance = $100 × (1.025)^130 = $2,478
