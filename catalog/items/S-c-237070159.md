What should you do?

What are your obligations as the citizen of an evil empire?

You mean: given that there's no fucking way in hell that you'll even casually entertain the question of whether there should be nations at all, run by psychopaths who not only don't give a fuck about shredding little girls into tiny bits, but actually enjoy doing it because it proves they can; and you have no fucking intention of abandoning your infantile death-clinging to the very systems that not only enable the psychotically murderous to do their evil, but rather spawn, incubate, nurture, and encourage their psychoses, creating "the rich and powerful" from which to select the most deranged and evil of them for the insanity known as "power" -- what are your obligations, then?

What obligations do you have as a responsible citizen and a good person in an evil nation?

What should you do in an evil society permeated by an evil culture, operating as an evil system -- one that you've no fucking intention of disowning or leaving, let alone terminating, let alone obliterating?

Yes -- what should you do?

Well, what can you do when one side of your brain critically depends on evil for your survival and fanatically clings to your identity as a member of a heinous, evil entity and the other side desperately scratches and grasps for a way to make words and spin yarns that present you as the golden ring in the swine's snout?

What then?

Well, honestly, really...

What the fuck does it matter?

What the fuck difference will it make for a person like you to do one thing instead of another, except as it valorizes your and signals your copious bullshit virtues?

Honestly, really...

What the fuck are your "obligations"?

Obligations?

In other words, you don't give an actual fuck.

The operative issue for you isn't what you feel like doing, what you want to do, what every fiber of your being is screaming for you to do, and failing to do it would constitute self-betrayal and self-violation.

No. The operative issue for you is what you're *obligated* to do.

Obligated by what and for whom? Do you even know? What would you do if you felt no obligation at all? Would you even know what you want? How would you even begin to go about deciding what to do if nothing obligated you one way or another?

Most people today won't even find those questions intelligible. They won't have a fucking clue what I'm asking.
