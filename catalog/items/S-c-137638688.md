It's not going to start in London. The change will start in the East. It's Eastern Europe that's going to snap first, because they've been pushed to the fucking edge. Not with Netflix and Starbucks. Not with rainbow flags and safe zones and distractions... With poverty with militarization with foreign puppets running the damn show.

They said EU will bring prosperity. Where is it? Half the youth is gone working in kitchens abroad. The governments don't protect them. They sell them. They sell them to Brussels, to NATO, and to whoever pays better.

These people, my people, they remember what dictatorship feels like. They remember the propaganda. They remember the checkpoints, the lies dressed as law. They’ve seen this cycle before, and they are done pretending this is freedom. And when they rise—when they rise—they won't be asking for reforms. They'll be demanding for heads. They'll be shaking the system.

Because when obedience becomes death, rebellion is survival.

//
