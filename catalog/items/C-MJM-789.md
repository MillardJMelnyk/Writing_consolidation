**Compare dialog (per-comparison settings):**

1. Review > Compare > Compare...
2. Click **More >>** to expand.
3. Under **Comparison settings**, check/uncheck what to track: Insertions and deletions, Moves, Comments, Formatting, Case changes, White space, Tables, Headers and footers, Footnotes and endnotes, Textboxes, Fields.
4. Under **Show changes at**, choose Character level or Word level.
5. Under **Show changes in**, choose Original document, Revised document, or New document.

**Revision display (how markup looks):**

* Review > Tracking dialog launcher (bottom-right arrow of the Tracking group) > **Advanced Options**.
* Set colors, marks (underline, strikethrough, etc.), moved text, table cell highlighting, formatting, and balloons.
* Review > **Show Markup** toggles which categories display (Insertions and Deletions, Comments, Formatting, etc.); **Specific People** filters by author.
