### Claude · 2025-12-18 18:48

No high-quality reputable brand exists for clear acrylic LED message boards.

**Why:**

* Entire category is generic Chinese manufacturing
* No established brands (Alpine, etc. only make black-frame versions)
* Consistent quality issues across all products: difficult to clean, visible streaking when lit, poor surface quality

**Critical problem from verified reviews:**
Markers leave residue that appears as streaks/smears when LED illuminates the acrylic. Even with proper cleaning (water, all-purpose cleaner), streaking remains visible. This is inherent to the transparent acrylic + LED combination.

**Options:**

1. Accept generic product with known cleaning issues
2. Buy black-frame version from Alpine Industries (quality manufacturer, but not what you want)
3. Don't buy this product type

The Amazon search you provided shows only generic products. None will be meaningfully better than others.
