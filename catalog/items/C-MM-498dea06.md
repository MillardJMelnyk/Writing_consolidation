BLOCKERS — fix before you sign on 15 Sep

Every notice mechanism in this contract runs by email: the 30-day renewal notice, the 48-hour defect notice, the 5-day cure notice for complaints. With no address filled in, Kelly cannot give valid notice and the landlord can later claim any notice she sent was ineffective. Do not sign until it is typed into both places.

**2. The landlord's notice address is the leased unit itself.** Clause 18 lists "EL ARRENDADOR" at Avenida de la Vida 104, interior C49, Condominio Helios — that is the apartment Kelly will be living in. Legal notices to the landlord would be served at the tenant's own door. Replace with Laura Daniela Gomez Meza's real domicile.

**3. Utility non-payment is converted into rent default.** Clause 9: if services aren't paid, "no se le tendrá por pagada la renta" — the rent is deemed unpaid. That cascades into Clause 16(A)/(K) rescission plus the two-month penalty in Clause 13. Worse, the same clause makes *suspension of any service, for whatever cause*, grounds for rescission — including a CFE outage, a billing dispute, or arrears left by the previous occupant. Demand: limited to amounts actually owed by the tenant, after written notice and a 10-day cure period.

**4. Automatic rescission on mere suspicion.** Clause 16, final paragraph: the contract dies automatically if the property is "resguardado, relacionado, investigado o asegurado" by any authority on suspicion of a crime — attempted or completed, inside the property or outside it, committed by the tenant, the obligor, or anyone either of them let in. No conviction required. No finding required. Combined with Clause 4, Kelly then owes the full sale value of the house. Demand: acts imputable to the tenant, established by a final non-appealable determination.

OBJECTIONS — negotiate
----------------------

**Clause 2 (Term).** Internally contradictory: the term "concluye el día prefijado sin necesidad de desahucio" — ends automatically, no notice needed — and then two paragraphs later requires both parties to give 30 days' written notice stating whether they renew or terminate. Get it stated that failure to give notice does not extend the term, does not renew, and triggers no penalty. Holdover is $1,000/day *plus* rent; ask for it capped.

**Clause 3 (Rent).** Payment window is the 15th–19th only — five days. The late-charge paragraph then refers to "la fecha de pago" without defining it. Define: due date = the 19th, late charges run from the 20th. The charges themselves are 5% of a full month's rent if paid within five days, 10% after — flat penalties, not interest. 10% of $8,300 is $830 for being six days late. Push for a single 5% tier. Separately, the sentence imposing an annual INPC increase is inoperative inside a fixed 12-month term; it belongs only in the renewal clause — have it deleted here. Verify the CLABE belongs to Laura Daniela Gomez Meza before the first transfer.

Arithmetic checks out: 12 × $8,300 = $99,600; penalty 2 × $8,300 = $16,600; deposit $8,300.

**Clause 3, final sentence.** Rent may not be withheld "por ningún concepto o determinación, ya sea judicial o extrajudicial" — no set-off even against a court order. Ask for removal, or at minimum subordination to the repair exception now in Clause 13.

**Clause 4 (Use).** Uncapped indemnity for the full sale value of the property under the Extinción de Dominio law, triggered by acts of anyone the tenant admits to the premises. Limit to acts imputable to the tenant.

**Clause 5 (Rules).** Three sanctions during the term = rescission, with no right to see the complaint, contest the fine, or cure. Demand written notice of each sanction and a right to contest before it counts toward the three.

**Clause 6 BIS (Hidden defects).** Kelly must report any defect within 48 hours of detecting it or pay damages for the delay. That is unreasonably tight — a slow leak noticed on a Friday. Change to "promptly, and in any event within 5 business days." It also contradicts Clause 7, which uses the looser "oportunamente" standard for the same duty; align them. And the landlord only has to *begin* repairs within 15 days — there is no completion deadline anywhere. Add an outside date and a repair-and-deduct remedy.

**Clause 7 (Condition and return).** The entire repair-allocation section — four paragraphs, from "conservar EL INMUEBLE en las condiciones en que lo recibe" through the duty to report defects — appears **twice, verbatim**. Delete the duplicate. If either copy is later amended and the other isn't, you have two conflicting versions of the same obligation in one contract. Also: two parking spaces are granted but not identified by number — specify them. Pets are permitted, which is good; note that pet damage is expressly excluded from natural wear.

**Clause 8 (Damages).** The landlord disclaims liability "en ningún caso y por ningún motivo." That purports to cover her own negligence and latent defects, and sits directly against the repair duties she accepts in 6 BIS and 7. Carve out the landlord's own fault. Clause 15 repeats the same overreach for injury to persons.

**Clause 10 (Joint obligor).** Claudia's liability survives any extension, renewal, modification, rent increase or forbearance, without notice to her and without her consent, and she waives orden, excusión y división. Open-ended. She should cap her exposure at this 12-month term and require her written consent to any renewal or increase. Her liability ends only when the landlord "formally receives" the unit — so insist on a signed acta de entrega-recepción on move-out day, and language that the landlord cannot unreasonably refuse to sign it.

**Clause 12 (Deposit).** Returned 45 days after handover; push for 30. The deposit cannot be applied to the last month's rent. The added paragraph requiring itemized deductions backed by photos, invoices or quotes is a real improvement — keep it, and add that any undisputed balance is returned on time regardless of a dispute over the rest.

**Clause 13 (Penalty).** Two months' rent for breach of *any* clause, with no materiality threshold and no cure period. Limit to material breach after written notice and failure to cure, and make it a cap on damages rather than additive to the costs and damages the same clause also claims. Separately: "en caso de haberse pagado el año de arrendamiento de forma anticipada no se devolverá el pago de rentas no devengadas" — if she prepays the year and leaves in month two, she forfeits ten months. **Do not prepay the year.** The added exception disapplying the early-termination penalty where the landlord fails to make its own repairs is good; tighten "plazo razonable" to the 15 days already in 6 BIS.

**Clause 14 (Legal policy).** On renewal she must renew a póliza jurídica with Despacho ACCR at her cost, or the contract is rescinded — and the amount is stated nowhere. Get the figure in writing now. Note that ACCR drafted this contract; they are the landlord's counsel, not neutral.

**Clause 16(G).** Designating the unit as a fiscal domicile is grounds for rescission. If she ever needs to register an address with SAT, invoice from home, or receive SAT correspondence there, raise it now. **16(N):** the contract terminates on the tenant's death, leaving any other occupant with no rights — relevant if a second person is living there.

**Clause 17 (Confidentiality).** Broad enough that having the contract reviewed or translated is technically a breach. Carve out lawyers, accountants, translators, and disclosure required by law.
