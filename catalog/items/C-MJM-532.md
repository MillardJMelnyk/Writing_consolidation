### Claude · 2025-12-26 16:47

Assuming you mean **compound hunting bow**:

**Maximum physical flight**: ~300-500 yards (optimal 45° launch angle, light arrow, high draw weight)

**Effective hunting range**:

* Most hunters: 20-40 yards
* Skilled: 40-60 yards
* Expert: 60-80 yards (ethical ceiling for most game)

**Limiting factors**: Arrow drop becomes severe past 60 yards (~30+ inches at 80 yards). Wind drift compounds. Ethical kill shot probability decreases sharply.

If you meant something else by "heading bow," clarify.
