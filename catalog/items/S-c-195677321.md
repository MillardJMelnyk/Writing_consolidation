If there were nothing to the idea that the universe is aware of us (sun included), there would be nothing to "dismiss" as "superstition".

That's the inherent hypocrisy of dismissal: we know something considerable is there waiting to be considered, but we don't want to consider it, so we dismiss it.

Few stupid moves are more basic.
