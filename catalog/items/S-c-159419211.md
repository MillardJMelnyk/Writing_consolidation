Chomsky was right, because this shit everyone (including him) calls "power" is **violence itself**.

You worship it. You think other kinds of power are weak compared to violence. That is your psychosis talking.

The definition of a "state": monopoly on legitimate **violence** (MLV). (Max Weber, "Politics as a Vocation", 1919, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopoly_on_violence>)

MLV as the minimal requirement that establishes a state has never been refuted.

Your country exists as a state wholly and only because it holds a monopoly on legitimate violence.

Nations are defined wholly and solely in terms of violence.

The "greatness" of a nation is measured in violence.

You'll say it's also measured in other ways -- "prosperity", for example.

No, it's not -- because the prosperity you're talking about belongs to the few, not all, and it was secured by violence to all by the few.

At this point, a lot of people knee-jerk their admission of ignorance as if it were an argument: "But it's the only way we know! It's the best we can do!"

Yes, I believe you, and it's tragic that you have no fucking clue how to share Earth's abundance without first threatening violence (extortion) or executing violence (seizure).

Explain ownership under any “economic system” you choose, (it doesn’t matter because they’re all pigshit hoaxes that disguise what’s really going on,) without extortion and seizure. Remove both extortion and seizure from any of them and they will collapse into senseless, meaningless rubbish.

The origins of all ownership lie in seizure and deprivation, and ownership is sustained by ongoing seizure and deprivation.

All ownership is the implementation of violence.

So, in violence-worshiping cultures like ours, it's no wonder that all those who "rise" to the "top" understand exactly one thing -- violence.

What Chomsky omitted is that all who follow "leaders" who understand only violence are, fundamentally, violent themselves.
