I was wondering about this just a couple of days ago. It’s called “iatrogenesis”.

Estimates of iatrogenic deaths range from a conservative **1/8** to a more extensive **1/3** of deaths annually in the U.S. This would translate to roughly:

* **1/8**: Around **35,000–40,000 deaths** per year.
* **1/3**: Around **100,000–105,000 deaths** per year.

Which airline that was responsible for 1/8 to 1/3 of all the deaths in the US annually would you fly?
