But why do you need power only when you want to do something harmful?

Because your concept of power is for shit. You think violence is power.

Think about the greatest, most powerful people and institutions. What does their power consist of?

Violence or the threat of violence used to extort submission and obedience.

Punishment is violence. Therefore law is violence. Therefore government is violence.

The definition of a state is the monopolization of legitimized violence. So nations are violence.

The only kind of power that ultimately holds sway in the political and legal affairs of humankind is violence. So politics is violence.

So why is violence the currency of the "rich and powerful"?

Because the "rich and powerful" are psychopaths.

Violence is the language and the motivation and the method of the mind of psychopaths. It's the only medium that makes any sense to them.

So, see what James says about the rich in light of all this:

James 5

New American Standard Bible 1995

//

Come now, you rich, weep and howl for your miseries which are coming upon you. Your riches have rotted and your garments have become moth-eaten. Your gold and your silver have rusted; and their rust will be a witness against you and will consume your flesh like fire. It is in the last days that you have stored up your treasure! Behold, the pay of the laborers who mowed your fields, and which has been withheld by you, cries out against you; and the outcry of those who did the harvesting has reached the ears of the Lord of Sabaoth. You have lived luxuriously on the earth and led a life of wanton pleasure; you have fattened your hearts in a day of slaughter. You have condemned and put to death the righteous man; he does not resist you.

//

James didn't qualify it. He didn't differentiate between good rich people and bad rich people. His words were to "you rich". They know who they are. They also know who isn't one with them.

What's going to happen to them is

for them to worry about. It's none our business. James wasn't talking to us there.

Our business is to realize that we should never, ever, in a million fucking years, look up to or honor, let alone idolize, being rich.

But instead of doing the smart thing, we've done the most stupid, insane thing possible: we've put them in charge of everything and in a position to tell us what we can and can't do. And now, as we're broaching into straight up totalitarianism, to tell us what we can and can't say or even think.
