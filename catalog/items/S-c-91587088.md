This is such a good example of the power of honesty.

We don't need to "prove" anything. We just need to be honest, report honestly what we experience and witness, share our honest perspectives on it, our honest opinions about what it means it, and ask honest questions about it.

If the reaction we get is to trample our honest pearls underfoot and turn to tear us to pieces, then we know we're dealing with pigs in the sense of Jesus' metaphor.

Honest people don't do that to honest people.
