There's no record of Gibson saying it, and fake Gibson quote-memes are common, so the attribution is likely fabricated (Likely). The meme argues that Palestinians, and Gaza especially, have no excuse for militancy because other bombed-out nations didn't produce terrorists. That claim is false on its own terms. The postwar generations in both countries did produce terrorists: West Germany's Red Army Faction (Baader-Meinhof) and the Japanese Red Army, which carried out the 1972 Lod Airport massacre. The comparison also leaves out the key difference: Germany and Japan were rebuilt with Marshall Plan and US money and got their sovereignty back within about seven years, while Gaza has been under blockade and occupation for decades.
