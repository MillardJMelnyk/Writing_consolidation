The conservative figure is Gaza Health Ministry's recorded count of 73,651 Palestinians killed as of 5 September 2026, which is mostly direct violent deaths and is widely treated as an undercount. The high estimate is the Lancet correspondence projection that, applying a "conservative estimate" of four indirect deaths per one direct death, up to 186,000 or even more deaths could be attributable to the war; that letter was not peer-reviewed and was calculated in mid-2024, when the recorded toll was about half today's figure.
