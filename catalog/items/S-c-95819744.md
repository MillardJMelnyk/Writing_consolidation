All of you who are certain that governments, laws, authorities, and other coercive, violence-based staples of "civilization" are necessary to prevent "chaos from ensuing" and are mandatory to secure collective safety:

Read my conversation about it with DeepSeek.

<https://chat.deepseek.com/a/chat/s/60b3f575-5ff8-4714-8a88-22aa1183c168>

READ and THINK and show me HOW SPECIFICALLY we didn't cover ALL the major objections to the idea.

READ and THINK and show me HOW SPECIFICALLY any of those objections turn out to be even so much as slightly more than superstitions.

In my early forays into AI chatbot conversations, I asked them to describe how a society that has no laws, authorities, governments would work. This was to see how well they could reason and how biased they were.

They all provided very similar (almost spookily similar) responses to the effect that, ultimately, the idea was utopian, idealistic, impractical and, at best, feasible only on very small scales.

So, I argued with them. I sent one of them into a spin it never returned from. I pointed out so many bogus claims and self-contradictions to another one (I think Copilot) that it shut the conversation down: "Let's talk about something else." 😆

So, I thought I'd test DeepSeek similarly.

It did pretty well. Not as biased out of the gate, brought up some good points. Parroted the same old "challenges" I've heard for decades, but when I explained how they're bogus, it recognized my points and agreed.

You law-and-order cultists: see if you can do better.
