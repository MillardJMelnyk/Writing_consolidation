I keep hearing people mention the heinous atrocities of Hamas as if it were the Goliath and Israel were David, and yet nobody who brings it up is specific about the nature or the scale of these purported horrors.

That's a huge red flag right off the bat.

So I did some fact checking and quantifying to make sure I wasn't missing something important.

No, I wasn't.

Hamas is not exonerated of committing war crimes, no more than Israel is. But the idea that there is any comparison between the two in terms of the gravity, scale, or sheer evil of their war crimes towards citizens, especially women, and most especially children and babies targeted by Israel, is just shit-assed bonkers. Fucking gaslighting.

I haven't heard a single person yet cite a count of dead children targeted by Hamas that increases by the day. Not one.

I haven't seen a single photo of an Israeli neighborhood leveled, reduced to rubble. Not one.

Any idiot could see that there has been no genocide in Israeli-populated areas or even in Israeli-occupied areas.

So these shit turds grossly exaggerating the atrocities of Hamas while ignoring the far greater atrocities of Israel don't even rate up there with idiots.

I'm pretty sure that sighted idiots have a better grasp on things than do the blind that even idiots make look like fools.
