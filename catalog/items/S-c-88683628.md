Someone mentioned recently that, more and more, people seem to be just sticking their heads in the sand.

That's understandable, of course. It's what happens when denial becomes untenable but we're still not ready to come clean.

Head in sand is temporary, though: otherwise, they die. Whenever a cult implodes, there's always a gaggle of diehards who go with it.

The rest, one way or the other, pull their heads out.

And we've got to keep in mind where our information is coming from. 99.9% of what's actually happening at any point in time is well beyond our awareness, let alone any reporting.

The truth always comes out. Always. It does its work even though it's rarely admitted and most of its effects are never recognized. It doesn't care. It doesn't need permission or approval.

I'm learning to be more like that. Bottom line, no matter what is or is not going to happen, it doesn't change what I need to do. I still struggle with disillusionment, depression sometimes, but that's all about letting my minuscule 0.1% awareness affect my motivation.

The one thing I have no doubt about is that everything I do will have an effect. The more I do the more effect it will have. Is it enough? Will it be enough? When will it be enough?

Who the hell knows what "enough" is? I certainly don't, and I don't know of a single person who does.

Makes everything really simple.

What should I do? What can I do? What will I do?

Everything I fucking can, along with learning to become even more capable, so that there's less and less "I can't".

❤️

In my version of Sisyphus, he blows the fucking boulder up, flips the gods the bird, and crosses the fucking hill.

Never looks back.
