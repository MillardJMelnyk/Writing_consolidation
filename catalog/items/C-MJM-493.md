### Claude · 2025-12-20 11:05

3I/ATLAS is the third confirmed interstellar object, discovered July 1, 2025 by the ATLAS survey telescope in Chile. It's designated "3I" (third interstellar) and C/2025 N1 (ATLAS).

**Trajectory (as of December 2025):**

* Hyperbolic orbit (unbound, won't return)
* Closest approach to Earth: December 19, 2025 at 1.8 AU (~170 million miles)
* Closest approach to Sun: October 29, 2025 at 1.36 AU (between Earth and Mars orbits)
* Currently departing the inner solar system
* No threat to Earth

**Composition (from JWST and VLT observations):**

* Unusually rich in CO₂
* Small amounts: water ice, water vapor, CO, carbonyl sulfide
* Emitting cyanide gas and atomic nickel vapor at concentrations similar to solar system comets

**Age estimate:**
Possibly 7+ billion years old (older than solar system) if originated from Milky Way's thick disk.

**Observability:**
Was visible through ground telescopes until September 2025, passed too close to Sun, reappeared early December 2025. Multiple NASA missions (Hubble, JWST, Europa Clipper, Psyche, MAVEN, others) observed it.

Pre-discovery images found dating back to May 21, 2025.

### Claude · 2025-12-20 11:07

**Avi Loeb's claimed anomalies (July-December 2025):**

1. **Trajectory alignment:** Retrograde orbit within 5° of ecliptic plane (P=0.002 per Loeb's calculations)
2. **Non-gravitational acceleration:** Trajectory changes near perihelion, with predicted perijove distance (March 16, 2026) matching Jupiter's Hill radius within 0.06 million km
3. **Size/mass:** Nucleus ~1 million times more massive than 1I/'Oumuamua, ~1,000 times more massive than 2I/Borisov, while moving faster
4. **Sunward jet (anti-tail):** Persistent forward-pointing jet/glow, confirmed by HiRISE imaging near Mars
5. **Nickel/iron ratio:** High nickel without corresponding iron (Loeb claims resembles industrial nickel alloy production); nickel-to-cyanide ratio orders of magnitude higher than known comets
6. **Extreme negative polarization:** Unprecedented level for comets, similar to trans-Neptunian objects
7. **Arrival timing:** Close approaches to Mars, Venus, Jupiter while unobservable from Earth at perihelion (P=0.00005 per Loeb)
8. **Directional origin:** Within 9° of Wow! Signal direction

**Scientific consensus response (as of December 2025):**

* JWST confirmed outgassing of CO₂, H₂O, CO, OCS
* Non-gravitational acceleration (~5×10⁻⁷ m/s²) consistent with cometary outgassing
* Coma and tail observed by multiple instruments
* Chemical composition within expected range for comets from different star systems
* Most astronomers regard it as natural comet; Loeb's statistical arguments criticized as post-hoc probability calculations

### Claude · 2025-12-20 11:08

**What Avi Loeb says is weird about 3I/ATLAS:**

1. **Path through space:** Its orbit runs backwards compared to planets and lines up almost perfectly with the flat plane where planets orbit (only 5° off). Loeb calculates this happens by chance only 0.2% of the time.
2. **Course changes:** The comet shifted trajectory near the Sun. Its predicted path will bring it exactly to Jupiter's gravitational boundary in March 2026—matching within 60,000 km.
3. **Massive size:** It's roughly 1 million times heavier than the first interstellar object ('Oumuamua) and 1,000 times heavier than the second (2I/Borisov), while traveling faster than both.
4. **Forward jet:** Most comets spray gas backwards (away from Sun). This one has a persistent glow pointing forward in its direction of travel.
5. **Metal composition:** Contains lots of nickel but little iron—a ratio Loeb says matches industrial metal production, not natural rocks. The nickel-to-cyanide ratio is way higher than any known comet.
6. **Light reflection pattern:** Reflects light in an extreme way never seen in comets—more like icy objects at the solar system's edge.
7. **Convenient timing:** Passed close to Mars, Venus, and Jupiter while hidden behind the Sun from Earth's view at its closest approach.
8. **Where it came from:** Arrived from within 9° of where the famous "Wow! Signal" radio burst came from in 1977.

**What most scientists say:**

* Space telescopes detected normal comet gases (carbon dioxide, water, carbon monoxide) evaporating off it
* The course changes match what happens when gas jets push a comet around
* It has a visible tail and cloud of gas like regular comets
* Its chemistry is different from our comets, but that's expected from another star system
* Loeb's probability calculations are questionable because he's cherry-picking weird features after already seeing the data
