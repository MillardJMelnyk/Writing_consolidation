I've maintained a HUGE open window for bloviators to let rip their flatulence, for two reasons:

1. Not every brain fart is 100% toxic. Some actually have some sweet spots that are worth it.

2. Even if a person is utterly chock full of shit, I get to learn how to deal with their ilk and their games.

I spent the majority of my life tolerating these stinky windbags, operating under the delusion that they knew something I didn't.

I finally wised up.

I spent the last 30+ years tolerating them in an extreme version of don't-throw-baby-out-with-bathwater that I've yet to see anyone else even come close to.

I've learned a shit ton.

The last year or two, though, I've found myself getting bored with it. I won't say that I've learned everything I need to know, let alone everything there is to know -- but boredom tells me when I've started to spin wheels letting others spin theirs.

Besides, if I'm getting bored, I ain't learning shit.

So, my open-for-bullshit window is rapidly closing. I just don't have time or energy or bandwidth to waste on drivel, anymore.

I went well over a decade on social media barely blocking anyone at all. I don't expect that trend to continue.

Just thought I'd FYI y'all.

I've got 10 - 20 years left (barring miracles of *actual* medical science), and I'm gonna use it as wisely as I can.
