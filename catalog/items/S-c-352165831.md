Caitlin, your points about so-called "capital punishment" (I'm so fucking sick of how these things get euphemized) by the State of criminalized individuals hold all kinds of merit. As applied to people who create and sustain systems of mass murder, rape, and extortion, they lose their clout because of something you didn't address: scale.

I see merit in your points even when applied to murderers, rapists, mass shooters, and serial killers. I don't see their merit when applied to genocidists, ethnocidists, war mongers, and other criminals against humanity.

On individual scales, especially once they've been stopped, the death of the killer can amount to as much harm and damage as the death of their victim.

On societal scales, that's a concern that people who seek and acquire "power" exempted themselves from.

If you can stop the genocidal, then I'd still agree -- there's no benefit in murdering them. I know what it feels like to want to see a mutherfucker burned alive, but satisfying that urge/compulsion is of no benefit beyond me and, arguably, is of no benefit to me, either.

But if the only way to stop the genocidal is to blow their brains out, then the thousands or millions of lives that would be saved blows away any concern or sympathy for the evil asshole. The best for the most people trumps benefiting their violators. Do both if you can. But if you can't, there's no question and no contest.

The other concerns about vindictiveness, judgmentalism, and the like, are ego concerns. Fuck that shit. They don't even figure in when we're focused on securing the good and eradicating evil.
