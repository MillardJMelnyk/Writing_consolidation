### Claude · 2026-09-16 00:43

Past perfect progressive is officially a B2 structure, so for A2 I'll keep the story vocabulary and sentences at A2 level and limit the grammar to the basic form and one use: an action that was going on before something else happened in the past.

Here's the plan for a one-page-per-section PDF, 60 minutes total:

1. **Warm-up (5 min):** Three picture-free discussion questions about a bad day.
2. **Vocabulary (5 min):** 12 A2 words from the story, with simple meanings.
3. **Story (10 min):** About 250 words, "Maria's Terrible Tuesday." She arrives at work wet, tired, and late, and we find out why (she had been waiting for the bus, it had been raining, and so on). The past perfect progressive forms are in bold.
4. **Grammar box (10 min):** had been + -ing, with positive, negative, and question forms, and a simple timeline.
5. **Comprehension (10 min):** 6 questions about the story.
6. **Fill in the blanks (10 min):** 8 sentences using verbs in brackets.
7. **Match the result to the reason (5 min):** 6 pairs.
8. **Speaking pairs (5 min):** "Why were you tired, wet, or happy?" with the answer frame "Because I had been…"
9. **Answer key** on the last page.

The story topic and the timing split are my picks, so change either if you want.

Here's how I read the request: a new worksheet on prepositions of place and orientation, with pictures and a short story, as a PDF styled like the last one.

Three things aren't clear, so here's what I'll use unless you say otherwise:

* **"Orientation":** I'm reading this as prepositions of direction and movement (into, out of, across, through, past, along, up, down, around, towards). If you meant facing words instead (opposite, facing, to the left of, to the right of), tell me.
* **Level and length:** A2, 60 minutes, same as the last one.
* **Pictures:** simple drawings I make myself, a cat and a box for place and arrow scenes for movement. I can't use photos or AI-generated images in the PDF.

Here's the change plan, following the timing of the last worksheet.

1. **Warm-up (5 min):** Three questions about your room and your way to work or school.
2. **Place picture cards (5 min):** in, on, under, next to, between, behind, in front of, above, near, opposite. Each has a cat-and-box drawing and one example sentence.
3. **Direction picture cards (5 min):** into, out of, across, through, past, along, up, down, around, towards. Each has an arrow drawing and one example sentence.
4. **Story (10 min):** About 220 words, "Leo Can't Find His Keys." Leo searches his apartment (place), then walks to his friend's house (direction). All 20 prepositions are highlighted, and there's a drawn street map of his route.
5. **Look and write (10 min):** 8 new cat-and-box drawings; students write the preposition.
6. **Story gaps (10 min):** 8 sentences from the story with the preposition missing, plus a word bank.
7. **Follow the map (10 min):** Students trace a route on the street map from written directions, then write directions to a second place.
8. **Your turn (5 min):** Pairs, with one example in speech bubbles. "Where's your phone right now?" and "How do you get from your home to the store?"
9. **Teacher's page:** timing and answer key.

The student pages will likely come to 6 because of the pictures. Say GO to proceed, or correct any of the three points first.
