“Consent of the governed”.

“Politicians don’t care.”

“Politicians don’t hold themselves accountable.”

*Holy bleeding Christ.*

I don't argue that this vid won't help people who are severely cultized to start realizing, at a superficial level, the insanity going on.

The deeper problem is the psychosis required to make "consent of the governed" seem like a credible, coherent idea.

Sure, if you're already bent on being governed, it's the kind of bullshit rationalization you might come up with to make yourself look like you're not the sniffling codependent you actually are. But that's about it.

The merit of the idea is on the order of making your older brother the head of the household, expecting him to behave with the kind of love and grace and care and forgiveness that a good parent would demonstrate.

*What the fuck?*

OMG, now I'm at the part where the problem is that the politicians don't care? What's up with expecting psychopaths to care? Even worse, what's up with the kind of sniveling codependence that can't conceive of any solution other than getting psychopaths to care?

*Holy shit.*

Now the problem is that politicians don’t hold themselves accountable.

Why would anyone in their right mind expect politicians to hold themselves accountable? And what's up with the psychosis responsible for thinking that it's a good idea to to put people in power who will abuse that power, always, unless we hold them accountable? Why not put people in power that we can trust? Why rely in the first place on a system predicated on the kind of power that just begs to be abused and that, to the degree that it's perfected, perfectly corrupts?

The only kind of mind that any of this could make any sense to is a mind that has been brutalized, conquered, enslaved, and has resigned itself that there is no hope for anything completely different. In other words, codependents.
