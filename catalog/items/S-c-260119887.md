Not certain beliefs.

Believing itself.

There is only one kind of believing that isn't evil: believing people.

"I believe you."

"I believe in you."

Those are beautiful, precious, and holy.

"I believe X, Y, and Z" is antichrist. Full stop.

True believing connects us to each other.

Believing ideas, abstractions, imaginations, hallucinations (ALL abstractions are imaginary if not hallucinatory) is what people do TO ***AVOID*** CONNECTING TO OTHERS, AS A VILE SUBSTITUTE TO CONNECTING TO OTHERS.
