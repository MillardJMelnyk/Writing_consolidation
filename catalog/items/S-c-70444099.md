Me neither. I'd even stop at the crushing part.

The penalizing part gets me way past no mercy or compassion and well into let's just blow the shit out of this mutherfucker.

But how is that different than any civilization that has existed anywhere, ever?

There have always been classes, there is always only one ruling class with others below it -- WAY below it.

As one person put it, the dreams of the ruling class are constructed from the nightmares of the classes below them.

What society that we have ever called "civilized" was any different?
