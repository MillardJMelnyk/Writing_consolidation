345,000 Ti — kTi is just kilo-Ti, one thousand Terraformation Index units, the running total of your planet's overall terraformation.

### Millard · 2026-08-26 23:44

how do i get more kti?

### Claude · 2026-08-26 23:44

Build and power more terraformation machines — heaters, pressure/drills, oxygen units, then plants, insects, animals; each one adds Ti/s and the total is your TI.

Fastest gains come from upgrading to higher tiers (T2 heaters, Ore Extractor T2, Vegetube T2/T3, etc.), unlocking the next stage so new machine types become available, and throwing fusion energy cells or high-rarity materials into things that accept them.

### Claude · 2026-08-26 23:46

Audit of what I told you:

**Wrong**  
- "Heaters/oxygen units go outside, skip rooms." T1–T3 heaters must go indoors; T4 is the first outdoor one. Same for vegetubes — first two inside, third outside.  
- "Throw fusion energy cells into things for more Ti." Fusion Energy Cells only open hidden doors in shipwreck reactors; player buildings don't consume resources to run. The actual boost mechanic is Machine Optimizers, which take Fuses and accelerate the nearest machines — a heat fuse makes a heater produce 500% of its base value, and multiple optimizers stack with no cap on fuses boosting one machine.

**Right**  
- kTi is thousands of Ti. Ti is the sum of Oxygen, Heat, Pressure, and Biomass (plus Purification on toxic planets).  
- More machines and higher tiers raise it; drills push Pressure, heaters push Heat, plant devices push Oxygen, and blueprints unlock off both total Ti and individual lane milestones.  
- Power matters. If demand exceeds supply, every terraforming and resource machine stops until you add production.

**What I left out that's bigger than anything I said**  
Terraformation rockets give a 1,000% multiplier to a specific stat and stack, so keep buildings of the same type grouped together. Late on, biomass dominates everything else.
