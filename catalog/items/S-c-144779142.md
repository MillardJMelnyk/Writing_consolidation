Step by step, you're just sitting there, watching the jackboots march us right into tyranny -- which I and others started raising the alarm about more than a decade ago, and many, many others had been sounding it long before us -- and still you're vacant-eyed, limp-limbed, aghast, LYING THAT THERE'S NOTHING YOU CAN DO.

I used to say "pretending", but no more.

At this point, pretense is untenable.

YOU ARE FUCKING LYING ABOUT IT.

This is something simple, easy, foolproof, and well within the means of even the weakest, stupidest, laziest of you.

STOP FUCKING PAYING FOR IT.

We're approaching a crossroads you'll be forced to make a choice about sooner rather than later. Why wait until your hand is forced?

At some point, the evils you're hoping you'll slither through and maybe they'll go away are going to surpass your ability to rationalize them away.

You're going to be forced to answer: WHERE DO YOU DRAW THE LINE?

AT WHAT POINT DO THE HARM AND DESTRUCTION OF THE EVILS YOU FUND ANNUALLY EXCEED THE RISK OF CUTTING OFF THE FUNDING?

In other words, how much is your integrity worth?

A lot of you -- MOST OF YOU, SO FAR -- have clearly demonstrated that your integrity is worth exactly fuckall to you. It doesn't have to stay that way.

But if there is no line you'll draw anywhere, at any point -- no excess of murder, rape, pillaging, and destruction that is too much for you, nowhere you'll stop and say NO MORE...

Then at least stop your fucking performance that you're someone who gives a shit. You're happy to give shit-as-words, you frauds -- no one argues that. Your charades will be the death of you.

The irony of sacrificing your integrity to save your skin is that you finally end up with neither.

It won't be long now...
