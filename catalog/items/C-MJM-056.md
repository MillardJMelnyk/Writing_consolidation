### Claude · 2025-06-30 02:35

Here are some sharp, sarcastic comebacks you can use:

**For general juvenile insults:**

* "Wow, did you workshop that one, or did it just fall out of your mouth fully formed?"
* "I'd agree with you, but then we'd both be wrong."
* "That's the best you've got? I've seen more creativity in a parking meter."

**For name-calling:**

* "Oh no, my feelings are so hurt by someone whose greatest achievement is remembering to breathe."
* "Thanks for that insight from someone who clearly peaked in middle school."

**For appearance-based insults:**

* "Coming from you, I'll take that as a compliment."
* "I'm sorry, I don't take criticism from people who look like they shop exclusively at gas stations."

**The nuclear option:**

* "I'd explain why you're wrong, but I don't have the time or the crayons to make it simple enough for you."

The key is delivering it with complete calm and a slight smile - nothing deflates a juvenile insult quite like someone who's clearly operating on a different level entirely.
