### Millard · 2026-09-06 03:05

where is osmium?

### Claude · 2026-09-06 03:05

On the floor of the ice caves — blue crystalline ore, blocked until your Heat melts the ice open — plus treasure crates inside them, late-game meteor showers, and a T2+ Ore Extractor placed in an osmium cave.

### Millard · 2026-09-06 03:28

where to get uranium?

### Claude · 2026-09-06 03:28

Two uranium caves — bright green glowing ore: Highlands northeast at 1785:25:1945, and Gate Desert at 585:35:-667; also blue crates and wrecks, and asteroid-attraction rocket meteor showers. Drop a T2 Ore Extractor in either cave for a steady supply.

### Millard · 2026-09-06 04:01

WHAT DOES SHREDDER MACHINE DO?

### Claude · 2026-09-06 04:01

Destroys items outright — up to 30 at a time, nothing returned — mainly to dump the junk ores your extractors keep spitting out.

### Millard · 2026-09-06 04:02

what about recycling machine?

### Claude · 2026-09-06 04:02

Breaks a crafted item back down into the ingredients that made it — one at a time by hand in the T1, automatically in and out of lockers in the T2.
