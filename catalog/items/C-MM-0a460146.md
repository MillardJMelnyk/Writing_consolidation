### Claude · 2026-10-17 19:00

Saturday, October 17, 2026, 12:59 PM CST

**Likely** a real business but not the hotel: SVH Travel is a California-based agency run by Vic Sarkisyan that looks legitimate, and its admission that it owns BookResorts means krystalixtapa.com is its third-party site posing as the hotel, the kind San Francisco sued other operators over this year for imitating real hotels and charging much higher rates. You already have the room through Expedia, so give them nothing, especially card details.

### Claude · 2026-10-17 19:02

**Likely** en.krystal-ixtapa.com is the hotel's real site, since Northstar's venue directory lists it as the Krystal Ixtapa's web address, with phone 52 755-555-0510, while krystalixtapa.com is the SVH/BookResorts lookalike. To be sure your 1310 USD booking is real, call that 755 number with your Expedia confirmation and have them confirm it's in their system.

### Claude · 2026-10-17 19:09

Pushing after you said no is the high-pressure pattern, and a credit card authorization form means emailing your full card number and security code, plus your signature on it makes a chargeback harder to win if anything goes wrong. His "licensed and accredited" claim can be checked against California's Seller of Travel registry, but your Expedia booking already does the job, so you've lost nothing by ignoring him.
