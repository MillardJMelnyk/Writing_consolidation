### Claude · 2025-09-23 22:31

Moving the goalpost: Changing evaluation criteria or success metrics after receiving evidence that challenges your position.

Example: Person A claims "No studies show X works." Person B provides studies. Person A shifts to "Those studies are too small" or "I meant peer-reviewed studies."

Mechanism: Avoids admitting error by retroactively altering what would constitute proof.
