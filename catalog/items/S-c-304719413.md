A lot in this one, but at the beginning Sasha explains why "diary" is a mischaracterization / misrepresentation. It contains memos and emails, etc., that Fauci wrote in his roles as a federal employee, but calling it a diary is theater.

Even if Fauci were prosecuted and convicted, even if he were executed -- hanged for crimes against humanity like some of the Nazis were -- as emotionally gratifying is it might be, it would amount to nothing more substantial than offering up any other patsy would.

Dramatics on the level of personalities always serve the purpose of distracting and deflecting attention away from actions that would actually change things.

Government materializes a spirit that operates beyond the confines of particular people, organizations, institutions, systems, or even nations. Paul talked about them: principalities and powers and rulers, the spiritual forces of wickedness in heavenly places.

In other words, in heavenly places where spiritual powers like love and care and hearts knit together should be the operative forces -- powers that cannot be organized or institutionalized -- we've replaced them with organizations and institutions that operate by means of supremacism, elitism, guilt, punishment, shame, and other forms of spiritual violence and spiritual death.

People looking to the organizations and institutions created to force life-giving powers out of heavenly places as the ones they rely on to protect us, protect life, and oppose evildoers that harm us and destroy life -- those people are deeply, seriously operating under grave delusions.
