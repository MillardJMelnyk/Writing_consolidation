### Millard · 2026-09-22 17:17

if I had 760 dollars from a US employer and were to have it wired to me in queretaro in mexico, how much would i loose on the conversion?

### Claude · 2026-09-22 17:17

Likely **$25–$55** (about 3–7%) on a normal bank wire into a Mexican peso account: roughly $15–$25 lost in the bank's exchange-rate markup and $10–$30 in wire and intermediary fees. At today's rate of about 17.5 pesos per dollar, the $760 is worth about 13,300 pesos before any of that comes out.

The same $760 sent through Wise loses about $5–$8.
