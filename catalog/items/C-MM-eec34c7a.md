### Claude · 2026-09-21 00:54

Yes, mechanically: the galaxy is procedurally endless, pirates and NPC factions spawn and attack, there are abandoned stations to salvage, shops to trade with, asteroids to mine, factories to build, warp gates to link it all up. Two people can run a faction and never hit a content wall in the sense of "nothing left to do."

But the actual content is you two building ships. The PvE is crude — pirate AI is dumb, combat is mostly who brought more blocks, and there's no quest structure, no story, no progression beyond bigger numbers. If you both like engineering — designing a hull, wiring weapon arrays, making a hangar that actually fits your fighter — it's hundreds of hours. If she wants a game that gives you things to do, she'll be bored in two evenings.

Honest read: Highly likely she bounces off it unless she's into the building side. Sky: Children of the Light or We Were Here from earlier are much safer bets for a girlfriend who isn't already a sandbox-engineering type.
