difference beteen borrow and lend

### Claude · 2026-08-04 19:38

Lend = give something to someone temporarily (you lend to someone). Borrow = take something from someone temporarily (you borrow from someone).

Example: "I lent Mario my car." / "Mario borrowed my car."
