### Claude · 2025-11-30 23:20

Francisco Tárrega (1852-1909) was a Spanish classical guitarist and composer. He established foundational techniques for modern classical guitar performance, including systematic fingering methods and the use of fingernails for tone production. His compositions include "Recuerdos de la Alhambra" (featuring the tremolo technique) and "Capricho Árabe." He also transcribed works by Bach, Beethoven, and Chopin for guitar. Born in Villarreal, Castellón, he studied at the Madrid Conservatory and spent most of his career performing and teaching in Barcelona and Valencia. His pedagogical approach influenced subsequent generations of classical guitarists, particularly through students like Miguel Llobet and Emilio Pujol.
