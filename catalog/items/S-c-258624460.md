I don’t deal in beliefs. I deal in facts.

If I don’t know something is a fact, I don’t pretend it is.

However, at a certain point, the mental gymnastics required to think that Shizzrael is NOT an evil, genocidal, terrorist state are simply too difficult to keep up.

Honesty doesn’t require us to believe, but it does demand that we say exactly what we think. And if you love honesty—as I have come to—there’s no demand about it in the first place.

It’s the only thing that makes any fucking sense.

And, dude, it’s just not difficult to see if and when you’re performing those gymnastics.
