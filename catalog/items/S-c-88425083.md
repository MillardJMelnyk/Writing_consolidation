I agree completely. But even this doesn't get to the root of it.

Overthrowing the principle of *preemptive deprivation* lies deeper.

What attracts people to preemptive deprivation?

The paranoia of infantile codependence extended into adulthood.

In a word, *infantilism*.

Independents don't need the rich in any way. Need them like cancer.

Codependents literally cannot conceive or imagine how to do without “leaders” and, in fact—although most are reluctant to admit it, their compulsive obedience-is-virtue behavior testifies to it loud and clear—they cannot do without ***rulers***.

That's the psycho-social seismic shift that needs to occur before we’ll be in a position to talk about what needs to be done. People must stop thinking and behaving like infants hanging from the teat.

More than 99% of the work and discussions happening now about how to solve the problems and cure the ills of the world occur in codependent contexts between codependent minds. That's the mentality that the rich have hammered into us, which enables and creates the problems and ills we need and want to overcome. Can't solve or cure them using the same mind that caused them and is trapped in them.
