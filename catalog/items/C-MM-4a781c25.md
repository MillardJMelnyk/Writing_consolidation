### Claude · 2026-08-25 10:21

Built for a one-to-one class — say the word if it's a group and I'll adjust the pairing steps.

**Timing**

* **0–5** Warm-up: ask them the worst interview question they've ever been asked, and why it was bad. Gets them talking, gives you a baseline.
* **5–15** Language input (Part 1 of the handout). Don't drill it — at C2 the gap is precision and register, not vocabulary. Focus on hedging and self-repair.
* **15–30** Competency questions (Part 2). You ask, they answer in ~90 seconds, no interruptions. Note errors silently, feed back after every second answer.
* **30–42** Pressure questions (Part 3). Interrupt them mid-answer, push back, ask "is that really true?" The skill being practised is composure and reformulation.
* **42–52** Full mock run: five questions back to back, no coaching, no stopping.
* **52–60** Feedback using the table on the last page. One thing to change, not five.

Two things to watch for at C2: over-long answers (fluency masking a lack of structure), and register slippage into either textbook-formal or too casual.
