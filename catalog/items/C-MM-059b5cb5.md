### Claude · 2026-10-01 16:30

Denim is a sturdy cotton twill fabric, usually woven with a blue-dyed warp and white weft, best known as the material jeans are made from.

what is cotton twill?

### Claude · 2026-10-01 16:31

Cotton twill is cotton cloth woven so the threads form diagonal ribs across the surface, which makes it stronger and more drape-friendly than a plain over-under weave.
