Sometimes. The problem is -- like with a wildfire, sure, it'll eventually burn out -- but what all will get burnt to a crisp along the way and at what cost?

I think the biggest problem in thinking that ends up with these myopic kinds of ideas is that people's emotional capacities -- their care, empathy, connectedness, humanness -- are woefully stunted. Otherwise, they'd realize it's not OK for the fires of psychopaths to burn themselves out and hurt everyone else as long as they don't touch "me".

The true power lies in our agency. We are adults. We have the power of honesty and truth. We have the power to learn and become more competent. We have the power to decide what is right and wrong and choose what to do about it AND THEN ACT.

No one, anywhere, has the power to undo or cancel what we have already **done**. The most they can do about it is manipulate minds to perceive and understand it falsely. But those minds don't control matter. The universe doesn't give a shit about their deceptions and delusions.

The effects of what we DO are invincible. They can be opposed, but they cannot be cancelled or undone.

Every single bloody one of us has agency -- and it's no less powerful than that of douchebags who **see their own agency as pitiful** and, because of **their deep sense of their own inferiority** have no choice but to manipulate others into subjecting their agency to the douchebags as “leaders” and “rulers”, sacrificing their agency and power to the service of these pieces of shit.
