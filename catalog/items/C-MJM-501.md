Is there a Mexican gift basket that's traditional at Christmas?

### Claude · 2025-12-21 15:46

Yes. The **"Canasta Navideña"** (Christmas basket) is a traditional Mexican gift basket.

It typically contains:

* Staple foods: beans, rice, cooking oil, sugar, flour
* Holiday items: ingredients for ponche (Mexican fruit punch), seasonal fruits, nuts
* Sometimes: canned goods, cookies, beverages, small toys

Commonly given by employers to employees as a Christmas benefit, but also exchanged as gifts between families.
