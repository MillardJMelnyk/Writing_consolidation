Why are epistemics important?

First, because if you don't know how you came to a conclusion, you have no idea whether you can trust the conclusion. The mere fact that you trust it for no known reason says nothing about its trustworthiness.

Second, because the deplorable fact is: hardly anyone knows or cares how they came to their conclusions. Hardly anyone does anything to figure it out. Even fewer do anything to ensure any figuring out they did made any real sense. In other words: hardly anyone cares about epistemics.

And that means -- straight up and undeniably -- that their conclusions are, by definition, bullshit.
