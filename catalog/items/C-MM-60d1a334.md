### Claude · 2026-10-01 15:11

99.9% is the advertised rate, and it is in line with other Mexican fintech cards like Stori, which lists 99.94% with a CAT around 148%.

Your actual rate can differ: under the contract, the maximum is TIIE plus up to 110 percentage points, disclosed when you sign up, on your carátula, and on each monthly statement. Your rate is on your Plata statement in the app.

Purchases cost nothing in interest if you pay the full "pago para no generar intereses" amount each month. Cash withdrawals and transfers from the credit line are the exception, because they accrue interest from the day of the transaction, even if you pay in full.

### Millard · 2026-10-01 15:23

Are there any banks that have cards with interest rates comparable to American cards?

### Claude · 2026-10-01 15:23

Yes, but mostly premium cards from traditional banks, and they want Mexican credit history.

* **Banorte Fácil (classic):** CAT around 18.1% before IVA, the only one in US range.
* **Banorte Infinite:** weighted average rate 30.83% before IVA.
* **Banregio Platinum:** weighted average rate 32.9% annual, CAT 38.4%, no annual fee, but requires prior experience with a bank credit card with a 50,000-peso line.

Fintechs like Plata and Nu sit at 86–100% because they lend to people without that history.

### Millard · 2026-10-01 15:24

What are Banorte's ATM fees

### Claude · 2026-10-01 15:25

It depends whose card you're using:

* **Banorte debit card:** withdrawals at Banorte ATMs are free, and the debit terms list $0 for withdrawals at other banks' ATMs in Mexico too.
* **Another bank's card, including a US card, at a Banorte ATM:** about 30 pesos per withdrawal and 10 pesos per balance check, and higher at airports, bus stations, and convenience stores. The exact fee shows on screen before you confirm. Your own bank may add its own fee on top.
* **Banorte credit card cash advance:** 6%–6.5% of the amount, plus interest from the day of the withdrawal.

### Millard · 2026-10-01 15:27

What's the information on BBVA's Start card?

### Claude · 2026-10-01 15:27

BBVA Start is BBVA's entry-level card, priced like the fintechs rather than like a US card:

* **Interest:** variable, weighted average 83.39% annual before IVA (about 7% a month), with a CAT promedio of 124.8%.
* **Annual fee:** none, for life.
* **Intro offer:** all purchases in the first 30 days automatically go to 3 months interest-free.
* **Payment window:** 20 days after the statement date to pay without interest.
* **Lost or stolen card replacement:** 192 pesos.
* **Requirement for foreigners:** a temporary or permanent Mexican residence card.
