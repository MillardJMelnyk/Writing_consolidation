Most of us begin by countering violence on the same level as the aggression. This is where we start, but we need to make sure it's not where we end.

In this mindfucked world, "power" means violence used to dominate. The goal of "power" is always to dominate and, if possible, to achieve complete control.

This is something that only psychotics are drawn to, and something that seems sensible only to them.

We need to get to the point where we're not threatened by violence anymore and become fully confident that we can deal with it. We make that progress by dealing with aggression on the level it's dealt out. But this is the level of opposing, not not the level of overcoming.

To overcome violence, you need to drop down a level and attack the assumptions on which the aggression was predicated.

This does 3 things.

First, it immediately deflates the aggression, because it's looking for retaliation on that same level where it was dealt out. When we respond on a more fundamental level, the aggressor has nothing to fight at that level, now -- the only level that makes sense to them.

So, second, it baffles and demoralizes the aggressor because they literally do not know what the fuck is going on.

Third, it draws the focus of the confrontation down towards the actual reasons for the aggression, which are always buried beneath the aggressor's psychosis that created it, below the level where the psychosis expresses in behavior. Now the question isn't about the status (right/wrong, true/false, good/evil) of the aggressive actions, but the assumptions, intentions, and motivations that gave rise to the actions.

Of course, a person who reads this will likely want an example of how to do this with someone that's about to punch them in the nose, which is curious -- because, when was the last time someone was about to punch them in the nose? 😆

I think the last time I faced a meathead that wanted to do me bodily harm was back in the early 00s when a guy that came at me with a golf club while I was sitting in my car. I sat there, looked at him straight in the eye, unfazed, and asked him, "What are you gonna do, hit me with the fucking golf club?" He stopped cold, looked back at me, then suddenly turned around, ran and got back into his car, and raced off. He had no clue what I was going to do, but he could tell I was going to do something. Maybe he thought I had a gun hidden In my lap or something, who knows? The funny thing is, there was nothing much I could have done, but it didn't matter.

I didn't fight the asshole. I overcame him.
