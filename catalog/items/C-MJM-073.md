how do you say "hit contract" in mexican spanish

### Claude · 2025-07-12 20:56

In Mexican Spanish, "hit contract" (as in a contract to kill someone) would typically be said as:

**"contrato de sicariato"** or **"contrato para matar"**

More colloquially, you might hear:

* **"encargo"** (literally "assignment" or "job")
* **"trabajo"** (literally "work" or "job")

The person who carries out such contracts is called a **"sicario"** (hitman/assassin).

These are the standard terms used in Mexican Spanish, though the specific slang can vary by region.
