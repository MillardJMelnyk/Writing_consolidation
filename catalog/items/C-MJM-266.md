### Claude · 2025-09-19 19:58

**Key distinctions (as of Sep 2025):**

**Scale & Structure:**

* Authoritarian: State-level control apparatus, bureaucratic hierarchies, formal institutions
* Cult: Small-group dynamics (typically 50-5,000 members), single leader focus, informal structure

**Target Populations:**

* Authoritarian: Mass populations, existing social/political identities, generational membership
* Cult: Individual recruits, often targeting psychological vulnerabilities, voluntary initial entry

**Control Mechanisms:**

* Authoritarian: Legal/state power, surveillance systems, economic coercion, physical force
* Cult: Psychological, emotional, or spiritual manipulation, isolating from outside influences

**Exit Costs:**

* Authoritarian: Geographic/citizenship constraints, family/community ties, economic survival
* Cult: Social/family disconnection, financial dependency, identity dissolution

**Legitimacy Sources:**

* Authoritarian: National tradition, historical continuity, institutional authority, demographic mandate
* Cult: Charismatic revelation, exclusive truth claims, spiritual/transcendent authority

**Resistance Patterns:**

* Authoritarian: Organized political opposition, underground networks, institutional challenges
* Cult: Individual departure, family intervention, deprogramming

**Counter-evidence:** Both use similar psychological techniques (thought-stopping, information control, us-vs-them framing). Overlap exists in personality cults within authoritarian regimes.
