> Humans as a collective cannot fix problems which we don’t clearly see and understand. Our rulers pour so much energy into maintaining influence operations like news media propaganda, Hollywood psyops, Silicon Valley algorithm manipulation and government secrecy in order to obstruct our clear seeing and understanding.
>
> -- Caitlin Johnstone, "The Empire Is Losing Its Ability To Hide Its Ugly Nature"

But the one thing that isn't even remotely clear yet is the codependence that enabled this psycho-killer, rule-and-be-ruled mentality to begin, let alone develop, let alone reach the pinnacles of horror that finally can no longer be denied are the natural results of the psychoses that spawned them.

What do I mean by codependence?

“Our rulers…”

Codependence is what makes you believe you need to be ruled.

Codependents are clingers, straight up. Their sense of existential threat when there is literally no threat drives them to cling to whatever they can latch onto for "protection" and "security" and the redemption of social acceptability and approval.

But simple codependence is not a problem, because we naturally mature out of that infantilism -- unless we are mindfucked and, often, literally fucked, thereby arresting our normal, healthy development.

The problem is that we carry our codependent clinging into adulthood.

Caitlin is right: a collective cannot fix problems it doesn't see and understand. I agree.

But a collective clinging for dear life to the very source of the problems it's trying to fix isn't just going to fail -- it's actively proving its own psychosis which guarantees failure.
