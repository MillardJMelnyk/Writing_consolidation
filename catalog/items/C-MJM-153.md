what's the old name for people who spurn technology?

### Claude · 2025-08-10 17:00

The classic term you're thinking of is **Luddite**.

This comes from the early 19th century English textile workers who protested against mechanization by destroying machinery. They were supposedly led by a figure called "Ned Ludd" (though he may have been fictional). The original Luddites weren't necessarily anti-technology in general - they were more concerned about how new machines were eliminating their jobs and livelihoods.

Over time, "Luddite" evolved to describe anyone who opposes or is reluctant to adopt new technology, though it's often used somewhat pejoratively today.

There are also some other related terms like **technophobe** (someone with a fear or dislike of technology) or the more archaic **machine-wrecker**, but Luddite remains the most historically rooted and widely recognized term for this concept.
