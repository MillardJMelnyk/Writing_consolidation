What do you think?

Trying to crystalize the difference between "good authority" and "problematic/imposed/corruptible authority".

One of the big points I didn't see clearly before was that the common element isn't authority, but deference of judgment.

With that clear, it becomes obvious that authority is about dictating who you defer to, while our self-determination of who we do or don't defer to is none other than simple trust.

This shines a bright light on the fact that our culture and, I have no trouble saying, the cultures of every society that we've ever called "civilized", are supremely trust-phobic. I don't think that's controversial at all.
