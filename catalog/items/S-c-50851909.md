A year and a half ago, I wrote up what I called *The Four Pillars of Humanness.* I’ve been trying hard to rid myself of the trappings—including the terminology—of supremacism.

I’ve realized since then that almost everyone in our culture is so deeply dogmatized that laws are, for them, absolutely necessary, maybe I should rename them: *The Four Laws of Humanness*.

Then they might listen with a little less screaming and shrieking and *DOOM! DOOM!* in their heads?
