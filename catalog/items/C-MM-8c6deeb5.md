### Claude · 2026-09-21 18:18

It's free. You file online at [dfs.ny.gov/complaint](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/complaint).

1. Click **File a Complaint**.
2. Choose **Bank/Lender, Virtual Currency or Other Financial Services Complaint**. That category covers any other complaint type not listed, including money transmitters, which is what Payoneer is.
3. Fill it out in one sitting. You must click Submit within 30 minutes of launching the online complaint form, so write your summary beforehand and paste it in.
4. Include payment ID **1034648649**, ticket **260917-025377**, and the chat transcript.

DFS may share a copy of your complaint with the company, which is the point: it lands with Payoneer's regulatory team instead of front-line chat.

If the portal is a problem, they also take complaints by phone. The toll-free number is 800-342-3736.

### Claude · 2026-09-21 18:38

Yes, GoFundMe allows fundraisers for legal costs (Likely; I haven't checked their current rules). But money isn't what blocks a group case against Payoneer; the contract is.

* **The block:** Your Payoneer terms force every customer into individual arbitration and waive group lawsuits, unless you opted out, which you almost certainly didn't. A fund could pay lawyers, but they'd still have to file a separate case for each person.
* **How lawyers get around it:** "Mass arbitration" means one firm files hundreds of individual cases at once, and the company's arbitration fees pile up until it settles. Firms that do this usually work for a share of the recovery, so you wouldn't need to raise money.
* **What stops it for a single case like yours:** Firms only take mass arbitration when there are hundreds of claimants with the same claim. One held payment is too small on its own, and so far I found no firm recruiting Payoneer customers for this.
