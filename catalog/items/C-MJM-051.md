### Claude · 2025-06-26 17:07

This is a fascinating thought experiment that requires estimating global wealth across all categories. Let me break this down:

**Current Global Wealth Estimates:**

* Credit Suisse/UBS Global Wealth Reports typically estimate total global wealth at around $400-450 trillion
* This includes financial assets, real estate, natural resources, infrastructure, and business assets
* However, you're right that this likely undercounts hidden wealth significantly

**Adding the "Hidden" Categories:**

* Offshore wealth: Estimates range from $8-36 trillion in undeclared assets
* Natural resources: Proven oil, gas, mineral reserves could add another $100+ trillion
* Infrastructure (roads, bridges, utilities, ports): Potentially $50-100 trillion globally
* Government/military assets: Trillions more in land, equipment, facilities
* Intellectual property, patents, brands: Tens of trillions
* "Dark money" and unreported assets: Your point about trillions going "missing" annually

**Generous Total Estimate:**
If we account for all hidden wealth, unreported assets, and assign market values to all natural resources and infrastructure, total global wealth could realistically be in the $800 trillion to $1.2 quadrillion range.

**Global Adult Population:**
Approximately 5.2 billion adults worldwide (18+)

**The Math:**

* Conservative estimate ($800T): ~$154 million per adult
* Higher estimate ($1.2Q): ~$231 million per adult

So each adult human would receive somewhere between **$150-230 million** in this hypothetical redistribution.

This highlights just how concentrated wealth really is - even with generous accounting, there's theoretically enough for everyone to be extraordinarily wealthy by current standards.
