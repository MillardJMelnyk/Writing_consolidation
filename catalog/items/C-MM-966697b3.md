Mercado Libre's own error screen points to the same fix: turn on QR code as your verification method from the app, then log in again on the computer.

One catch: the phone app itself may require facial verification when you set this up, but the phone camera usually works where the PC fails. The labels above come from user reports, not current screenshots, so if your app shows something different, send me a screenshot.

### Claude · 2026-09-25 16:02

There's no Mercado Libre setting for that (Likely). The PC stays logged in only as long as your browser keeps Mercado Libre's cookies, and Mercado Libre can still force a new login whenever it decides to.

What you control is the browser not wiping those cookies:

In Edge, go to Settings → Privacy, search, and services → "Clear browsing data on close" and make sure "Cookies and other site data" is off, or add mercadolibre.com.mx to its "Don't clear" list.

In Firefox, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data and make sure "Delete cookies and site data when Firefox is closed" is unchecked, or add mercadolibre.com.mx under "Manage Exceptions…" as Allow.

Use just one of the two browsers for Mercado Libre, and don't use private windows for it.
