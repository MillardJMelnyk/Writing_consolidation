### Claude · 2025-12-22 16:25

**As of December 22, 2025:**

**Hardest-hit counties (10 counties affected, 600+ rescues conducted):**

1. **Skagit County** - Most severe impact

   * Burlington: Entire city evacuated (10,000 residents), 2-3 feet of water in areas
   * Mount Vernon: Skagit River crested at 37 feet (record), downtown protected by 2018 flood wall
   * La Conner, Edison, Conway, Stanwood: Threatened by levee failures
2. **Snohomish County**

   * Monroe, Snohomish: Rivers exceeded major flood stage by 3+ feet, reached record levels
   * One fatality (33-year-old man drove past warning signs into flooded ditch)
3. **King County**

   * Tukwila: Levee breach on Green River
   * Pacific: Levee breach on White River, parts still under evacuation orders
   * Auburn: Green River flooding
   * North Bend: I-90 landslide closures
4. **Whatcom County**

   * Sumas: Homes flooded by ~15 feet, helicopter rescues from rooftops
   * Welcome: Two homes collapsed into Nooksack River
5. **Pierce County** - RV parks and lowlands affected

**Rivers at/above record levels:**

* Skagit River (Concrete, Mount Vernon)
* Snohomish River (Monroe, Snohomish)
* Cedar River
* Nooksack River

**Current status:** Minor to moderate flooding continues; worst is past but elevated river levels may persist through late December. ~50 miles of US-2 east of Skykomish washed out, potentially closed for months.
