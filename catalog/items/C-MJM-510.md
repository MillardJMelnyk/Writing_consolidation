### Claude · 2025-12-22 16:57

I'll search for current information about this claim.

**No, this is not true.** (As of December 22, 2025)

Japan's OHISAMA satellite project has *not* successfully beamed space-generated electricity to Earth. The satellite has not yet launched. All current reporting describes it as:

* "Planned for 2025"
* "Scheduled for launch in 2025"
* "Aims to be operational later this year"

**What actually happened:**

1. Japan Space Systems announced plans (April 2024) to launch a 180kg satellite
2. Ground-based and aircraft tests of wireless power transmission have been conducted
3. No orbital test has occurred yet

**Previous actual achievements:**

* 2023: Caltech's MAPLE transmitted power *within space* (not to Earth)
* 2015: JAXA transmitted 1.8kW over 55 meters *on the ground*
* 2020: US Naval Research Laboratory's PRAM tested technology in orbit

**The claim's errors:**

* States this "achieved" milestone as completed fact
* Claims it's a "world first" for space-to-Earth transmission (Caltech did space transmission in 2023, though not to Earth)
* Describes it in past tense when it remains future/planned

The text you quoted appears to be promotional content that premature presents a planned demonstration as an accomplished fact.
