"It is a learned dependence on systems, on structure, on authority, combined with just enough distance from consequence to keep us still."

That has a name: *codependence*.

It's the only thing standing between where we are now in this shit and where we could be -- completely out of the shit.

Our codependence.

It's not complicated or esoteric or mystical or mysterious.

* *Stop demanding that others do for you what you can and should do for yourself.*
* *Stop lying.*
* *Stop keeping your mouth shut while other people demand to be done for and lie.*
* *Grow the fuck up and start behaving with all the humanness and maturity of which you're capable, and more will come.*

Your development was arrested. You need to restart it.

Do it now.
