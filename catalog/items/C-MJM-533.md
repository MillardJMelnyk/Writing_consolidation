### Property Crime

Residents reported via social media that thieves stole three transformers from preschool, primary, and secondary schools, leaving them without electricity. The primary school Bicentenario de la Independencia was stripped of wiring, plumbing, bathroom fixtures, metal protections, and classroom equipment. Residents stated this was not the first time and that despite filing police reports, no one is detained and nothing is recovered. (Jan 2023)

---

### Infrastructure/Safety Conditions (2025)

Residents of Fuentes de Balvanera and Villas del Rey reported three months without public lighting as of October 2025, stating the lack of streetlights worsens insecurity. They have suffered years of water supply deficiencies and now face constant lighting failures.

---

### Cartel Context

The area is in apparent dispute between the Santa Rosa de Lima cartel (led by "El Marro") and Jalisco Nueva Generación (led by "El Mencho").

The fraccionamiento is approximately 4 km from the nearest Querétaro population center. Events in the area include placement of threatening banners against police, vehicle burnings on the Querétaro-Celaya highway, and the severed heads discovery.

---

### Municipal Context (Apaseo el Grande)

Apaseo el Grande ranks first in Guanajuato state for robbery with violence cases in 2024.
