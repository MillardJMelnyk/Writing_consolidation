Faulkner -- a racist, sexist, unfaithful, alcoholic Confederate and, given this quote, a hypocrite -- had no experience with what he recommended.

He didn't know what the fuck he was talking about.

"Never be afraid" is couch potato wisdom.

Fear is the very *first* thing you'll experience when you ***decide*** to raise your voice for honesty and truth and compassion against injustice and lying and greed—not in the *doing* of it, but in the *anticipation* of the doing.

Every single person who has ever raised their voice knows this.

None of them would ever tell you "never be afraid".

Fear is what tells you that you’re actually about to do something real.

Instead, they'll tell you: "You'll be scared as shit, but don't worry. 99% of that fear is hallucinated. None of it is even remotely close to what will really happen. Take the leap -- you'll see."

We all need to stop listening to hypocritical bullshitters.

Find the reality first -- listen only when you do.
