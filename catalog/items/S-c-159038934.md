When my wife and I decided to have a child, we chose a fully natural path—no medical interventions. That meant no pregnancy tests, no ultrasounds, no routine doctor visits, no hospital birth. We didn’t want early cord cutting (which can deprive a baby of up to 30% of their blood), no blood draws minutes after birth, no pushy staff pressuring for a C-section just to clock out, and no toxic vaccines injected into our newborn within hours of him taking his first breath.

Some see that as irresponsible. For us, it was the opposite—it was about taking full responsibility, not outsourcing it to a system we don’t trust. We spent nine months learning everything we could. We met with experienced midwives, connected with others who’d home birthed, and listened to countless stories and podcasts.

The result? A peaceful home birth by candlelight, with me catching our healthy baby. A far cry from the clinical, often traumatic experience hospitals normalize. Birth isn’t a medical emergency—it’s a natural process. And 99% of the time, hospitals interfere with it, turning it into a procedure instead of a moment of life. For anyone planning a child, just be aware there is another way. They'll tell you it's dangerous, but everything we learned suggested it was not only safer, but the long term outcomes for your child are much better.
