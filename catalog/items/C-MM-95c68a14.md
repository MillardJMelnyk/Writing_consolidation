### Claude · 2026-05-29 18:36

Two corrections before answering your actual question:

1. I'm Claude Sonnet 4.6, not 4.8 — that version doesn't exist.
2. I can't honestly benchmark myself against Opus on pattern recognition without actually doing the task you're describing.

On the substance: vision-based pattern recognition on candlestick charts is genuinely hard for any current LLM, including me. The failure modes you saw — hallucination and missed obvious patterns — aren't Opus-specific quirks; they reflect a structural limitation. We're not trained as chart analysts; we're trained on text with vision bolted on. A model that sounds more confident isn't more accurate.

CSV data gives a more reliable path than images if the data is well-structured — I can reason about numbers directly rather than infer them from pixels. But even there, "did the model detect the obvious" depends entirely on how the question is framed and what context is given.

If you want to test me on this chart or a CSV right now, I'll tell you plainly what I see and where I'm uncertain — no false confidence.
