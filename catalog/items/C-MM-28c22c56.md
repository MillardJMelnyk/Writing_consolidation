With your US passport there's no visa process for a visit: no advance application, visa fee, or online registration is required. You need a passport and proof of onward travel, and you'll most likely get up to 180 days, set by the immigration officer at entry (some sources still say 90).

### Claude · 2026-10-09 17:57

You qualify through one category: Pensionado needs a $1,000/month lifetime pension (Social Security, military, corporate), while Rentista needs $2,500/month guaranteed for 24 months or a $60,000 deposit in a Costa Rican bank, and investment and digital-nomad routes also exist. You file with immigration, which you can do after arriving as a tourist, with certified passport copies, an apostilled criminal background check, an apostilled birth certificate, and passport photos.
