Your cult belief in the sacredness of medical science is ***wildly*** baseless.

To the tune of 30:1.

From 1900 and 1950, for every 30 deaths avoided thanks to improvements in sanitation, nutrition, and living conditions, 1 person was saved by medical intervention of any kind.

Read that again.

92.3% of the decline in mortality in the US between 1900 and 1950—from 1710 to 960 deaths per 100K—had fuckall to do with "medical science" at all, let alone vaccines.
