### Millard · 2025-12-08 16:00

how do I send someone an invitation to join a group on Whatsapp?

### Claude · 2025-12-08 16:00

**Via invite link:**

1. Open the group chat
2. Tap the group name at the top
3. Tap "Invite via link"
4. Choose "Share link" or "Copy link"
5. Send the link to the person through any channel

**By adding directly:**

1. Open the group chat
2. Tap the group name at the top
3. Tap "Add participants"
4. Select the contact(s)
5. Tap the checkmark/add button

The invite link method works even if the person isn't in your contacts.
