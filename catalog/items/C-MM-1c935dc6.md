### Claude · 2026-06-04 23:37

Ah — so you're asking what "based" means, not asking me a question about myself. Got it.

"Based" originally came from rapper Lil B, who used it to mean being yourself without caring about others' judgment. It spread through internet culture (especially 4chan and later mainstream social media) and now generally means:

* Holding unpopular or contrarian opinions confidently
* Being unapologetically authentic
* Saying something others think but won't say out loud

Someone might reply "based" to a take they find admirably blunt or refreshingly honest. It's basically the opposite of "cringe."

### Millard · 2026-06-04 23:38

When did it come out

### Claude · 2026-06-04 23:38

Lil B started using "based" around **2009–2010**, originally to reclaim an insult ("basehead," meaning a crack addict) and flip it into something positive — being free, authentic, himself.

It stayed mostly in his fanbase and niche internet circles for years, then went mainstream around **2020–2021** largely through Twitter/X and meme culture.
