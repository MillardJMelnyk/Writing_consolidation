Hi Grace, new here.

In your review of \*Don’t Talk about Politics\* you said: "... if you want to change someone’s mind, they have to trust you, and you have to listen to them."

True enough, but not if the implication is that it's the \*only\* way to change their minds.

Granted, if you don't want to piss people off, lose friends, get emotionally pummeled, or be uninvited to next year's family dinner, it's probably the only way. But if we're truly after changing people's minds, with the eventual result that they'll change their behavior, there are other far more effective ways that aren't shackled by ego and relational issues:

1. Simply state they're wrong, over and over and over. If you're not an idiot and they know you're not an idiot, they'll have to reconcile the tension internally.

2. Call them out: for bullshit, for presenting opinions as though they were facts or even truth, for lying, etc.

3. Expose the void of facts, reason, and evidence under their baseless opinions. It's on them to explain what they did that led them to draw their conclusions, where they were, who they listened to, what they observed, etc., along the way. 9 times out of 10, there simply is no such. They just made that shit up. They have no clue how to describe their epistemic process because there actually was none.

Just a few ideas. There are many, many more.
