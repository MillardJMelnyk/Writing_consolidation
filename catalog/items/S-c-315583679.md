There is no doubt that light and exposure will win in the end, as certain as gravity or any other kind of physics.

No doubt at all.

Four reasons (there are more, too):

First, economics. Light and visibility are free. Exposure is easy. The cost of distorting, hiding, misrepresenting, deceiving, and the like, pound for pound, is stupidly expensive. So the free, cheap, easy, and everyone-can-do-it side is against the high-cost, low-yield, terribly-difficult-to-sustain side.

Conflict of interest. The resources and power required for darkness to combat light originate from light in the first place. The only way that agents of darkness can darken is by using light that enables them to do anything at all. Cut off the light they depend on and they become blind and helpless.

Psychology. Everyone hates liars, deceivers, con artists, and other deprivers of the truth. Even liars, deceivers, con artists, and other deprivers of the truth hate them when they get played by them. But everyone, to the extent they themselves are honest, loves the honest, the revealers, and other light-shiners. What's more, to some extent -- unless the person has become a complete psychopath having lost 100% of their soul -- the remaining human part of every darkener hates themself. So throughout the short time they're able to maintain the darkening, their own pain and self-loathing fight against it. The hated don't last long.

Numbers. There are ridiculously more light-shiners than there are darkeners, and I don't just mean people. When someone darkens a bit of the world, they've taken a stand against the entire universe, because nothing in the universe -- except the very few darkeners they can enlist to help them -- is going to abide that shit. 24/7, the universe works against that shit on an incomprehensible scale, second by second, undermining it, exposing it, and destroying it.

When someone decides to darken a bit of the world, the odds they take on are so astronomical, no sane person with knowingly do it. It requires entering into a literal psychosis in which they believe the stupidly impossible is possible and, with no sane basis for thinking so, that they are capable of pulling it off.

This psychosis is the reason people will take on lying in full confidence that it's doable.

So the question becomes how, when the odds against them are so incredible, do they manage to darken anything at all?

The only way they're able to pull this off is by leveraging the flip side of the same psychosis. They need to neutralize and enslave people into playing their game and performing in their charade, people who would otherwise expose them – and they need enough of them to make it look like they’re all dancing around something real, not the noxious hooey it actually is.

Taken together, this entanglement of psychoses on the part of darkeners and their minions has a name.

Codependence.

That’s the part no one wants to mention. Everyone wants to pretend that we’re all a bunch of well-meaning, honest, but uninformed/misinformed/insufficiently informed people who would do the right thing if only we weren’t some shade of blind.

But that’s not what we are.

If you can get people to believe that they’re existentially dependent on you, that psychosis will make them feel they will buck the odds as long as they follow you in your darkening project.

So the darkeners depend on the minions that depend on them. All of them alike enter a psychotic state in which they become oblivious to the actual odds against them, manufacturing a phony confidence, not simply creating it from nothing, but creating it as a product of psychotic perverseness.

All you need to do to render people into this infantilistic state of abject incompetence is beat them down long enough that they forget they’ve got feet to stand on, so they go around everywhere crawling on their knees.
