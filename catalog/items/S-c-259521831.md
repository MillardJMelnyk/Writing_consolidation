LOL, like this should need to be explained.

So, why does it need to be explained?

Because the problem has nothing to do with ascertaining the truth.

Knowing the truth is simple. All you need is:

1. The understanding that truth has nothing to do with theory.

2. An absence of liars.

3. Interaction with what you want to know the truth about.

4. Clear thinking.

There.

I just described the ideal of science. Real science.

A lot of people will object to #1. Why?

Because in a bullshit world like this one, the plausibility of the hypothetical, even the imaginary -- after all, bullshitters just make that shit up as they go along, lol -- passes for demonstrated alignment with reality.

When the fact is: all it aligns with are their prejudices and preconceptions.

Among codependents -- which is to say, cultists -- all it takes to dub it "true" is to plausibly support their prejudices and preconceptions. Is it actually true? That question never even gets asked. For them, there's no need.

For a codependent, if it aligns with The Truth, it's true. Who cares about reality?

That's the disconnection between honest, intelligent people who get accused of "conspiracy theories" and the numbnuts who defame them. The former care about the truth. The latter **disregard** and **avoid** the entire question of truth.

Codependents **redefine** both truth and the question of truth into something unrecognizable to people who actually care about what's true. They reduce both to matters of egocentric comfort.

You can see it in Griffin's own language. "Advocating". "Endorse".

For codependents, the issue isn't whether an idea is true, but whether they're confident in their stand for or against the idea, utterly disregarding the question of its actual veracity. That's why they simply can't understand what the so-called "conspiracy theorists" are doing: they have **no concept** of "Is it true?" in the realistic sense.

That's why evidence means nothing to them and, when you force them confront the evidence, they put all their energy and cleverness into finding ways to discount or dismiss it instead of consider it.

They simply cannot allow themselves to take up a level, noncommittal position from which to ask, "What is true, here?" -- because they are interminably under attack. That's their psychosis. They need a **safe** place afforded by their Truth before they can allow themselves even to just **think**.

That's why they love thought-terminating clichés like "conspiracy theory" so much.
