To everyone who ever supported or defended the practice of ownership, here are some indisputable facts:

* Every society known to us that was not dubbed "primitive", "barabaric", "wild", or "savage" organized itself around the promotion and protection of ownership.
* Many "primitive", "barabaric", "wild", or "savage" societies, likewise, organized themselves around the promotion and protection of ownership.
* "Ownership" in these societies boiled down to the legally recognized right of one person or party to preemptively, unilaterally deprive a necessity or desirable from all the rest of humankind.

I want you to think about that for a minute.

Think about the "preemptively deprive" part and what its implications are.

People mock the idea of a single person or party standing against the rest of humankind to say they're wrong while, at the same time, when it comes to depriving humankind of real necessities and desirables, it’s, "Oh, well! That's different! That's all the rage, and more power to it!"

🤯 🤦🙇‍♂️

And then think of the *magnitude* involved in establishing, legitimizing, and protecting this right to deprive **all humankind** of a necessity or desirable **without any regard to fact**. (More about that in the next note.)

No wonder laws and violent punishment must be brought to bear to protect such insanity, because otherwise no one in their right mind would allow it.

What do I mean by "without any regard to fact"? How does ownership disregard fact?

I mean that without even wondering, let alone questioning, let alone ascertaining, whether deprivation is *in fact* either necessary or advisable with regards to the real people who in fact actually could take or use or destroy resources or property, ownership preemptively, in principle, unilaterally deprives all others, period, case closed without a hearing, let alone a trial. Whether it's a good idea or not, whether it's beneficial or destructive, or whether it's even relevant or not, **ownership is single-minded in its obsession that THERE WILL INDEED BE DEPRIVATION**.

I'd argue: *psychotically* obsessive.

Now, *ask yourself*: does ownership more closely fit the model of sharing, friendship, peerness, care, and love? Or does it fit the model of predation, adversariality, paranoia, self-interest, and violence?

Then, *answer the fucking question*.
