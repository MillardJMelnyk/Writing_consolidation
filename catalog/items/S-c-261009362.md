"Orgy of antisemitism".

I'm not saying there's no such thing as antisemitism. I'm saying that the entire issue has been so fucked over, compromised, and distorted that merely asking what could constitute valid opposition, criticism, and outrage against the actions of self-identified Jews gets met with such knee-jerk, bigoted antipathy and suspicion that most honest inquiry effectively gets squashed before it takes its first breath.

People need to learn to recognize thought-terminating clichés. The whole point of the term "antisemitism" is to shut down discussion by presenting the issue in such prejudicial terms that any honest question of the reasons for antipathy towards Jews is damned before you even look at it, presumptively reduced to moral defect, character fault, or psychological problem, before a word is said.

Honest people don't load language and skew the frame in their favor before listening to a single thing, because when you have the truth you don't need to. You can listen openly, not having judged in abject ignorance what you haven't even heard.
