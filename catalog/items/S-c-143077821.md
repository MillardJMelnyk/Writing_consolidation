Makes sense, but people need to stop floating these unverifiable clips and quotes, unless their intention is to gaslight—in which case, carry on: you’ll just fodder my cannons.

Name, date, and place. It’s really not that hard.

Do you find it “hard” to lift the fork from the plate to your mouth? Is it hard to push on the gas pedal?

People who love honesty don’t find the simple, basic requirements of honesty “hard” to do.
