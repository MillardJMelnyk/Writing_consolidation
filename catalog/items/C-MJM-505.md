### Claude · 2025-12-21 19:48

I need to search for the exact quote and its source to verify it accurately.

The most commonly cited authentic quote from Mullis about PCR limitations is:

**"[PCR is] just a process that's used to make a whole lot of something out of something. That's what it is. It doesn't tell you that you're sick, it doesn't tell you that the thing you've ended up with really was going to hurt you or anything like that."**

Source: 1997 talk in Santa Monica during HIV/AIDS discussion.

**Context:**

* Statement was about HIV viral load testing, not diagnostic testing generally
* Mullis died August 2019, before COVID-19
* He was an HIV/AIDS denialist, which colored his PCR skepticism

**Commonly misattributed quotes:**

* "Anyone can test positive for practically anything with a PCR test" - No verified source
* "Cannot detect free infectious viruses" - Written by John Lauritsen, NOT Mullis
* Any quotes about COVID-19 - Impossible (he died pre-pandemic)

**Actual documented Mullis quote about quantification:**
"Quantitative PCR is an oxymoron" - regarding measuring viral load amounts, not detection capability.

No verified quote exists where Mullis called using PCR as a medical test "stupid" or equivalent. Most viral claims are fabrications or context-stripped fragments from his HIV/AIDS denialism period.

### Claude · 2025-12-21 19:51

Based on the searches, the verified Mullis quotes about PCR diagnostic limitations are:

**Verified authentic quote (1997 Santa Monica talk):**
"With PCR, if you do it well, you can find almost anything in anybody."

**Context:** Said during 1997 "Corporate Greed & AIDS" talk in Santa Monica, California, in context of HIV/AIDS testing.

**Additional verified quote (same 1997 talk):**
"[PCR is] just a process that's used to make a whole lot of something out of something. That's what it is. It doesn't tell you that you're sick, it doesn't tell you that the thing you've ended up with really was going to hurt you or anything like that."

**Verified quote from article context:**
"Quantitative PCR is an oxymoron."

**Factual clarification:** The quote "these tests cannot detect free, infectious viruses at all; they can only detect proteins that are believed, in some cases wrongly, to be unique to HIV. The tests can detect genetic sequences of viruses, but not viruses themselves" was written by John Lauritsen in his December 9, 1996 article "Has Provincetown Become Protease Town?" in the New York Native—NOT by Mullis. Lauritsen quoted only "Quantitative PCR is an oxymoron" from Mullis.

All statements were in the context of HIV/AIDS testing, not general diagnostics. Mullis was an HIV/AIDS denialist. He died August 7, 2019, before COVID-19.
