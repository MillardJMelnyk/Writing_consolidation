Another little excursion into AI botland…

I’ve looked, almost a decade now, for thinkers who seriously critiqued the concept/device of authority as catastrophically self-defeating by its very nature and design.

I’ve looked for almost a decade for anyone who even *knows of* a thinker who seriously critiqued the concept/device of authority as catastrophically self-defeating by its very nature and design.

Results: *nichts.*

I needed a break from my project today, so, seeing as how they have upgraded ChatGPT, I thought I’d give the new and improved version a test drive.

Imagine that! Now the “intelligence” can actually remember information from chat to chat! Hopefully, this is the last time I’ll need to scold it for mansplaining, lol.

Here’s our chat on the question whether anyone has critiqued authority as *intrinsically self-defeating* to the extent of *guaranteeing* catastrophic failure by its very nature and design.

By that, I mean on the order of sprinkling arsenic on your oatmeal in order to sweeten it.

I asked two questions:

1. Has anyone explained how authority is the substitution of command for competence?

It did give me some names on that one. So, that idea should not be controversial. The fact that it’s very controversial to most people is evidence of their membership in the Cult of Authority—but it says *nothing* about authority’s actual nature and design, nor anything about how authority actually works in the real world.

2. Has anyone clearly explained in systems theory terms how "authority" is catastrophically self-defeating by its very design?

Isn’t it just mind-blowing that the key device for the operation of everything we’ve ever called a “civilization” or an “organization” has been predicated on an idea which *NO ONE HAS EVER SERIOUSLY EXAMINED TO SEE IF IT’S FANTASTIC OR A FUBAR???*

“Hey! I’ve got a great new flying machine based on a concept that no one has ever analyzed, let alone tested! But don’t worry! *Everyone knows* that it’s bulletproof! (Or, at least, the best we know how to do!)  *COME ON!* Let’s pack up the family and fly to Australia for holiday!”

Sure, why not? What could go wrong?

So, this is in honor of all the law-and-order cultists who, when I tried to open conversations about this topic, treated the idea as if it came from a mental ward patient. 😆
