Every single horrific act in the history of the human race has, ultimately, been the result of beliefs: supremacism, racism, xenophobia, self-righteous moralism, etc.

You might argue that every single wonderful, good, beautiful, beneficial act in the history of the human race has, ultimately, been the result of beliefs -- but no, that's absolutely wrong.

They've been the result of love, and love has nothing to do with beliefs.

But if you were right, then you'd have a big problem you'd need to explain: how the same cognitive act could be responsible for both the heinous and the beautiful.

Oh, you say, but beliefs are neutral. It all depends on the believer.

That just raises a different problem: how the mentality of the heinous could be the same as the beautiful when it comes to this thing called "belief".

If I showed you how you were thinking and doing exactly the same thing that a psychopath does, wouldn't you immediately wonder if there might be something wrong?

Your thinking makes no sense -- at a psychosis-grade level of incoherence.
