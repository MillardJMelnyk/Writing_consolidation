What would your reaction have been to this meme 10 years ago? Or even just 5 years ago?

That's how quickly minds are changing. It's never been this rapid in my lifetime.

So expect things to change even more rapidly over the next 5 to 10 years. Change happens according to exponential curves. Learn what that means.

But the most important thing is to see the direction of the change, its vector. You can tell where the arrow is going to hit by looking at how it's aimed.

Look at where people say we're going. That will be a certain direction, depending on whom you talk to.

Then look at the direction of the shift in people's understanding of what's really going on as more and more facts come to light. That's the true direction. It doesn't require propagandizing, it's set by what actually happened, not by what people say.

If those two directions -- what people say versus the direction their understanding is shifting towards -- are closely aligned, you're living in a very honest society.

If those directions strongly diverge or oppose each other, you're living in a cult.

This isn't neuroscience. There isn't a single one of you that can't figure these things out for yourself.

The biggest problem, however, is that hardly anyone is even aware of the vectors I just described, and even fewer know how to gauge them and track them.

That's because this world has been made into a cult. What should be common knowledge has been hijacked and held hostage, only to be shared with those who prove themselves loyal, and for a price.
