Jesus wasn't non-violent.

That's no different than people living in a cesspool calling the guy that refuses to jump in "non-shitty".

Not only does it say more about them and their frame of reference than it does about the guy with his feet on the ground, but it's really not even about him.

“Non-violence" is something that's meaningful only when and to those for whom violence is central. The truly non-violent don't define or name or label or transact in the terminology of violence.

The issue here isn't our stand on violence -- it's that we don't take a stand at all unless something is psychologically central to us.

For example, you'd never call yourself a "non-rapist", would you?

So why would you call yourself non-violent? Why would you call Jesus non-violent?

Jesus was violent. He just knew when to be violent. And he knew he was talking to people who were psychotically violent and insanely accepting of violence. So, he tried to open their eyes to see three things about violence:

1. They had no fucking clue when it was and was not appropriate.
2. Their attraction to violence was insane. Psychotic.
3. Of all methods, violence is the least effective, one that should be used as a last resort not for moral reasons, but because it's just stupid when more effective options are available.

Violence isn't a moral issue.

Violence is a psychological issue.

This is how I know that those who frame violence as a moral issue, in terms of virtue and vice, without a clue that it's a psychological issue, are hypocrites. They're not interested in what actually works. They're interested in looking good and being judged as good whether they succeed to improve things or not.

The church has moralized violence because the church itself is violent. By becoming the moral authority on violence, it put itself in a position to define the rules around violence, just like governments do with their laws and their completely unjustified, self-bestowed entitlement to create laws.

Both church and state ensconce themselves as authorities in order to create monopolies on violence -- and specifically, especially, to make sure that none of us have any right whatsoever to become violent against them, no matter how violent they are to us, and no matter how violently they violate us.

It cracks me up that so many so-called Christians think that Jesus is going to come back riding a white horse with a sword that comes out of his mouth to literally slash to death millions of people "... slain by the sword that came from the mouth of him who was sitting on the horse, and all the birds were gorged with their flesh," but that somehow \*that\* Christ is completely different than the one whose "body" is supposed to be on Earth now.

Two different Christs, apparently.

🤣

When I talk about "psychotic", I'm not being metaphorical or rhetorical.
