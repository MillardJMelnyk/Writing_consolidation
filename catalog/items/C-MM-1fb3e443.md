Likely the Division of Research Grants (now the Center for Scientific Review), whose genetics study sections ran most grant site visits; if he reviewed program-project or center grants it was probably NIGMS, or NHGRI if it was genome work after 1989.

### Claude · 2026-10-27 18:28

Likely NIGMS (the National Institute of General Medical Sciences, founded 1962), which funded most genetics research then, with site visits run either by its own review staff for program-project and training grants or by the Division of Research Grants' Genetics Study Section for regular research grants.

### Millard · 2026-10-27 18:29

I don't remember him ever mentioning those. My recollection is that he said he did site visits for NIH.

### Claude · 2026-10-27 18:29

That fits: study-section members were appointed as NIH consultants through the Division of Research Grants and reviewed for whichever institute funded the grant, so "for NIH" is how reviewers normally described it. His exact assignment would be listed by name in the annual *NIH Public Advisory Groups* rosters from those years, which are searchable on HathiTrust and Google Books.
