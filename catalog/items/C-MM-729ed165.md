### Claude · 2026-09-18 00:28

Your safest bet is **Mercado Libre**, filtered to Querétaro, because it's the only route I found where you get your money back if the fridge arrives dead or not as described. That protection comes from Mercado Libre's standard buyer policy, not from these search results. Search "refrigerador usado Querétaro" and stick to sellers with a green reputation bar and many sales.

Other options, ranked:

* **Facebook Marketplace Querétaro** has the most used appliances listed locally (new and used appliances for sale in Querétaro). There's no buyer protection, so only pay after you've seen the fridge plugged in and cold.
* **AFAG Mueblerías (Línea Blanca Querétaro)** isn't used. It sells new refrigerators and other appliances at outlet prices, which can come close to used prices and includes a warranty.

I didn't find a dedicated used-appliance shop in Querétaro that offers a written warranty. The ones with warranties that turned up are in the Mexico City area.
