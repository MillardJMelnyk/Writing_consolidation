Statements like this usually have the false assumption behind them that 80% of people are inherently incapable of critical thinking.

The incapability was conditioned into us, and I'm living proof that we can de-condition ourselves out of it again.

It's not a matter of intelligence, but of honesty and refusal to be dictated by fear.

The proportion remains at 80/20 because the 20% allow it to remain.

We are the ones who can change it, but obviously we haven't yet done what we need to do to change it.
