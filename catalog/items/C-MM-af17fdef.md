### Millard · 2026-10-30 00:12

Is there any such thing as a vinyl upholstery repair material that you can apply to crack vinyl, either spray or brush on?

### Claude · 2026-10-30 00:12

Yes: flexible liquid vinyl/leather filler (Coconix, Permatex, or generic "reparador líquido de piel y vinil" on Mercado Libre México) is brushed or spatula'd into the crack and air-dries; spray products like SEM Color Coat only recolor the surface and won't fill cracks.
