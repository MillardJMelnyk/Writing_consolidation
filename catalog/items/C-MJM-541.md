### Claude · 2025-12-27 19:09

**Preceding Magna Carta (1215):**

* Code of Hammurabi (c. 1754 BCE) - Mesopotamia
* Twelve Tables (c. 449 BCE) - Roman Republic
* Constitution of Medina (622 CE) - early Islamic governance document
* Charter of Liberties (1100) - England, Henry I
* Constitutions of Clarendon (1164) - England, limited church/state powers

**Following Magna Carta:**

* Petition of Right (1628) - England
* Habeas Corpus Act (1679) - England
* English Bill of Rights (1689) - England
* Act of Settlement (1701) - England
* Virginia Declaration of Rights (1776) - American colonies
* United States Declaration of Independence (1776)
* Articles of Confederation (1777/1781) - USA
* United States Constitution (1787)
* French Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen (1789)
* Polish Constitution (1791) - first modern European constitution
* Canadian Constitution Act (1867/1982)
* Weimar Constitution (1919) - Germany
* Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948) - UN

Chronological, limited to documents with direct constitutional/rights influence on democratic governance systems.
