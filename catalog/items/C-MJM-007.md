### Millard · 2025-05-28 13:39

I have a one quarter to three quarter inch gap around the door frame. insects are getting in. what product can I use to stuff in the gap and keep the insects out. I don't want to use any sort of fiberglass product.

### Claude · 2025-05-28 13:39

For gaps that large, you have several good non-fiberglass options:

**Foam backer rod** - This is a flexible foam cylinder that comes in various diameters. You can stuff it into the gap and it expands to fill the space. It's specifically designed for sealing large gaps and won't attract insects.

**Expanding foam sealant** - Spray foam that expands to fill gaps. Look for ones labeled for doors and windows, as they remain flexible. Great Stuff and DAP are common brands. Just be careful not to overfill, as it expands significantly.

**Weatherstripping** - For gaps around the door frame itself, adhesive-backed foam weatherstripping or rubber weatherstripping can work well. You may need multiple layers for larger gaps.

**Steel wool** - While not as comfortable to work with, fine steel wool can be stuffed into gaps and insects won't chew through it. You can cover it with caulk for a cleaner look.

**Rope caulk** - A putty-like material that stays pliable. You can press it into gaps and it won't harden completely, making it removable if needed.

For the best seal, I'd recommend backer rod pushed into the gap first, then sealed over with caulk or expanding foam for a permanent solution. This gives you both the gap-filling and the insect barrier you need.
