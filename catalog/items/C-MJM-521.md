### Claude · 2025-12-24 17:53

**Gender dysphoria**: Clinical term (DSM-5) for distress arising from incongruence between one's experienced/expressed gender and assigned gender at birth. Diagnostic criteria include marked distress or impairment lasting at least 6 months, with specific manifestations like strong desire to be another gender, conviction one has typical feelings/reactions of another gender, or strong desire to be rid of one's sex characteristics.

**Gender anxiety**: Not a standardized clinical term. In common usage, may refer to:

* General anxiety about gender roles, expectations, or presentation
* Anxiety symptoms accompanying gender dysphoria
* Social anxiety related to gender nonconformity

**Key distinction**: Gender dysphoria is specifically about distress from gender incongruence itself. "Gender anxiety" (when used) typically describes anxiety as a symptom or response—either to dysphoria, to social contexts, or to gender-related pressures—rather than the core incongruence experience.
