### Claude · 2025-09-01 17:18

"A war to end all wars"

Originally used to describe World War I (1914-1918). The phrase format "An X to end all Xs" now applies to anything meant to be the final, definitive version of its type.

### Claude · 2025-09-01 17:20

"Not even close" or "way off base" are current.

The baseball metaphor "wrong ballpark" has largely been replaced by:

* "Not in the same league"
* "Miles off"
* "Completely off track"
* "Not even in the conversation"

Most direct equivalent: "Not even close."
