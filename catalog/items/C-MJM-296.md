Alternatives for "invocation" in your context (creating conflict from nothing):

* **fabrication** (making up false conflict)
* **confabulation** (unconsciously generating false narratives)
* **invention** (creating what doesn't exist)
* **conjuration** (summoning into existence, already in your phrase)
* **manufacture** (producing artificially)
* **generation** (bringing into being)
* **construction** (building from scratch)
* **projection** (imposing internal states onto external reality)
* **hallucination** (already in your phrase, perceiving what isn't there)
* **confection** (assembling artificially)
* **contrivance** (devising artificially)

Most precise for your meaning: **fabrication** or **manufacture** (emphasis on falsity) or **projection** (emphasis on psychological mechanism).
