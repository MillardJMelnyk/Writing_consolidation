People talk about a war between good and evil. That’s bunk, because they’ve moralized the entire issue, and morality is bunk.

What we have—quite obviously—is a war between psychosis and sanity. The psychotics must control everything, so they get enraged whenever someone thinks outside their precious, prescribed boxes.

I love articles like these partly just because it pisses those assholes off.

But far more I love them because I feel like “GO GIRL!!!” 😁

We need to get iconoclastic in belligerent *disregard* of the boxes, the dimension-determiners, and the box manufacturers. Fuck everyone who tries to stuff humanity and humanness into X by X spaces. We weren’t built for that.

We were built to run.
