### Claude · 2025-05-28 16:49

The plural of "apparatus" is **"apparatus"** (same as the singular) or **"apparatuses."**

Both forms are correct:

* "Apparatus" as both singular and plural follows the original Latin pattern
* "Apparatuses" is the anglicized plural form

"Apparatus" (unchanged) is more common in formal or scientific writing, while "apparatuses" is frequently used in everyday English.
