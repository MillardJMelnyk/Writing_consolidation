what's the difference between PPI, HPI, and CPI?

### Claude · 2025-11-24 17:04

**CPI (Consumer Price Index)**: Measures price changes for goods/services purchased by consumers. Tracks retail/end-user prices.

**PPI (Producer Price Index)**: Measures price changes for goods/services sold by producers/wholesalers. Tracks prices before retail markup.

**HPI (House Price Index)**: Measures changes in residential housing prices. Specific to real estate market.

Key distinction: CPI = what consumers pay, PPI = what producers receive, HPI = housing prices only.
