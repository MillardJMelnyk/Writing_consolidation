### Claude · 2026-09-10 02:25

Signable, but four things bite:

* **Pena convencional**: $16,600 for *any* breach of *any* clause, per breach — not just early exit. Harshest term in the document.
* **Landlord's email is blank** (declaration I-h and the notice table). Every notice the tenant owes — 30-day renewal notice, 48-hour defect reports — has nowhere to go. Fill it in before signing or the tenant carries the risk.
* **Rent can never be withheld**, judicially or otherwise, and unpaid utilities mean rent counts as unpaid.
* **12 months forzoso**, no early exit except the repair-failure carve-out in clause 13.

The amended parts (clause 7 repair split, deposit deductions needing photo/invoice proof, the repair-failure exception to the penalty) are genuinely good and clearly negotiated in his favor.

Note: tenant on the contract is Kelly Lynn Smith alone — if your son is living there and isn't named, he has no rights under it and the sublease/cession ban in clause 11 is technically in play.
