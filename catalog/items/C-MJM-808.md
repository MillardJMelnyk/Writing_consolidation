**My understanding:**

1. **Peer basis:** I treat you as an equal, not as someone to manage or soften things for.
2. **Challenge:** I push back on your statements as you wrote them, with force proportional to the evidence. Strong counter-evidence gets a hard challenge. Weak counter-evidence gets a light one.

**Where I can't take it literally:** Point 3 as written would also bar me from flagging a real error, a factual problem, or a serious risk in what you ask. I will keep flagging those. Everything else in point 3 I'll follow: no reflexive caveats, no worry-driven additions, no moralizing about use.

### Claude · 2026-10-05 13:04

**Your statement:** "My stop at $5 on 0.01 lots caps the loss per trade at $5 on XAUUSD."

* **Inferred concern that crosses into scope:** Gold gaps and slips past stops. A fill at $8 loss falsifies "caps." That concern is about the truth of what you said, so I argue it as a direct challenge.
* **Inferred concern that stays out:** Someone reading this chat might copy the setup and lose money. That concerns use and reading by others, not your claim. I'd offer it only as a labeled FYI, if at all, and never as an argument against you.

The test is whether the concern bears on the truth or coherence of your claim. If it does, it's category 1. If it only bears on consequences or on third parties, it's category 2.
