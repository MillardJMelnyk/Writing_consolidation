### Millard · 2025-09-16 14:49

how do epistemics differ from epistemology

### Claude · 2025-09-16 14:49

Epistemics: The study of knowledge dynamics - how beliefs form, spread, change, and interact in real systems (individuals, groups, institutions).

Epistemology: The philosophical study of knowledge itself - what knowledge is, how it's justified, what makes beliefs true or rational.

Epistemics focuses on processes and mechanisms; epistemology focuses on foundational concepts and principles.

### Claude · 2025-09-16 14:56

Yes. Two distinct circularities:

1. Justification circularity: Determining adequate justification presupposes knowing what's true.
2. Belief circularity: Belief inherently treats the proposition as true, making post-hoc justification meaningless - you're justifying something already accepted as true.

Different logical problems with the JTB framework.
