This is just one example. People do this shit all the time.

Atheism isn't wrong in asserting there are no gods.

Atheism is *irrational* in adopting an identifier that is meaningless without the very idea they're trying to debunk.

Atheists are also greatly misled, because they mistakenly think that attacking a rationalization will affect the motivations and intentions that led to its formation.

“God” isn't something that "believers" believe -- it's a rationalization used to explain and justify what they *want* to do and are *bound* to do, "God" or not.

"God" is an excuse, created by "believers" precisely because they otherwise would have no real clue what to believe -- but ***believe they must*** -- so they make up stories and believe those.
