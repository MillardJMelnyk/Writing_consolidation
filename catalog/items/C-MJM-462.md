Friedrich August von Hayek (1899-1992). Austrian-British economist and political philosopher.

Key facts:

* Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences, 1974
* Major works: *The Road to Serfdom* (1944), *The Constitution of Liberty* (1960)
* Central contributions: spontaneous order theory, price signals as distributed knowledge, critique of central planning
* Austrian School economist, classical liberal political theory
* Taught at LSE, University of Chicago, University of Freiburg
