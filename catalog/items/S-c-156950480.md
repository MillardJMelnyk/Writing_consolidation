It's unfathomable to me how so many people listening to someone relating there own experience -- and, as the ultimate authority on what happened in their experience, interpret their experience and explain it to us -- will get triggered and freak out, erase and dismiss the person, judge them as some shade of delusional, with absolutely no reason, facts, or evidence to support that judgment, but solely because what they're hearing is too strange for them to handle.

That alone is enough for these morons to judge what they're hearing as ridiculously untrue and scorn-worthy.

This applies to the shit-for-brains application of "conspiracy theory" to ideas that don't closely approximate what they've already decided is true and real, for no other reason than that the ideas don't fit their mold.

It's like they've decided that nothing can be real unless it's something they're already familiar with.

You realize that's a form of psychosis don't you? Not to mention the self-aggrandizement of insisting that reality must reduce itself to the little speck of experience they've had and the infinitesimally small bit of it they're capable of comprehending.

Why does the radically unfamiliar freak people out so badly that they can't bear to listen and must malign and dismiss it like a knee-jerk without any rational basis?

No wonder we've turned into a world of compulsive liars, when simply, honestly sharing our experiences, feelings, and understanding of them consistently serves as an invitation to be attacked and demeaned.

Sane people simply do not respond like that.

This is how I know that every single person who invokes "Conspiracy theory!" isn't just an idiot, but is suffering from a mental condition.

To all of you sad fucks who still do that:

Freaking out at no more than merely hearing information that is strange to you is not a normal, healthy, sane reaction.

To then malign, deride, marginalize, dismiss, and erase a human being on the basis of your triggered reaction is nothing short of evil.

Your grasp of things and your OCD defense of it are not more important than a human being and their grasp of things, you self-worshiping fool.

And pay attention to the huge, decisive, and undeniable difference between imagination xenophobes like you and people, like Dick here, simply relating to you what they've actually experienced.

He's not defending anything. (You can feel him stealing himself for the backlash he knows he's going to get, though.)

Nor is he attacking anything or anyone -- least of all you, flaming narcissist that you are.

It's not all about you, you know.

Nothing is all about you.
