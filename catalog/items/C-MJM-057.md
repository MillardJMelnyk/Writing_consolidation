Based on the search results, I found the exact quote and citation. According to the Wikipedia entry on Steve Bannon, the quote is:

"The real opposition is the media. And the way to deal with them is to flood the zone with shit."

This statement was made by Steve Bannon in an interview with journalist Michael Lewis in February 2018. The full context from the Wikipedia source shows that Bannon was discussing Trump's electoral success and said: "We got elected on Drain the Swamp, Lock Her Up, Build a Wall. This was pure anger. Anger and fear is what gets people to the polls." He then added the infamous "flood the zone" quote about dealing with the media.

**Citation:**
Steve Bannon, quoted in interview with Michael Lewis, February 2018, as cited in Wikipedia's Steve Bannon entry (accessed via search results from July 1, 2025).

The quote has become widely referenced as describing a deliberate strategy of overwhelming the information environment with misleading or confusing content.
