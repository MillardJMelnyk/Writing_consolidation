### Claude · 2025-05-28 15:41

You don't need all those "that"s. Here are a few cleaner options:

**Option 1 (removing one "that"):**
Their *cluelessness* about the possibility they might be proving they're cultists—because they've defined it out of the realm of possibility—is what proves they're cultists.

**Option 2 (removing both "that"s):**
Their *cluelessness* about the possibility they might be proving they're cultists—because they've defined it out of the realm of possibility—proves they're cultists.

**Option 3 (restructuring slightly):**
Their *cluelessness* about possibly proving they're cultists—because they've defined it out of the realm of possibility—is exactly what proves they're cultists.

The second option flows most naturally while keeping your meaning intact. The repeated "that" constructions do make the sentence feel a bit heavy.
