Single "most important" reason, US women, 2004 Guttmacher survey (Finer et al.), the most-cited source on this:

* Not ready for a child or another child: 25%
* Can't afford a baby now: 23%
* Done having children: 19%
* Relationship problems / don't want to be a single mother: 8%
* Too immature / young: 7%
* Would interfere with education or career: 4%
* Mother's health: 4%
* Fetal health: 3%
* Partner or parents wanted it: under 1%
* Rape: under 0.5%
* Other: ~6%
