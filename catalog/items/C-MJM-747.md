### Claude · 2026-08-18 00:17

No unconditional free shipping change found. Current structure, per Walmart MX's own page (as of Aug 2026):

* **Free shipping threshold**: unlimited free shipping on online purchases from $299 MXN through Walmart and Walmart Express, and on items marked Walmart Pass — i.e., ordinary free shipping still exists but requires a $299 MXN minimum, not universal.
* **Below $299**: standard delivery fee applies (not itemized in these results — check checkout).
* **Unlimited/no-minimum shipping**: only via paid **Walmart Pass** membership.
* **Unrelated but concurrent change**: Walmex confirmed via its Q2 2026 financial report that it began transitioning away from "Martes de Frescura" toward permanent "everyday low price" (EDLP) pricing — this is a pricing-promo change, not shipping.

If you're seeing no free-shipping option at all regardless of cart size, that's either a UI/eligibility bug, a category exclusion, or your account isn't hitting the $299 threshold — not a policy elimination. No source indicates Walmart MX removed the $299 free-shipping tier outright.

Specify: order total, and whether checkout shows a fee or no free option at all.
