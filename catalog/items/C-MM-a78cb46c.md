### Millard · 2026-10-21 21:40

I'm fed up with the notes app that came with my OnePlus 12 pro/Android. I want something very bare Bones, simple, fast and easy to use that has the basic drafting operations including text formatting. Give me your top three recommendations.

### Claude · 2026-10-21 21:41

Wednesday, October 21, 2026, 3:40 PM CST

1) [NotallyX](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.philkes.notallyx): minimalistic, open source, rich text with bold, italics, monospace and more, and everything stays on your device; 2) [Google Keep](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.keep): fast, with bold/italic/underline/headings; 3) [JotterPad](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jotterpad.x): a clean drafting editor with fuller formatting.

### Millard · 2026-10-21 21:43

Why does every single Android app in existence scroll text way too fucking fast when you trying to select it by dragging down or up?

### Claude · 2026-10-21 21:43

Because nearly every app uses Android's built-in text box, and that box's "auto-scroll when your finger hits the edge" speed is hard-coded by Google with no setting to slow it, so every app inherits the same bad behavior (Highly likely).

### Millard · 2026-10-21 21:43

Why don't they fix it? It's unbelievable that something like that we get through a usability quality assurance audit.

### Claude · 2026-10-21 21:43

Because Google designs and tests the behavior around short selections like a word, a link, or a sentence, and long drag-selection on a phone is a rare enough use that it never ranks high on their fix list. Changing core text-box behavior also risks breaking thousands of apps, so once it ships "good enough," it tends to stay frozen (Likely).
