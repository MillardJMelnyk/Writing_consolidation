### Claude · 2025-08-23 21:42

No. KBC void (or Local Hole) and Local Void are different structures:

**KBC Void/Local Hole:**

* ~2 billion light-years (600 Mpc) diameter
* Named after Keenan, Barger, Cowie (2013)
* Density contrast δ = 0.46 ± 0.06 between 40-300 Mpc

**Local Void:**

* Different structure, extends up to 300 Mpc from Milky Way
* 15-50% less dense than surrounding areas
* Some media reports conflate the names, but they're distinct in scientific literature

Both contain the Milky Way but are separate underdense regions at different scales.
