### Claude · 2026-10-06 14:55

There is no evidence of a deliberate policy or effort to target babies specifically. The evidence supports deliberate killing of civilians in their homes, with infants and children among the dead.

**What happened, by the numbers:**  
- **Total dead:** The AFP tally, built from Israeli official data, counts about 1,189 killed on October 7, including about 815 civilians and 36 children.  
- **Infants:** Haaretz's review of the dead found one baby killed that day, 10-month-old Mila Cohen in Nir Oz.

**The specific baby claims:**  
- **"40 beheaded babies":** This came from an i24 reporter quoting soldiers in Kfar Aza. Biden repeated it, and the White House then said he had not seen evidence. No such case has been documented.  
- **Baby in an oven, and a pregnant woman cut open with her fetus killed:** Both came from ZAKA volunteer Yossi Landau. Haaretz and kibbutz records found neither event matched any actual victim.  
- **Kfir Bibas (9 months, taken alive):** He died in captivity. Israel's forensic institute says he and his brother were killed by hand in late 2023. Hamas says an Israeli airstrike killed them. No independent examination exists.

**Independent findings:**  
- **UN Commission of Inquiry (June 2024):** It found armed groups deliberately killed civilians, including children, which is a war crime. It also flagged several widely circulated atrocity claims as false or unverifiable.  
- **Human Rights Watch (July 2024):** It reached the same conclusion on deliberate civilian killings. Fighters fired into safe rooms and burned houses with families inside.

**Grading:**  
- Children, including one infant, were killed as part of deliberate attacks on civilians: highly likely.  
- Babies were singled out as targets: unlikely. Every specific claim to that effect has collapsed under checking.  
- Kfir Bibas was killed by his captors: this rests only on Israel's forensic finding against Hamas's denial, with no independent forensics to settle it.
