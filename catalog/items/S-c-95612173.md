So, another contribution to my efforts to expose the chocked-full-of-shit insanity of this thing we call "ownership".

Just a little math exercise -- not so much to prove the point that ownership is a mental illness, but just to get you clear on what I'm talking about as I demonstrate exactly why it's a mental illness.

Consider the fact that an item of property has been deprived by the owner from 8.2 billion others (minus the owner).

Just take in, as best you can, the unimaginable scope of the ever-present deprivation occurring every fucking second of every fucking day for every single human being on Earth. Even the "rich" are deprived just like everyone else -- deprived of everything they do *not* own -- which lack is exactly what turns them into obsessive, deranged, hoarding fiends.

Nobody grasps numbers in the sextillions. Nobody grasps numbers in the quintillions. Nobody grasps numbers in even the quadrillions. We kind of have a feeble grasp of millions and billions and trillions -- barely. No one has actually seen them. We've seen thousands, though.

Realize how profoundly evil this deprivation on scales we can't even imagine is. Real deprivation of real necessities and desirables from every single person on the planet, all the time. For the sake of what?

For the sake of "security" from absolutely paranoid, hysterical fantasies that everyone wants what we have and anybody, potentially everybody, could take it away from us without notice or a clue.

Fucking bizarre levels of insane, phobic narcissism, in other words.

No. Virtually no one is dying to be you. Almost no one is dying to take what you have. Almost no one even knows you're there -- and most of them don't really care about you one way or the other.

You are not all that, not to anyone. Only in your sick, delusional, fever dreams.
