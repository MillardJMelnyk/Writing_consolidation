Codependence has turned into a big deal for me. I’ve realized that it’s the seedbed for cults, which means it’s the seedbed for every institution and organization that operates in “civilized society”—because “civilization” is none other than the process and product of cultification.

A lot of people think that idea is so broad and all-encompassing, they’re sure I’m off my rocker.

Naw, I’m just following my love for the truth wherever it leads me, just like an honest detective would.

Here’s an easy way to tell which side of the line something or someone falls. The question hinges on the nature of their dependence.

Healthy dependence and interdependence characterize free people.

Codependence characterizes trapped, enslaved people.

There’s no confusing them.
