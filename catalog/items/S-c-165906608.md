Kind off the ReLOVEution topic—except failures of clear thinking are always well in scope—but a couple of memes that show how dumbshit thinking can persist among even our best and brightest for centuries without anyone seeming to notice.

The first is the culmination of a gnarly convo with Grok which I finally got straightened out:

1. NO direct empirical evidence for the HISTORICAL ACTUALITY of macroevolution.
2. Direct empirical evidence we have supports the POSSIBILITY that macroevolution occurred the way we conceive of it.
3. Trenchant conflation of possibility and actuality ever since Darwin’s time.

The second was my eensy “holy shit!” this morning. 😁

ReLOVEution would be the natural, unavoidable result of people simply being honest, thinking clearly, and paying attention to what’s really going on inside them and around them.

Just look at how much work it’s taking me to think it through myself and articulate it!

My friends, we were MASSIVELY mindfucked.

Rabbit holes are the result of trying to dig our way out of the mountains of lies, bullshit, and gaslighting we were buried under. Escape doesn’t come magically or instantly, and there are no magic keys or silver bullets. Just honesty, love of the truth, and a shitload of deconstruction work.

This is why Nietzsche recommended doing philosophy with a hammer. Now, after well more than a century of additional PhDs (pile it higher and deeper) buffaloing us, it’s gonna take a sledge hammer… if not a jackhammer.
