**Mass behavior metaphors:**

* Sheep following the flock over a precipice
* Moths drawn to flame
* Stampeding cattle over a canyon edge
* Fish swimming into nets
* Birds flying into glass windows during migration
* Rats abandoning a sinking ship (reverse - mass exodus from danger)

**Factual correction:** Lemmings don't actually jump off cliffs en masse. This is a Disney-perpetuated myth from a 1958 documentary where filmmakers forced lemmings off cliffs.

**Reasons for falsification:**
