No "MX Master 4S" model exists as of this search — only "MX Master 4" (released Oct 2025) as successor to the 3S. If you meant 4 vs 3S:

* **Actions Ring**: replaces the 3S's side scroll wheel/gesture button with a touch-sensitive ring controlThe Actions Ring, which replaces the physical side scroll wheel found on the 3S.
* **Haptic main scroll wheel**: adds haptic feedback, a change from the 3S's non-haptic MagSpeed wheel.
* **Surface material**: fewer rubber panels, more exposed plastic/PTFE feet it's got fewer rubber elements, strategically placed in the wing and right side; wide PTFE feet give a smoother glide.
* **Size/weight**: MX Master 4 is slightly larger and ~6% heavier than the 3S.
* **Larger buttons/palm rest**: larger click buttons and an expanded palm rest area.

Same sensor (8K DPI), same battery life, same connectivity/multi-device switching per the WorkstationSetup comparison. If you actually meant "4S," that suggests a future refresh — none confirmed as of Aug 2026.

what's the best logitech wireless keyboard?

### Claude · 2026-08-26 21:19

Depends on use case:

* **Office/productivity**: MX Keys S — high-quality wireless keyboard with extra features at a decent price. Standard pick for typing all day.
* **Gaming**: G915 X LIGHTSPEED — full-size wireless, strong for both gaming and everyday use, or the tenkeyless G915 TKL LIGHTSPEED for less desk footprint.
* **Mechanical feel + quiet**: G515 — tactile low-profile mechanical switches, LIGHTSPEED + Bluetooth, minimal noise.
* **Tactile mechanical typing (non-gaming)**: MX Mechanical.

No single "best" — state your use case (typing/office, gaming, or mixed) if you want a single pick instead of a category breakdown.
