Think how many people are in the world.

Think about how many of them you’ll get to know. How many you’ll even get to meet.

Then think how many people in the world you love.

And how many people that you’ll meet from here on out that you’ll love.

And how many of them do you plan to offer love when you first meet them?

How many do you plan to offer respect when you first meet them?

Contemplating those questions will lead you to see what I’m talking about.
