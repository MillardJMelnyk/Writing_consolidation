Yes, institutional cultists (and now will you stop reading, proving that ego matters more than what the fuck is actually going on?) are TERRIBLY afraid of "radicalized" people.

Instead: there's no way they could be right.

Result: you don't need to look.

Because that's the whole point with cultists. THEY DO NOT WANT TO LOOK, AND THEY REFUSE TO LOOK.

Why?

Because looking at all implies some modicum of credibility, and that's something they simply can't allow.

It CANNOT be possible that your wife's "long COVID" was caused by experimental mRNA shots. (And I'm truly sorry that she's suffered.)

Right?

So, what exactly did you do to demonstrate that the mRNA shots were NOT the cause?

Anything at all?
