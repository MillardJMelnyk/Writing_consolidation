I'm tired to death of this idea of "support".

The fact that you have an opinion that agrees or disagrees with an idea, a movement, a group of people, a situation etc., means exactly diddly squat.

The idea that it's important and makes a difference to anything material is all kinds of narcissistic.

No, you don't matter that much, and I don't matter that much.

The fact that you or I have an opinion has no bearing on the questions:

1. Should we have an opinion?

2. Are we qualified to have an opinion?

3. Does our opinion reflect reality or contradict it?

4. Is it smart?

5. Is it crazy?

6. Should other people have the same opinion?

7. If everyone had the same opinion, would that be a good thing or a fiasco?

I could go on and on with questions that everybody seems to take for granted in the mere fact that they present an opinion or "take a stand" or express support.

As if the fact that they did so is about the most important thing a person could do.

Well, I'm here to say that if that's the most important thing you can think of doing, you've done exactly fuckall -- the irony being that you think you've done everything.

And the proof that you think you did everything lies in the fact that you do absofuckinglootely nothing more.
