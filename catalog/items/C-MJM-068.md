What does it mean when my very old dog smells like vinegar?

### Claude · 2025-07-10 16:58

A vinegar-like smell from your elderly dog could indicate several things, and it's worth having your vet take a look since some causes need medical attention.

The most common causes include:

**Ear infections** - These often produce a sour, vinegar-like odor and are more frequent in older dogs. You might notice head shaking, scratching at ears, or discharge.

**Skin conditions** - Bacterial or yeast infections on the skin can create acidic smells. Check for red, irritated areas or excessive scratching.

**Dental issues** - Severe dental disease or infected teeth can sometimes produce sour breath odors that seem to come from the whole dog.

**Kidney problems** - In senior dogs, kidney disease can sometimes cause unusual body odors as toxins build up.

**Diabetes** - Though more commonly associated with a sweet smell, diabetes can occasionally cause other odor changes.

**Anal gland issues** - When these glands become impacted or infected, they can produce strong, unusual smells.

Since your dog is very old, I'd recommend scheduling a vet appointment to rule out any underlying health issues. In the meantime, gently check their ears for discharge or redness, and see if the smell seems stronger from any particular area of their body. This information will help your vet narrow down the cause.
