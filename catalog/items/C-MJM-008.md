Ah, you're absolutely right! He's talking about an electric scooter or hoverboard. At 9 years old, he could be referring to a few different things:

**Electric Scooter**: These have handlebars and you step on the platform, then it moves with electric power. You can control the speed with a throttle. Brands like Razor make good ones for kids.

**Electric Skateboard**: Similar concept but shaped more like a traditional skateboard.

Given his age and the way he described it ("step on them and their wheels start moving"), I'd guess he's probably talking about either a hoverboard or an electric scooter. Hoverboards are really popular with kids that age since they seem almost magical - you just step on and lean to go!

For a 9-year-old, you'd want to make sure whatever you get has appropriate speed limits, good safety features, and definitely get him a helmet and protective gear. Most have weight limits and age recommendations on the packaging.
