Cooperative efforts have always been not just successful, but so successful that they shred both paltry expectations and defeatist resistance.

So why do they eventually fail?

In every case I've learned of, state or corporate programs get initiated deliberately designed to undermine them and bring massive resources to bear to compete them out of existence *precisely because they do work so well, they can't be allowed to continue*.
