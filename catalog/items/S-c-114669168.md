And this is far as I've seen anarchist thinking go.

No concept of a culture which makes it a *practical impossibility* to concentrate power in the hands of the few, only for them to wield it against and at the expense of the many.

Anarchists have all fallen to *cultic capitulation*: the key corruption of sound thinking that flips beliefs resistant to change into cultism.

Here’s how it works: a person, certain that something is true, having no facts or evidence and so blind in their belief that they can’t even tell themself why they believe it, *refuses* to find out if it might be a mistake. Instead, with all the certainty and confidence of someone who already made sure it’s not a mistake, they claim it's true in fact *without ever noticing the circularity*. Indeed, if you try to point out their circularity to them, you instantly become a threat—which is strange, because wouldn’t their certainty and confidence mean (if they were genuine) that their belief is unassailable?

So, instead of thinking systemically, anarchists, too, refuse the possibility of an asshole-resistant world and rest their hopes on *hero saviors*: developing a critical mass of self-thinking individuals that can eventually outweigh and eventually overcome in the perennial fight against evil statists, lol—”eventually” meaning so far ahead that it’s little more than fantasy for all present purposes.

No wonder that anarchism, to date, has amounted to little more than talk.
