It’s not just that the facts blow them away. The fact that you would have to be psychotic to deny the facts is what really blows them away. They embody cult psychosis. It’s the same psycho-social phenomenon you’ll find in every cult, no matter if it’s just a domestic partnership or a family or an organization or identity group or—as everyone now sees—an entire nation.

Don’t expect to reason with a psychotic the way you would a sane person. Facts don’t matter to them. Nor does reason nor sanity itself. Don’t look for admission or confession or even agreement.

You don’t ask psychotics for permission or consent.

That doesn’t mean you have to deal with them inhumanely. No, that’s what they do. They are, in fact, psychologically debilitated. To them, “subhuman” means open season and full license to do whatever the fuck harm they want—and they love it.

We’re not like that.
