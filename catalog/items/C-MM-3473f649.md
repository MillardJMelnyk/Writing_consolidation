### Claude · 2026-05-28 13:49

Yes, Israelis keep pets at similar rates to other developed countries. Dogs and cats are the most popular, with dogs especially common in urban and suburban households. Other common pets include fish, birds, hamsters, and rabbits.

Israel has a well-developed veterinary industry, pet food market, and animal welfare organizations. There are also ongoing debates and legislation around stray cat and dog populations, which are significant in Israeli cities — municipalities manage TNR (trap-neuter-return) programs for strays.
