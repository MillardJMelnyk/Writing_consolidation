Obviously.

But to get out of the loop, you'd first have to credibly envision a world without rulers.

And fucking no one has done that yet, not even the anarchists.

Here's one of the non-optional characteristics of a world without rulers: no ownership.

Not common ownership, shared ownership, gifting, or any of the other alternatives that absolutely do not break out of the ownership paradigm.

No ownership, period.

And before you think it I'll tell you what your next thought will be: how could that work?

Before you have a hope of answering that question, you need to fix the problems in your thinking -- and for that matter in your heart -- because that question isn't rational, it's triggered.

That's why everybody asks it, almost verbatim.

I've been working at this for decades, and that's always the question where people's thinking stops. It's a conditioned reflex, because they just sit there waiting for someone else to give them an answer. And, unless someone does, they idiotically assume there must not be an answer.

The fears that trigger that question need to be resolved before you have a hope and hell of answering that question.
