**$100M Energoatom Corruption Scandal** (November 2025)

On November 10, 2025, Ukraine's National Anti-Corruption Bureau exposed an alleged $110 million kickback scheme at state-owned nuclear company Energoatom. The 15-month investigation ("Operation Midas") included approximately 1,000 hours of wiretaps.

**Key allegations:**
Contractors paid 10-15% bribes totaling ~$100M to maintain government contracts

**Central figures:**

* Timur Mindich (film producer, Zelenskyy's former business partner from Kvartal 95)
* Oleksandr Tsukerman (businessman)
* Herman Halushchenko (Justice Minister)
* Multiple current/former government officials

**Zelenskyy's position:**
Not under investigation, though prosecutors reportedly have recordings of him speaking to implicated Justice Minister Halushchenko during group calls with Mindich. Zelenskyy forced resignations of Halushchenko and Energy Minister Svitlana Hrynchuk, imposed sanctions on Mindich and Tsukerman.

**Political impact:**
Atlantic Council characterizes this as "the biggest corruption scandal of Zelenskyy's presidency". Timing is particularly damaging as Kyiv residents currently face 8-11 hour daily power blackouts from Russian attacks on energy infrastructure.
