What's the advantage of an induction pot as compared to other types of pot

### Claude · 2026-10-25 23:32

Sunday, October 25, 2026, 5:31 PM CST

An induction pot has a magnetic base, so it works on induction cooktops (which won't heat aluminum, copper, or glass pots) and still works on gas and electric stoves. On induction it heats faster and more evenly than a non-induction pot does on a regular burner.

### Millard · 2026-10-25 23:32

How does the power consumption compared to a an electric top stove

### Claude · 2026-10-25 23:32

Induction uses roughly 10–20% less electricity for the same cooking, because about 85–90% of its energy goes into the pot versus about 70–75% for a coil or glass-top electric burner. It also wastes less by heating up and cooling down almost instantly.
