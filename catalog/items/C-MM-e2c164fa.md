I need a refund on my $20 paid yesterday for an anthropic subscription. How do I get it?

### Claude · 2026-06-13 15:54

For Claude.ai subscription refunds, contact Anthropic support directly:

**support.anthropic.com** → submit a billing support ticket

Include your account email and the transaction date/amount. Anthropic handles billing disputes case-by-case; there's no self-serve refund button.

### Millard · 2026-06-13 15:55

Don't tell me to do something that you give me no instructions on how to do. What is a support ticket and how do I submit one properly?

### Claude · 2026-06-13 15:55

Fair point.

1. Go to **https://support.anthropic.com**
2. Click **Submit a request** (or similar button in the top-right)
3. Fill in: your email, subject (e.g. "Refund request - $20 subscription"), and a message with your account email, charge date, and amount
4. Submit — you'll get a confirmation email with a ticket number

That's it. They reply by email, usually within a few business days.
