### Claude · 2026-10-15 13:09

With DNA the interpreter is inside the system (the ribosome reads codons by a fixed code), but entropy has no reader in the system at all; the only "interpretation" is outside it, in choosing which variables count as the macrostate, which is why Jaynes called entropy anthropomorphic and why the Gibbs paradox turns on whether you can tell the gases apart. So entropy is description-relative, not interpreter-relative, though a macrostate the physics itself distinguishes still has to be described, and erasing a bit in it still costs the same energy whoever is watching.
