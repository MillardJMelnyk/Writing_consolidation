The reason people carry on in the irrational, downright psychotic ways they do is thanks to the result of the mindfucking they suffered non-stop throughout their upbringing and "socialization".

What result?

Codependence.

Their existential fears, all of which are in fact imaginary -- rejection, poverty, alienation, loneliness, being despised, being counted worthless, being abused -- make them cling for protection and security and social salvation to the very assholes creating and perpetuating and profiting from the "dangers" that threaten them **in their imaginations**, keeping them chained to the fear-driven merry-go-rounds spinning in their heads.

People don't cling for dear life to ideas and systems and practices and social structures that are irrationally circular and insanely impossible when they're operating intelligently with a sense of competence and nominal safety.

Codependence is how "leaders" lead, tyrants tyrannize, dictators dictate, domestic abusers terrorize, and every other kind of cult ruler from Pope to president to neighborhood gangbanger to family monster maintains control over groups of people, even massive groups, that simultaneously hate them and cling to them, pretending to respect, revere, or even worship them, when the fact is that just a minuscule fraction of those crowds could easily turn them into greasy smears on the sidewalk.

It doesn't matter that the bullshitter at the lectern on the end of the plank hanging out off the edge of the cliff could be made easy, quick work of if their listeners simple stepped off their end of the plank. The fucker knows they won't.

The "power" they pretend to have over us and everyone acts like they have is a lie, a delusion, and a joke. The only actual power in it is what we give it in our delusional imaginations.

In our common psychosis: after the end of rulers, rule, the "rule of law", etc., is "chaos".

"Chaos".

Where in the actual fuck in the actual world do you find actual chaos?

Wherever these mind-fucking, child-fucking, child-eating mutherfuckers get their way with us.

Only then. Only there.

Look as you might elsewhere and you won't find a single shred of chaos.

Chaos is always created and manipulated and exploited by the lords of rule and power.

Always and only.

That makes it really easy to test for codependence.

One of the easiest tests is to look for a person's demand to be ruled. Of course, they don't think of it like that, and they'd never admit that's what it is, because that would be a bad look. But that's exactly what it is.

Take away leaders from their picture of the world and watch them start to shit a cow.

I have a composite image etched into my memory of all people who have looked at me with their deer-in-headlights stares -- at a complete loss once they realized I'm serious and have a point they've got nothing for -- and asked, "But we need leaders don't we? What would we do without leaders?"

There it is, big as life.

All of them: exactly the same eyes, the same expression, the same bewilderment at the mere idea of it.

They'd clearly never once thought about it before.

Well, obviously, we'd do whatever the fuck we wanted.

And that scares the living shit out of them.

Because never in a million years could they see themselves living and surviving and navigatubf a world where there were no "leaders" to make their decisions for them and tell them what to think and say and do. So, never in a million years will they allow a world to even begin to emerge that gives the slightest whiff of anything like that, because they're so fucking sure they know exactly and everything about it -- despite never having any experience with anything remotely like it and, in fact, knowing shitall.

That's their psychosis, which they take refuge in to avoid confronting reality and actually finding out what's really there and what's really going on.

Freedom isn't winning some delusional war against evil, child-fucking psychotics who have created a delusional world and mindfucked the rest of us into believing it's real. There's nothing real to be won in a fight between psychotics who think that violence is power and whose concept of success is domination.

Freedom is to exit the psychosis, swear off and abandon the codependence, and start living as fully human adults with other fully human adults instead of like the little pissant nobodies we see ourselves as.

You know what you find out then?

You don't just realize, you see for a fact that there's nothing real to fight over, and that our clinging itself is the problem -- not the presumed character of the ones we latched onto like we were some kind of sick groupies -- and that these so-called "rich and powerful" people are actually the weakest, stupidest, most deranged, psychopathic, vile-fuck rejects of us all.

And we, the idiots we've been, placed them high above us, over us, as "authorities", even to the point of treating them as lords and gods.

That's the only thing that needs to change, because as soon as it does, all the other problems we've struggled with so desperately, no end in sight, no clear solutions in the offing, will start vaporizing, spontaneously -- because none of that shit happens among sane, competent people when they've realized the guns to their heads are all lightweight plastic shooting air and papier-mâché dripping goop.
