The asininity of Zionism lies in its never-spoken, hidden premise: Jews are perfectly innocent. So, their persecution over thousands of years has been portrayed as perfectly evil.

Of course, "perfectly" is the stuff of myths and fables and children's stories.

When it comes to Jews, the cultism isn't just palpable, it's glaring -- because you are not permitted to ask intelligent, quantifiable questions like:

Is there anything about Jewish thinking, traditions, social practices, etc., that provoked and continues to provoke anti-Jewish hatred?

If so, what's its role into what extent did and does it play a role?

To what extent is anti-Jewish hatred a reflection of and reaction to Jewish hatred of non-Jews?

Ignorant people that don't know how to think rationally will jump to the collusion that these questions are taboo, motivated by victim shaming, because mock empathy always idealizes the victims it pretends to vindicate.

There is no such thing as perfectly innocent in this world.

So, the mere fact that people purport such an absurd contention is proof enough of their cultism.
