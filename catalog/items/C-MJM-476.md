### Claude · 2025-12-17 14:41

**Factual accuracy:**

* True: Freezing a baby would kill it
* True: Freezing embryos (cryopreservation) preserves them
* False/Invalid: The conclusion that this difference proves "an embryo isn't a baby"

**Logical flaw:**

The argument commits a non-sequitur. Differential survival capability under one specific condition (freezing) does not establish categorical identity or moral status. By this logic: an adult human dies at 150°F; thermophilic bacteria thrive at 150°F; therefore humans aren't organisms. The premise doesn't support the conclusion.

**What happens to thawed embryos:**

Standard protocols:

1. **Transfer to uterus** - Most common. Embryo is thawed and implanted as part of IVF
2. **Discarded** - If patient/couple decides not to use them
3. **Donated for research** - If consent was obtained
4. **Donated to another individual/couple** - Less common

Thawing survival rates: ~90-95% for vitrified embryos (flash-frozen), ~60-70% for slow-frozen embryos (older method). Non-viable embryos after thaw are discarded.
