### Claude · 2026-07-13 12:59

Exness is the stronger pick of the two for your specs. Fusion Markets is good too but weaker on one axis. Here's the breakdown as of my last info (Jan 2026 — verify current terms):

**Exness** — accepts Mexican residents, MT5 yes, minimum deposit ~$10 on Standard accounts, gold spreads are among the tightest in the industry (Raw Spread account: near-zero spread + commission), and their withdrawal reputation is genuinely their standout feature — instant/automated withdrawals is their marketing centerpiece and it mostly holds up. Weakness: **no cTrader**. MT4/MT5 and their own terminal only.

**Fusion Markets** — Australian-based (ASIC, plus VFSC offshore entity which is what Mexican residents get), no minimum deposit, very low commissions ($2.25/side), MT4/MT5 **and cTrader** both supported. Withdrawal reputation is clean. Weakness: smaller operation, gold spreads good but not Exness-tier, and support is smaller-team.

So the trade-off: if cTrader is a hard requirement, **Fusion Markets** is your answer — Exness simply doesn't offer it. If MT5 alone is fine, Exness edges it on gold spreads and withdrawal speed.

Also worth a look: **IC Markets** (Seychelles entity for Mexico) — MT5 + cTrader, $200 minimum, raw spreads on gold, established. It's basically the third name that fits every one of your criteria.

Caveats: as a Mexican resident you'll be onboarded under offshore entities (Seychelles/Vanuatu/Curaçao) for all of these, which means less regulatory protection than ASIC/FCA clients get — that's the structural reality, not specific to any one broker. And Pepperstone's suitability quiz rejection isn't income-based per se; you can often retake it answering with more trading experience emphasized.

Verify current spreads and entity terms on their sites — this stuff shifts.
