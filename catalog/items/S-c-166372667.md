Overall, this brings us right back to the "where is the truth" question. It's not in the words on the page. The truth is what those words \*refer to\*. The truth exists only in consciousnesses capable of perceiving what's really going on AND self-aware enough to check their own perceptions and detect disconnections between their perceptions and what their perceptions inform them about. That's why the "word of truth" (as God is truth) is living and active. It doesn't stand still. It stays intimately connected to what's really going on as it happens and as things change. The words on the page are at best a map, not the territory.

As far as the Israelites, I've thought about this now for almost 60 years. (I became a born-again, spirit-filled Christian at 17, but my ponderings on Christ and Christianity began around 12 years old.) The reason God (if it actually was God -- Gnostics would disagree) "chose" Abraham and his descendants was that he couldn't find a more psychopathic group of human beings to work with. If the God of the Jews was every bit the psychotic, genocidal, brutal, bloodshed-loving ghoul that the Jewish writings portray him to be, he was the devil, just like the Gnostics claim. A being that finds the smell of burning feathers, fur, and flesh a "soothing aroma"??? Give me a fucking break. Have you ever smelled that odor? A being that finds that stink soothing isn't anything I'm going to respect, let alone "worship".

Or if, as Christians believe, the God of the Jews was the same being as Jesus' Father, then the OT writings describe what happens when psychopaths interact with God. They simply cannot fathom a god that isn't "all powerful" when their only concept of "power" is \*\*violent might\*\*: the capability to destroy, annihilate, and extort by means of the threat of destruction and annihilation.

Sorry, any "god" that distinguishes itself by superiority in power-as-violence is absolutely not the god of Jesus or of John, who wrote "God is love".

Either way, the devil as god or the god that psychopaths bastardized and corrupted into a genocidal devil, the OT writings are valuable and instructive, but they are in no way a reliable map of the truth, not as is. The ideas in it need to be reverse engineered to be valuable, eliminating the beast mind that originated them and which they reflect.
