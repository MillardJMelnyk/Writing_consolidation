What's a safe dosage of valerian root for pregnant women

### Claude · 2025-12-26 01:47

There is no established safe dosage of valerian root for pregnant women.

**Current medical consensus:** Valerian is generally classified as "likely unsafe" or "insufficient evidence for safety" during pregnancy by major health authorities including the American Pregnancy Association and NIH. No adequate human studies have established safety or appropriate dosing in pregnancy.

**Specific concerns:**

* Potential uterotonic effects (may stimulate uterine contractions)
* Unknown effects on fetal development
* Some animal studies showed developmental toxicity at high doses

**Standard guidance:** Most OB-GYNs and pharmacists recommend avoiding valerian entirely during pregnancy due to insufficient safety data.

If sleep or anxiety is the underlying concern, this should be discussed with the prescribing physician or midwife for pregnancy-appropriate alternatives.
