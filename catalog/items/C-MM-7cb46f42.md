### Millard · 2026-09-18 16:05

### Claude · 2026-09-18 16:05

* "The kids" (affectionate, if they won't read it as talking down)
* "Our kids" (puts you on their side)
* "The younger crowd"
* "The next generation"
* "The ones coming up behind us"
* "Younger people" (plainer, but less fishbowl than "young people")
* "Twenty-somethings" (only if the age range fits)
