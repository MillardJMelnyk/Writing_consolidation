Love is just what naturally happens when you actually see a person for who they are.

Unless you're too psychologically damaged -- seeing a human being for real, unretouched and uncontorted, is to behold someone precious. Appreciation and love, then, are as certain as gravity.

But you can't see them without simultaneously letting yourself be seen for who you really are.

Our shame and self-loathing -- which force us to hide, to project personas, pretend roles, bullshit, and lie -- drive us into a deep, disastrous dishonesty.

That dishonesty is why people feel abysmally alone, abandoned, lost.

They've betrayed and abandoned themselves -- because the truth is: every single person, the real person underneath all the shit and bullshit, is precious, beautiful, and would inspire love in the hearts of everyone who saw them, if only they'd let themselves be seen.

For fear of liars we become liars, and rather than brave the bullshit of the self-condemned, we choose utter isolation instead.

You don't feel lonely when you're seen.

In a world like this one, belligerent honesty is a revolutionary act.
