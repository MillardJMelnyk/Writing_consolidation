### Claude · 2026-09-01 05:06

No — harvesting gives you the crop itself, but you craft the crop back into a seed (1 crop → 1 seed), so a farm sustains itself as long as you don't eat everything.

### Millard · 2026-09-01 05:07

so how do i create a farm if each seed i plant doesnt give back seed son harvest?

### Claude · 2026-09-01 05:07

You expand by converting food into seeds, not by getting seeds back from the harvest.

Each harvest gives you the crop, and the crop crafts back into a seed 1:1 — so one plant just replaces itself. You grow the farm by:

* Buying/looting extra seeds to seed the initial plot
* Crafting every harvested crop into a seed instead of eating it, until the plot is as big as you want
* Only then eating the surplus

Some plants (berry bushes, and a few crops depending on the map/mod) drop more than one item per harvest, which is where actual growth comes from. On vanilla maps, stock up on found seeds early and don't eat your first few harvests.

### Claude · 2026-09-01 05:14

Yes — plant it and come back later.

Crops grow on a timer and then sit there fully grown until you harvest them; they don't rot or die. Just watch out for two things: on multiplayer servers other players can harvest your plants, and unclaimed farms can despawn if you don't visit the area for a while.

### Millard · 2026-09-01 05:17

### Claude · 2026-09-01 05:17

Correct — one crop per plant is vanilla behavior. Harvesting adds the crop to your inventory and the plant disappears. My "Health/2 harvests" line came from the modding documentation: it's a property server owners and modders can configure, not how standard vanilla crops are set up.

What actually gets you more:

* **Agriculture skill.** Without it you get one crop per seed, full stop. Leveling it eventually doubles your output per harvest.
* **Crafting fresh crops into seeds.** One fresh vegetable crafts into 2 seeds. That alone doubles your plot each cycle even at one crop per plant — eat nothing until the farm is the size you want.
* **Rotten crops give only 1 seed.** Craft them while fresh.

Also worth knowing: tap F to harvest, but hold F and you salvage the plant and get only the seed back.
