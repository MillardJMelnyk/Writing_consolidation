### Claude · 2025-09-19 20:46

**Homer's Iliad** is the standard title.

**"The Iliad"** omits the author attribution, which creates ambiguity since:

* Multiple ancient epics exist
* Author identification disambiguates from other works titled "Iliad"
* Academic convention includes author names for ancient texts

**Your assumption**: The definite article "the" makes author name redundant.

**Flaw in your assumption**: Dozens of ancient epics exist. "The Iliad" assumes universal recognition without context.

**Counter**: In casual reference, "the Iliad" is sufficient and more common than "Homer's Iliad."
