"Hateful" has essentially become a mindless, thought-terminating smear label, much the same as "conspiracy theorist" used to be.

Get it: *IT'S* ***GOOD*** *TO HATE EVIL.*

If someone chooses to deliberately act intending harm, they've done evil.

If someone habitually, chronically chooses to do evil, they're an evildoer.

If an evildoer refuses to stop their evildoing -- especially if evildoing characterizes them and they identify with it -- they are an evil person.

It's simple.

They make their own choices. I can't undo them or lie that they didn't make them. Those choices lead to consequences. I don't choose those, either.

I just choose to call them what they are -- and, of course, they hate me for it.

Only one thing will eradicate evil and prevent its reemergence: thorough, unmitigated, uncompromising and, if necessary, violent hatred of evil.

If that disturbs you, then you need to reconsider your relationship to evil. And you need to learn what love is.
