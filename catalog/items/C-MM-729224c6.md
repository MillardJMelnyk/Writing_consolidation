### Claude · 2026-06-24 11:48

No. It's fake, and it's not even attributed correctly — Edward Snowden didn't post this.

The real facts:

* EO 14318 is real, but it was signed July 23, 2025, not June 2, 2026. It's titled "Accelerating Federal Permitting of Data Center Infrastructure."
* It doesn't classify data centers as military installations. It directs the Secretary of Defense to identify suitable sites on existing military installations and competitively lease that land to AI data center developers, as one option among several federal-land provisions. That's "data center built on a military base," not "data center legally becomes a military base."
* The actual goal is accelerating AI infrastructure development by reducing regulatory barriers and deploying federal resources — fast-tracked environmental review, federal land access, financial incentives. States retain authority over zoning, utility approvals, and permitting, contrary to the "no longer apply" claim in the post.

The image you uploaded is a screenshot of a screenshot — it's circulating from a site called "Sunny's Journal," and the same exact text appears credited to an account called "AXIOS4B," not Snowden. It's a conspiracy-blog repost dressed up with a fake "FOR YOUR EYES ONLY / APPROVED" stamp graphic to look leaked. The real EO is a fairly conventional permitting-acceleration order, not a secret reclassification scheme.
