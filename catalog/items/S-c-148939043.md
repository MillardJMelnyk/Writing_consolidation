Stumped me all my life. Not just, “Hmm…” but “WTF???”

How quickly people derail at the slightest poke to their egos, even if they’re discussing something momentously more important than all of them put together amount to.

*Especially* when it comes to the most important problems known to humankind, this proves that their priorities are completely fucked. Their egos are more important to them than babies, children, or innocent men and women starving, being blown into pink mist and ground meat, getting raped on the regular, and struggling under horrendous poverty.

Suggest that there’s something they don’t know, and the whole world vanishes from their heads, everything must stop, and nothing can proceed until their offendedness is assuaged.

So, now, I poke on purpose—because these narcissists don’t belong in a serious discussion in the first place. They’re not there for the sake of the matters under consideration. They’re there serving their egos which, just like kings, popes, and other assorted psychotics, must needs be groveled to and honored—or else, knives out.

And it’s so easy to trigger them into excluding themselves—unless you’re in a crowd of them. In that case, good to know, don’t you think? Just excuse yourself and find people who aren’t such infantiles.
