Otherwise, you’d have to conclude that adversariality is in harmony with basic human psychology, even that it fills a basic human need.

If that were true, then why is it that adversarial interactions, not harmonious interactions, traumatize us and give rise to PTSD?

I go by the rule of beer: Anyone I wouldn’t want to enjoy a beer with is someone who must be wrong in the head. The converse is also true. 😁
