### Claude · 2025-11-21 19:18

Rasmussen Reports is an American polling company founded by Scott Rasmussen in 2003. It conducts public opinion polls on political, business, and lifestyle topics, with particular focus on U.S. presidential approval ratings and election polling.

**Key characteristics:**

* Uses automated telephone polling (interactive voice response/IVR) as its primary methodology
* Publishes a daily Presidential Tracking Poll
* Headquarters in Asbury Park, New Jersey

**Methodological criticisms:**

* Reliance on automated calls excludes cell-phone-only households (historically skewing older/more conservative)
* FiveThirtyEight and other poll aggregators have rated it with a Republican lean in their accuracy assessments
* Scott Rasmussen left the company in 2013; it has operated under different leadership since

**Current status (as of early 2025):**

Still active and publishing daily tracking polls, though its methodological reputation remains contested among polling analysts.

### Claude · 2025-11-21 19:19

**538 (as of March 2024):** Dropped Rasmussen from its polling averages and analysis entirely, stating Rasmussen failed to meet 538's standards for pollsters.

**Historical 538 ratings (pre-exclusion):** Previously rated C+ with noted Republican lean. Before exclusion, Rasmussen was ranked #69 out of 277 pollsters with 2.1 stars in combined accuracy/transparency ratings.

**Silver Bulletin (Nate Silver's current platform):** Rates Rasmussen a "B".

**Accuracy track record by election cycle:**

* **2024:** Rasmussen's final poll showed Trump +3 over Harris; actual result was Trump +1.5. Error: ~1.5 points.
* **2020:** FiveThirtyEight ranked Rasmussen the third most accurate pollster of the 2020 presidential election, with average error of 2.8 points for final 21-day polls.
* **2018:** Harry Enten called Rasmussen the least accurate pollster in the 2018 midterms—projected Republicans +1, actual result Democrats +8.6 (error ~10 points).
* **2012:** FiveThirtyEight ranked Rasmussen 20th out of 23 pollsters, average error 4.2 points.
* **2010:** Nate Silver concluded Rasmussen was the least accurate major pollster, with 5.8-point average error and 3.9-point pro-Republican bias.

**Bottom line:** Performance is inconsistent across cycles. Excluded from 538's averages since March 2024; still rated middling-to-acceptable by Silver Bulletin. Documented Republican lean in most assessments.
