### Claude · 2026-10-19 18:07

Nick Hanauer wrote it (Politico, 2014) to warn his fellow ultra-rich that runaway inequality would end in revolt against them unless they backed higher wages for the middle class, which he argued are the real job creators, not the rich.

### Claude · 2026-10-19 18:10

Highly likely connected: Hanauer and labor leader David Rolf kicked off the 2013 SeaTac drive that produced the country's first $15 minimum wage law, and in 2014 he sat on Seattle's Income Inequality Advisory Group, which proposed the $15 ordinance the city passed. The link is his advocacy, not the article itself, which ran around the time the ordinance passed.
