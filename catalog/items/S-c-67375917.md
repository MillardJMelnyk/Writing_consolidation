Once people leave codependency behind, they have no more use for "beliefs". "I think" is strong enough, because *they* are strong enough not to need to exaggerate their ideas beyond "think", personalize them, identify with them, evangelize them or coax or cajole or trick or seduce or extort others into accepting them, use them as weapons, defend with them, aggress with them, hide behind them like bricks in a wall, or barricade themselves inside them like a fortress. They don’t do *anything at all* to make what they think look more formidable than it already is, *because they're honest*.

Real power doesn't lie in what we think, nor in what we claim about what we think, nor in how we characterize it. That's just bluster and bullshit.

Real power lies in *aligning what we think with what's really going on*.

The power isn't in our thoughts, but in our *relationships* to the universe and everything in it.

The truth is for all, available to all (except when mutherfuckers secret it away and hide it from everyone else), and *everyone* has a right to know what it is -- not just some chosen few Very Special People who lie that only they rightly have privileged access to it.

Well, I guess maybe that's not technically a lie, because they've made sure that everyone thinks they are VSPs, they've made sure that everyone thinks they are privileged, and they've made sure that no one but themselves can get access to the information they've deprived everyone else of.

Maybe in that particular point they're not liars -- just assholes.
