### Claude · 2026-09-26 00:18

Mostly false. Only the move to Australia is real.

**Name change to "Covinda Pfizer Ardern":** Highly unlikely. It's satire or a meme. She is still reported as Dame Jacinda Ardern, and she never worked for Pfizer.

**Fled to Australia in fear for her life:** Unlikely. She did move to Sydney in February 2026, but it was from the US, not from New Zealand. She had been at Harvard, and her spokesperson said they were "basing themselves out of Australia" because "they have work there, and it brings the added bonus of more time back home in New Zealand." No report I found ties the move to threats.

**64,000 injuries (CARM):** Likely the wrong reading of a real number. CARM received tens of thousands of voluntary adverse-event reports, which is roughly that range. A report means someone suspected a link. It is not a confirmed injury.

**ACC reports 5 deaths:** Likely close to real. ACC accepted a small number of vaccine-injury death claims. That is a real but small figure.

**Barry Young's data shows 10,000+ excess deaths caused by the shots:** Highly unlikely. Young was a Te Whatu Ora database worker who leaked vaccination-and-death records in 2023, and he was later convicted over the leak. Health authorities and independent statisticians said the data can't show causation, because older and sicker people were vaccinated first and naturally die at higher rates. That's the core flaw. NZ's excess-death spikes also track with COVID waves, not with vaccine rollout dates.
