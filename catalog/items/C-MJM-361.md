### Millard · 2025-11-26 13:10

what's currently the highest rated AI chatbot for accuracy and truth-telling?

### Claude · 2025-11-26 13:10

Perplexity AI achieved 91% accuracy with 96% relevant source citations when tested against 500 fact-checkable claims (as of June 2025 testing). Multiple sources position Perplexity as "unmatched" for verifiable information, emphasizing its real-time fact-checking and citation transparency.

Google Bard/Gemini and Perplexity are both noted as focusing "heavily on factual accuracy and sourcing", with Bard's strength specifically cited as accuracy over creativity.

No single authoritative "accuracy and truth-telling" ranking exists across all chatbots as of November 2025—evaluations vary by methodology and tested domains.

### Millard · 2025-11-26 13:11

how does Claude rate?

### Claude · 2025-11-26 13:12

Claude achieved an 87% success rate on complex coding challenges in head-to-head testing (June 2025 data). Claude "puts ethics at the front" and "tries hard not to mislead—useful if you're sharing output in sensitive environments".

Claude is noted for breaking down problems methodically, showing its work, and explaining logic rather than "jumping to conclusions", and for superior performance on "complex, multi-step problems" and document analysis.

The sources don't provide a direct accuracy percentage for Claude comparable to Perplexity's 91%. Evaluations emphasize Claude's strength in reasoning depth, coding, ethical filtering, and analytical tasks rather than speed or citation-based fact retrieval.

The term "ethical filtering" refers to this screening mechanism—refusing or modifying responses to requests deemed risky. The sources don't specify what qualifies as "risky" or detail the filtering methodology.

### Millard · 2025-11-26 13:15

what's the word on Perplexity's Comet browser?

### Claude · 2025-11-26 13:15

Released July 9, 2025 for Windows/Mac, November 20, 2025 for Android. Initially limited to $200/month Max tier subscribers, became free in October 2025.

**Security concerns:** Brave discovered prompt injection vulnerability allowing attackers to access user emails via hidden webpage instructions; Perplexity acknowledged and implemented initial fix July 27, 2025. LayerX Security identified "CometJacking" exploit that could exfiltrate personal sensitive data to attacker-controlled servers, disclosed August 2025.

**Performance issues:** AI-driven actions like shopping cart automation often fail or are slower than manual browsing, voice mode underperforms, browser consumes significant system resources when AI features active, Perplexity-based search engine sometimes lags behind Google in result relevance.

**Reception:** Digital Trends notes it "occasionally fails at fundamentally simpler tasks" including refusing to bookmark pages despite having the feature. User adoption mixed—some praise automation convenience, others flag bugs and privacy concerns.

### Millard · 2025-11-26 13:17

where does it get its search data from?

### Claude · 2025-11-26 13:17

Perplexity "searches the web live" rather than relying on a static training dataset, pulling from academic publications, news outlets, or trusted websites.

Perplexity states it "prioritizes trustworthiness by sourcing its information from a select group of reputable sources rather than relying on an index as expansive as Google's"—meaning a smaller, curated pool compared to Google's index.

The sources don't specify the technical mechanism (proprietary web crawler, licensed search API, or other method). Perplexity has faced lawsuits from Forbes, The New York Times, and Dow Jones alleging copyright infringement and unauthorized content scraping, suggesting direct web crawling.

what are its "select group" of sources?

### Claude · 2025-11-26 13:19

Perplexity maintains a manually curated list of reputable domains, but "the full list of manually approved domains isn't published".

**Citation frequency data (June 2025):**
Top domains by mention share in Perplexity: Wikipedia (12.5%), YouTube (16.1%). Reddit and Quora don't crack the top 10 in Perplexity (unlike Google AI Overviews).

**Industry-specific examples:**
Healthcare: mayoclinic.org, nih.gov, medlineplus.gov. Education: coursera.com. Travel: booking.com and Airbnb. Developer content: GitHub.

To identify favored domains in a specific niche, search Perplexity for topics in that space and note which domains it frequently references.

Perplexity has not disclosed selection criteria or the complete approved list.

### Millard · 2025-11-26 13:20

how does it rate for cultural/political objectivity, or does it have a bias?

### Claude · 2025-11-26 13:20

Comparative analysis using Pew Research Center's Political Typology Quiz, Political Compass assessment, and ISideWith quiz found Perplexity exhibits conservative bias, while ChatGPT-4 and Claude exhibit liberal bias, and Google Gemini adopts centrist stances (July 2024 study).

Perplexity uniquely displayed "Libertarian Capitalism" ideology—economically conservative with socially permissive positions.

**Controversy:** User claims Perplexity "stopped using right-wing websites in their sources" after receiving funding, blacklisting far-right sites but not far-left sites (unverified claim from February 2025 forum discussion). Truth Social's chatbot relies on Perplexity for its answer engine.

The study noted biases stem from training datasets and underscore "the critical need for transparency in AI development and the incorporation of diverse training datasets".
