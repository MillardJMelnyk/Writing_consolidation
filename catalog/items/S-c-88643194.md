This is the primary sociological trait of our times.

Dig into any important discussion you like. You will find that all involved are talking from *knowing-about*, not *knowing-having-done* -- whether it's their own having done or someone else's.

How do you tell?

The same way a football player can tell if you've ever dribbled a ball downfield (or for you Yanks, set foot on a gridiron) as opposed to merely having read about it; or a skydiver can tell if you've ever actually jumped out of a perfectly good airplane; or a sailor can tell if you've ever lived at sea; or someone who has known love can tell if you have or not, even if just once.

It's easy.

When everything said could have been said by someone who ared a book about it, and nothing gets said that shows they have a fucking idea what it is to do it, experience it -- you've got an entire discussion that has been disconnected from reality.

And that’s the primary indicator of psychosis.
