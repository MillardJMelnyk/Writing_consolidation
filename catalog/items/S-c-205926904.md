Good explanation of an aspect of the nature of information and its dynamics.

This is why the truth is so much more powerful than lies. It's freaking blatant once you look at the amount of energy and resources required to maintain lies over a period of time compared to the investment needed for honesty.

I didn't watch the whole thing so I don't know if he mentioned it, but the greatest expenditures required for each, lying or honesty, are psychological. And no one talks about them.

In that respect, honesty crosses the finish line before lying even gets to the first turn.

We're designed for honesty. To lie, you must set up an inner war between compartmentalized areas of your own psyche. Not only are you doomed to lose that war -- because both sides are you -- you're doomed to burn your very life energy just to stage it.

And the part that I have no doubt he fails to mention is that all this lying done by the other guys could not have been sustained, not in a million years, without our complicity.

You can't con an honest person.

The escape from the madness of this last 12,000 to 15,000 years does not critically depend on stopping the liars.

It depends solely on us stopping ourselves from being liars.

As soon as you become honest, lies lose their power over you. And so, liars can't get what they want from you. So, suddenly, their return on investment goes negative. They're burning all their own energy and resources to maintain a campaign that not only fails to get them what they want -- it guarantees they'll get exactly the opposite.

There are very few people on Earth who are so psychotic that they'll press on regardless at that point. Even the most psychotic -- on the level of the Hitlers and Stalins and Maos and Pol Pots -- don't keep going once they've become convinced their chances of succeeding are zero-to-negative.

No, when they're convinced it's over, they abandon their lying and self-destruct, taking as many with them as possible -- just like the devil does, just like Israel has sworn it will do.

It's funny when people ask me the "how" question. It means they've capitulated. They know I'm making sense. They also know that they want what I'm making sense about.

But it's beyond them. It's beyond their ability to take it credibly, so they make a liar's move and pretend it's all about feasibility. And then they expect me, like I were their teacher or an expert, to tell them, like I would green school kids or idiots, how we could ever go about achieving a world like that.

Fucking liars.

Every person who has ever honestly decided to get what they want, committed themselves to getting it, knows the answer to that question:

Any fucking way we can.

"How" is no impediment to those who have decided they will.

"How" is the smarmy, moronic excuse of people who refuse but aren't honest enough to admit it. So, they wrap themselves with cloaks of their own ignorance, incompetence, and feebleness as though those honestly make them look innocently at a loss -- when they fucking know they have no intention of being otherwise.

Everything starts getting so incredibly clear once you go through that strait gate onto the narrow way that almost no one wants to take. It's strait because we go through alone, one at a time, without regard to what anyone else does. It's narrow because truth is singular. The way to destruction is broad because lies and bullshit are aplenty.

None of this is complicated.

Complicated to explain to people addicted to lying, sure.

Complicated to experience, to live out?

There is nothing simpler than to honestly be who you fucking are, to say what you fucking see and know, and to learn from what fucking happens next.
