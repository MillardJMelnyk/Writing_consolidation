### Claude · 2026-08-19 19:00

It's antisemitic propaganda, and the structure of it is the giveaway.

Three things wrong with it:

**The quote.** Clips like this circulate stripped of source and context. The line about gentiles being annihilated traces to Kabbalistic and eschatological texts about the messianic era — where the thing "annihilated" in most readings is idolatry and the nations as political-spiritual categories, not people. Whoever posted it gives you no rabbi, no date, no full audio. That's deliberate.

**The leap.** Even granting some rabbi said exactly that: going from one man in one synagogue to "this is what rabbinic Judaism as a whole believes and aspires to" is the entire trick. Judaism has no central magisterium, no pope, no binding creed on this. Mainstream halakha holds gentiles who keep the Noahide laws have a share in the world to come. Chabad specifically runs a global Noahide outreach campaign *to* non-Jews — hard to square with wanting them exterminated.

**The frame.** "Behind the wars, behind the refugees, behind the conflicts — they are." That's the Protocols of the Elders of Zion, near verbatim in structure: hidden Jewish hand orchestrating world events, profiting from blood, claiming a master race, weaponizing "antisemite" to silence critics. It's been the load-bearing text of European antisemitism for over a century, and it was a proven forgery by 1921. "Religion of the Antichrist" is the medieval supersessionist capstone.

You can criticize Israeli policy, Chabad theology, or any rabbi's specific words on the merits. This post isn't doing that. It's using a decontextualized fragment as a pretext for a claim about an entire people.
