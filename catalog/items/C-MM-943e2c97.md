### Claude · 2026-09-28 20:14

As exact spellings, "fotball" is only Norwegian and "futball" isn't used by any of these languages. Were you counting the fot- vs. fut- beginnings instead? If so, fot- has 5 (fotball, fotboll, Czech and Romanian fotbal, fótbolti). Fut- has about 14 (futbol ×8 counting Cyrillic, fútbol, futebol, futbal, futbols, futbolas, futboll), so fut- is well ahead.
