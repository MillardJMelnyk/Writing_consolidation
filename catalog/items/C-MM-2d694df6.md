### Millard · 2026-09-29 14:43

how to stop a pomeranian from groweling when he feels like he has done something wrong

### Claude · 2026-09-29 14:43

Stop scolding him, looming over him, or confronting him after the fact. That growl is fear, not guilt, and punishing it only teaches him to skip the warning and bite. Instead, calmly interrupt the behavior while it's happening, redirect him, and reward him when he relaxes.

### Millard · 2026-09-29 14:43

really, does that work?

### Claude · 2026-09-29 14:43

Likely, yes, if you're consistent for a few weeks. Research on the "guilty look" (Horowitz, 2009) found dogs show it in response to the owner's scolding tone, not because they know they did wrong, so removing the scolding removes what triggers the growl.
