SHOCKING:

This woman was nearly killed in a hospital for being unvaccinated.

Gail Seiler got Covid in 2021.

When doctors discovered she was unvaccinated, they told her:

“You’re going to die.”

She was denied food for 12 days.

She was denied water for 7 days.

Doctors refused her requests for ivermectin, hydroxychloroquine, budesonide, zinc, and vitamin C.

“I faced aggressive pressure to take remdesivir, escalating oxygen protocols, and repeated pressure toward ventilation.”

“I was isolated from my family.”

“I was denied access to my Catholic priest and my sacraments until I received remdesivir.”

“I was designated a DNR, or Do Not Resuscitate.”

“I experienced physical, verbal, and psychological abuse at the hands of some of the nurses because I was not vaccinated.”

“I was left naked and uncovered for extended periods of time while staff observed me through the ICU windows.”

“My family was repeatedly told that I was unsavabale and needed to be allowed to die.”

Getting her out took a six-hour police standoff.

Her husband Brad, a former ER nurse and Army captain, walked into the ICU to take her home.

“The hospital called the police, resulting in a six-hour standoff, as officers tried to persuade him to leave.”

It took an intervention from a Colonel and a State Senator to rescue her.

“With the intervention of Colonel Allen West and Texas Senator Bob Hall and our attorney, we were finally allowed to leave.”

“Doctors insisted that my lungs were permanently damaged and that I would not survive outside their care.”

“They were wrong.”

Treated at home with the drugs the hospital refused her, Gail fully recovered.

“I encourage our government to fully investigate what happened to patients and families during the Covid response so that no American family will ever experience these abuses again.”
