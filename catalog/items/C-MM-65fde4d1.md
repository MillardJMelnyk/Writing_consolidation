### Millard · 2026-09-21 01:05

how to play multiplayer with girlfriend on zumbi blocks 2?

### Claude · 2026-09-21 01:06

Both of you need the Steam build (Zumbi Blocks 2 Open Alpha, free) and each other on Steam Friends — then one of you hosts a multiplayer game and the other joins via the Steam friends list or by pasting the host's lobby code. Steam friends-list joining and lobby codes replaced the old IP/port method, so no Hamachi or Radmin VPN needed.

The menu wording I have is from the 2023 build (host: Start Multiplayer, joiner: Connect to Game) — highly likely still close, but I won't hand you labels I can't confirm for the current version. Send me a screenshot of the main menu and I'll give you the exact clicks.

### Claude · 2026-09-21 01:45
