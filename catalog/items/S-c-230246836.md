There's someone lately who keeps barking, "BULLSHIT!" in response to my Israel-related posts. He especially doesn't like anyone stating that Netan-fuck-the-yahoo is dead. It's pretty funny.

Since he obviously doesn't have a micro-clue what bullshit actually is, I thought it might be a good time to review for everyone else who has no more clue than he does.

"Bullshit" is basically meaningless when most people upchuck it. And it smells the same. They aren't emitting anything more meaningful than projectile vomit tripped by a gag reflex triggered by the mere smell of information that thoroughly contradicts their cling-to-death-for-survival beliefs. That's exactly what they're doing, and that's exactly how much thought they did NOT put into it, resulting in how void their words and behavior are of anything that could be called intelligence.

I don't use "bullshit" that way except after a few Tequila shots, shooting the shit with family or friends. Never in my writing or posts.

When I say "Bullshit!" I mean something quite clear, definite, and well-defined. And, by the time I use it, you can be sure that I've got plenty of reason to use it and plenty of evidence to support my use of it -- far more than the bullshitter has, and usually consisting of things that have never even crossed their mind.

So, what's bullshit, then?

Bullshit is shit you say intending it to be taken as true but for which you have done SHITALL to make sure whether it's true or not.

So, there's a difference between lies and bullshit.

Liars know what the truth is and they say something else.

Bullshitters don't know what the truth is, don't give a fuck what the truth is, but they say whatever the fuck serves their interests at the moment. And then, next moment, something else. It might even contradict and disprove what they said the moment before. They don't give a shit.

No interest. No care. No attention. You're trying to relate what they said to reality. **That** is exactly what they want. **That** is exactly what they need to use against you.

Like seducers leverage your care and need for love, bullshitters leverage your care and need for the truth.

People make a huge mistake when confronted by bullshit. They treat it like they would a potential lie.

You determine if something is a lie or not by digging into the facts being claimed. If the purported facts are false, not facts at all, you can say the claim is false. You still can't say it's a lie yet, though. That requires the person to have knowledge of the facts and intentionally distort them.

Whether truth-teller or liar, the same assumption applies to both: they're both acquainted with the actual state of the facts. The sin of lying isn't in ignoring the facts, but choosing to misrepresent them.

You can't make that assumption about bullshitters, though. Bullshitters have no intention of acquainting themselves with the facts. No interest. No care. No attention. No trail from what they say back to the facts that support it.

But wait a minute! Isn't it that very trail that establishes whether something is factual or a misrepresentation?

Exactly. The bullshitter doesn't just not know what the facts are, they have no way of knowing what they are, because they never walked that trail. Never even set foot on it. It's irrelevant to what they're doing. They've completely cut themselves and what they say off from reality as far as their bullshit is concerned.

So, not only are they not accountable, "accountability" is meaningless in this context.

This is why bullshitting is more psychotic than lying (when you understand psychosis as a state of mind that's been completely cut off from reality, in whole or in part.)

And that's why bullshit is way easier to deal with than lying is.

You don't detect bullshit by means of facts. That's the loser's game the bullshitter actually wants to entrap you in, because they want to mire and drown you in it.

The bullshitter doesn't give a flying fuck about the facts one way or the other -- so besides your efforts to disprove their claims being incredibly work-intensive, they've got zero motivation to listen to you go on and on about how counterfactual their claims are. Couldn't care less.

Your focus on facts is exactly the rope they wind around your neck and then jerk, putting you into a spin like some kind of meatsack humming top -- except that, in your case, you'll end up screaming as they switch goalposts on you every time you manage to come close to undeniably undermining what they said.

That's frustrating as hell, so don't even go there.

Here's all you have to do:

Simply ask them WHAT THEY DID to come to that conclusion.

Past tense, point of time and location. Who were they with, what happened, how did it affect them? How did it make them feel? Not "why". WHAT AND HOW.

Notice that this has *nothing* to do with the truth of what they said or the facts involved. It's all about what they did and what happened.

What you're doing is asking them to divulge what basis, if any, they have for saying anything the fuck at all.

Then lean back and watch their eyes go blank.
