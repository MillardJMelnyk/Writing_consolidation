### Claude · 2026-08-02 20:02

Ephesians 4:6 — "one God and Father of all, who is above all, and through all, and in you all" (KJV) / "in all" (other translations).

Colossians 3:11 — "Christ is all, and in all."

Colossians 1:16-17 — "For by him were all things created... and by him all things consist" (creation/sustaining framing, not identical "in all" language but often cited alongside).

1 Corinthians 15:28 — "that God may be all in all."

Acts 17:28 — "For in him we live, and move, and have our being" (Paul quoting Greek poets, addressed to Athenians, not a direct "God in all things" doctrinal statement).

Most directly matching your description: Ephesians 4:6 and 1 Corinthians 15:28.
