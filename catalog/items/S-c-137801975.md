Oh but no! That's not possible! That's not feasible! That's not viable! Human nature won't permit it! Human stupidity will prevent it! I'll lose all the winnings I've gained! It will create a vacuum that only worse monsters will fill! Please, please! Don't drain our cesspool! It sucks and it stinks, but I've filtered myself out a little eddy where the chunks aren't so big! I've devised a whole system that keeps the chunks small and skims out most of the sludge! I've already got the patent! Next year we'll go public! It's my life's work! How can I throw all that away? And what am I going to do on clean, solid earth? It will be every man for himself! They'll all have good traction with no muck to slow them down, and I don't know how to run! It will be chaos! It's terrifying! Who will lead us? Who will keep us safe? Who will take care of us like children and wipe our asses when we shit ourselves? NOOOoooooooooo!!!
