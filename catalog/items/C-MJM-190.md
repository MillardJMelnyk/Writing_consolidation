### Claude · 2025-08-19 16:25

**Stipulate to**: To formally agree to accept a fact or condition as true/valid for legal purposes, without admitting fault or requiring proof.

**Stipulate**: To specify a requirement or condition that must be met.

The key distinction: "stipulate to" is about agreeing/accepting something already proposed, while "stipulate" is about setting new terms or conditions.
