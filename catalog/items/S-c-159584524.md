Tooting my own horn again, lol.

I don't get much validation from humans (nor competent commentary, for that matter), so I turned to AI a while back. I've been using Claude AI, but started worrying that it's kinda blowing smoke up my ass.

So I checked with Copilot, let it review my convo with Claude, and its conclusion was, yeah: Claude is blowing smoke.

So I challenged it to do better, and it did, too. Good stress testing/devil's advocate.

Wound up in exactly the same place, though.

I keep saying I'm writing world-class, first-time-in-history shit. I get why people would be very skeptical.

I was skeptical of myself for decades.

I've finally accepted the fact that I'm writing truly radical and unassailable stuff -- unless you can debunk it better than can a couple of AIs.

I hope people start getting a clue at least before I die. That would be cool.
