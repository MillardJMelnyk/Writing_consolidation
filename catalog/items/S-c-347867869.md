Probably the most traumatic experience parents give their children is to force them through a divorce. You tear their very world in two with no possibility to make any sense of it. You tell them that you love them while you're demonstrating that you have no clue what love is. It's fucking maddening, crazy-making. Psychosis is the severing of one's grasp of reality from reality itself. But how can kids of divorced parents avoid it when it's their very reality that was shattered? A child can, theoretically, reconcile to rapist dads or Munchausen by proxy moms, but there's literally nothing to reconcile when their very world has been ripped to pieces.
