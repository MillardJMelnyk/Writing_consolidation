When it comes to government, ignorance and stupidity do not constitute innocence, ever. Same for corporations.

If they do it, they want to do exactly that and not something else.

So, we have to conclude that they do not want the most beneficial, efficient, effective solutions.

They want the opposite -- just not quite so opposite that it triggers backlash, but just enough that they can pretend they're "doing something about it".

A bit like constructing a beautiful bedside stand to put tissue on for your child's runny nose when they're dying of pneumonia.
