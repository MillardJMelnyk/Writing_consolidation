Toby, I hear you, but a new metaphysics won't help. The problem isn't intellectual or abstract. It's psychological. It turns out that people really need something to worship. There is where we need to dig. It turns out that's true -- but only for a certain, very distinct and easily identifiable type of people: codependents. Fix the codependency problem and the rest will come naturally, of its own. People really need something to worship because they're debilitated. Fully human people who make their own terms, form their own understandings, define themselves, and live accordingly don't need something to worship, because they experience life and themselves as integral to the whole. Not just talk about it, experience it, in what Csikszentmihalyi described as "flow". Living itself becomes their "form of worship, not some artificial, ritualized human contrivance, not some more-than-natural experience or state (although I'm by no means knocking the latter, they're awesome.)

We were all fucked in head and heart 25 ways from Sunday throughout our development and maturation, stymieing both, and then we try to "fill the hole" and find answers that will get us what we want while we remain fucked. Not gonna happen. Gotta unfuck first.
