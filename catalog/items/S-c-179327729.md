If you don't know this, then start learning.

I'm glad someone who has spent their entire career studying the question said it. I've been certain about it for a very long time, but who am I?

Every single fucking one of us has genius.

There has been lots said over the last while about the corruption involved in modern-day "education".

Well, no, that's all wrong.

What we've called education is a deliberate program to **severely** dumb us down.

That can be thought of as "corruption" only under the delusion that it was originally intended to do something else.

This is not the least bit controversial to those who have honestly looked into the history and nature of modern education. So, take stock and realize what that says about you if you find it controversial.

Land's work nailed shut the coffin on our delusions about "education". You cannot deny the results:

* 5-year-olds: 98% genius level
* Same children at 10 years: 30% still at genius level
* At 15 years: only 12%
* Adults (280,000 tested): 2% at genius level

It doesn't matter what you say. Say-so doesn't make so. You'll know the tree by its fruit, and the tree of modern "education" is ***POISONOUS***.
