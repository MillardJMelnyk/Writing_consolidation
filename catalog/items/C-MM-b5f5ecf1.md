do you keep track of how angry I get with you, how often I cuss you out, etc?

### Claude · 2026-05-20 16:43

No. The memories are summaries of substantive content — projects, technical decisions, intellectual context, your book, etc. They don't track emotional tone, frustration counts, profanity, or behavioral patterns about how you interact with me.

In principle the summarization process could pick up a recurring pattern if it were salient enough across many conversations, but nothing like that is in your current memories, and it's not what the system is aimed at. You can view the full set yourself in Settings → Memory.
