Build Quality
-------------

| Spec | BoBo Home 2.0 | GoBalance Sport |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material | Plywood with grip tape | Wood with anti-slip surface + removable TPE yoga mat |
| Diameter | 15" (14" per some sources) | 18" wide |
| Weight capacity | 450 lbs | 450 lbs |
| Board weight | ~3 lbs | 4.8 lbs |
| Balance element | Inflatable cushion | Inflatable cushion (adjustable) |
| Price | $279-299 | ~$119 |

GoBalance Sport is described as "premium" and "crafted from high-quality wood" with "exceptional build quality." BoBo's board is described as "a plywood board with grip tape elements on both sides."

**Build assessment:** GoBalance Sport offers a larger platform (18" vs 15"), heavier construction (4.8 vs 3 lbs), and includes the yoga mat layer for planks—at roughly 40% of BoBo's price. BoBo has been on market longer (founded 2015), potentially indicating more refinement, but construction descriptions suggest comparable materials.

Game Fun Factor
---------------

| Feature | BoBo | GoBalance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Game count | 14 games | 12 games + 4 sports modes |
| Game types | Mini golf, car racing, mazes, submarine | Snowboard, spaceship, car racing, figure-8s, Space Ball, Ice Adventure |

**BoBo app criticisms (App Store reviews):**
"The games are ok and on-screen balance monitor is good—so it is still useful. But I really wish Bobo would make the training stats more useful and meaningful."
"The games become boring about a minute into gameplay for every single game."
"The stats are largely, useless. Dates incorrect with number codes that make no sense."

**GoBalance positive notes:**
Reviewer "personally loved the snowboard one. Space Ball is good to work on all the positions on the board."
"Suddenly, you're not just exercising—you're controlling a spaceship with your feet. You're racing cars. You're making figure-8s or snowboarding without ever leaving your living room rug."

**Game assessment:** Both have similar game mechanics (tilt-controlled). BoBo has more games but documented complaints about repetitiveness and buggy stats. GoBalance is newer (app ~2024), fewer long-term user complaints found, but also less independent review data available.

Summary
-------

GoBalance Sport appears to offer better value on hardware (larger, heavier, cheaper) with comparable game functionality. BoBo has longer market presence and professional/clinical adoption but documented app quality issues. Neither has substantial independent comparative testing available.
