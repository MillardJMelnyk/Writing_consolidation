If you'll think it through and see what kind of factual basis decriers have for their accusations of CONSPIRACY THEORY! and then look at the factual basis for the ideas they claim are "conspiracy theories", you'll realize, no question, that the real conspiracy theorists are the ones shouting it.

They ain't got dickall to support their accusations -- nor do they feel like they need more than dickall.

The simple fact that information and theories formed to explain it lie far outside their dogmatic blinders is all it takes for them to brand both facts and theories as ridiculous and the people presenting them as idiots.

But who are the idiots, really: the people who looked at and thought about things that their decriers **refuse to look at** under the dumb pretense that all such is delusory, or the decriers who refuse to look and refuse to think by claiming *don't-see-so-can't-be* as their excuse?

With no evidence that the information and theories are incorrect, and with no interest to even understand how the information actually arose or how the theories were actually formed, they just dismiss the whole train without having ridden it an inch.

Does that sound intelligent to you?

Does that sound like the kind of people who want to know the truth?

Or does that sound more like people suffering from diagnosable paranoid delusions which the slightest bit of fact could topple so, to avoid the crash (which is inevitable anyway), they play ostrich?
