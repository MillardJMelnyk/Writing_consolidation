What the fuck is wrong with everybody?

The reason you see this as a fight is because of the mindfuck done to you by the very same psychotic "powers" you pretend you're going to fight.

You're fighting for control of a slave-powered train designed and created and controlled by people you fully believe are powerful and, increasingly, realize are evil.

It's a joke.

The whole system runs on slavery and extortion, so the whole system is an accurate expression of the psychosis we've long called "power", and you want to fight over it because, in your mind, you're existentially dependent on it.

First, how could you ever hope to win a fight when you're so mindfucked as to be oblivious that the fight itself is part of the psychosis?

And even if you win this "fight", what are you going to do with the slave-run extortion train? Keep it going with you at the controls?

This is why Paul the apostle wrote that our fight is not against flesh and blood. It doesn't matter if you win a fight against flesh and blood when both you and those you conquered continue psycho-spiritually locked under exactly the same psychosis.

It doesn't matter who controls the slave extortion train. What matters is that you refuse to get off it.

And you have no fucking clue in hell how to get off it, what to do and where to go if you got off it, and no concept or confidence that you could survive if you did get off it.

So, you have absolutely no intention of getting off it.

And that's why you fight over it. And that's why the fight is phony, ridiculous, and insane.

But it's all you know. And you use that as your excuse, too. Even though you know for a fucking fact the corrective for your ignorance, you refuse it. You absolutely refuse it.

You'd rather cling to the dubious innocence of ignorance and die than rectify your stupidity and live.

I guess you'll have to mount this fight, win this fight, and realize that you accomplished absolutely nothing positive before, maybe, you'll stop clinging to your psychosis.
