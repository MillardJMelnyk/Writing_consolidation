### Claude · 2026-09-09 18:32

Two weeks in, it's crushing Iran's economy but hasn't changed Tehran's behavior.

* Rial at ~2.2 million/dollar, inflation estimated over 80%; it was ~2.05m on launch day, against an official rate near 1.5m.
* Seven straight weeks with no meaningful crude out of Hormuz — a first — with the naval blockade doing most of the work, not the paperwork.
* China, which took 80%+ of Iranian crude, has switched suppliers, a shift that may stick even after the war ends.
* EU signed on Aug 31; UAE had already suspended all trade with Iran.
* Iran's central bank governor says it will have "no impact"; no ceasefire deal, fighting resumed.

For you: the squeeze is feeding gold. WTI settled $91.48 on Sept 1, up 5.1% on renewed US-Iran fighting — headline risk on this story is what's moving XAUUSD intraday.
