### Claude · 2025-09-11 15:28

**Core Kabbalah/Zohar teachings (13th century CE, uncertain authorship, attributed to Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai):**

**Fundamental concepts:**

* Ein Sof (Infinity) - infinite, unknowable divine source
* Ten Sefirot - divine emanations/attributes through which Ein Sof reveals itself
* Partzufim - configurations of Sefirot
* Four Worlds - cascading levels of reality from divine to physical

**Core doctrine:**

* Hidden meaning within Hebrew Bible and rabbinic literature
* Divine light flows through Sefirot to material world
* Soul's journey through spiritual worlds
* Tikkun (repair) - correcting cosmic fragmentation
* Scanning/reading Zohar restores universal balance

**Key assumptions exposed:**

* Authorship disputed (likely 13th century Spanish composition, not 2nd century)
* Claims of exclusive access to divine secrets
* Anthropomorphic descriptions of infinite divine

**Measurement limitations:**

* No empirical verification possible for metaphysical claims
* 1,000+ pages dense Aramaic text
* Historical emergence: 12th-13th centuries

**Counter-argument:** Rational Judaism views Kabbalah as medieval mystical overlay on Torah, not original divine revelation.
