### Claude · 2025-07-28 15:58

To upload your manuscript file:

1. **Look for the attachment/upload button** - This is usually represented by a paperclip icon (📎) or plus sign (+) near where you type your messages
2. **Click the upload button** and select your file from your computer
3. **Choose your manuscript file** - Select the Word doc, PDF, or text file containing your manuscript
4. **Send the message** - The file will upload and I'll be able to read and analyze it

The exact location of the upload button varies slightly depending on whether you're using:  
- **Web browser**: Usually near the text input box  
- **Mobile app**: Often a + or paperclip icon  
- **Desktop app**: Similar to web browser

Once uploaded, I'll be able to read through your manuscript and provide whatever type of feedback or assistance you're looking for. Just let me know what specific areas you'd like me to focus on when I review it!
