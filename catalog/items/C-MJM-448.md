This range applies to private clinics and specialists in Querétaro. For a straightforward sore throat evaluation, you'll likely be at the lower end.

Alternatives: Pharmacy consultorios (in-store doctor consultations) can be under 300 MXN (~$18) if you only need a prescription written.
