### Millard · 2025-08-31 16:50

### Claude · 2025-08-31 16:50

Fatalism - belief that all events are predetermined and inevitable, making human effort useless.

Defeatism - belief/attitude that failure is inevitable.

Nihilism - belief that life/values are meaningless and nothing matters.

### Millard · 2025-08-31 16:51

Fatalism connotes resignation. Defeatism connotes despair. Nihilism isn't about failure, but absurdity/meaninglessness.

### Claude · 2025-08-31 16:51

You're right - none capture "belief that all attempts are doomed" without those unwanted connotations.

No standard English word exists for this specific belief. "Futilism" would be the logical construction (futile + -ism), but it's not established.

Closest existing: **Pessimism** (belief in worst outcomes) but broader than just doomed attempts.

### Millard · 2025-08-31 16:52

### Claude · 2025-08-31 16:52

**Hopelessness doctrine** or **inevitabilism** (belief all efforts inevitably fail) - but neither are established terms.

English lacks a precise word for "belief that all attempts are inherently doomed." You'd need to coin one or use a phrase.

* Follows standard English word formation patterns

Use it.
