”Thought-provoking” and nonsensical at the same time.

"Actual rights" cannot rely on *any* system whatsoever.

"... a system in which no one has the power to take them away" sounds good until you get real about what it would require and involve.

The only way to make sure a system doesn't get hijacked and manipulated is one that's operated directly by God without human intervention.

Nature is such a system.

Beyond that, the very concept of "rights" is intrinsically incoherent. There are no such things as rights unless someone *grants* them and *declares* them, even if it is you for yourself.

So, there is no such thing as rights that do not depend on human beings to create, press, and enforce them.

So, there is no such thing as rights that cannot be ignored, disregarded, nullified, or revoked.

If it comes down to us to make sure that what we want, we get and what we don't want, we prevent, then who is there for "rights" to be relevant to? Us? The people who would violate our "rights"? The people who would never violate them?

No one has actually thought through the facts about rights:

1. Any rights that exist anywhere were *declared* by people "in power" -- which are exactly the same caste of people "rights" are supposed to protect us from.

1. Even the United Nations admits that rights do nothing unless they are “protected” by laws.

   > Whereas it is essential, if man is not to be compelled to have recourse, as a last resort, to rebellion against tyranny and oppression, that human rights should be protected by the rule of law…
   >
   > — Third paragraph of the Preamble to the *Universal Declaration of Human Rights* (UDHR) <https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights#:~:text=Whereas%20it%20is%20essential%2C%20if%20man%20is%20not%20to%20be%20compelled%20to%20have%20recourse%2C%20as%20a%20last%20resort%2C%20to%20rebellion%20against%20tyranny%20and%20oppression%2C%20that%20human%20rights%20should%20be%20protected%20by%20the%20rule%20of%20law%2C>

   So how are rights supposed to protect you when they need to be protected by laws that could very well be created to violate your rights?

1. Declaring that you have "rights" that insist you remain unviolated does *absofuckinglootely NOTHING* to stop someone from violating you. Sure, go ahead -- tell them you have "rights"; show them your bill of "rights"; show them your universal declaration of "rights". See what they do next.

"Rights" do only *one* thing, ever: ***AFTER*** people have violated you, rights provide a basis for **ARGUING THAT THEY SHOULD NOT HAVE VIOLATED YOU**.

Otherwise they either fall on deaf ears or preach to the choir.

Fucking useless.
