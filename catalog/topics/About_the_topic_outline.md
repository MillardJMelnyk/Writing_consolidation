# About the topic outline

[← Master catalog](../Master_catalog.md)

The topic outline groups the catalog's items by what they are about. The topics and subheadings were drawn from the content of the items themselves, and grow as new material is added.

## How it is organized

- **Topics:** the broad subjects, largest first, each with its number of items.
- **Subheadings:** the narrower subjects under each topic, also with their number of items.
- An item is filed under every subheading it substantially addresses (up to 9), so one item can appear under several topics and subheadings.

The start page, [Topic outline](Topic_outline.md), shows every topic with its subheadings.

## How to use it

1. Open [Topic outline](Topic_outline.md).
2. Click a topic to see every item under it, or click a subheading to see only its items.
3. Use **by date** or **by title** at the top of the list to jump to the order you want.
4. Click any title to open the item.
